## Overview

The Perception system gives every guard its senses — raycast vision cones (FOV angle + range + line-of-sight), event-based hearing (radius + occlusion), and the early-warning suspicion meter that integrates what those senses report. It is also the player's instrument: the two-component meter — a sustained-LOS **accumulator** that reads *"how long and how close has this guard been watching me"* and a **residual** that reads *"how wary is this guard of my presence"* — is the game's fair-challenge contract made visible (Pillar 2), and the threshold-sinking residual marker renders the machine's cognition in the world's own light grammar (Pillar 4). Perception emits **events** — LOS gain/break, noise-heard, threshold-crossing — never commands, and never escalations (an escalation is an FSM decision, R12). The Guard AI FSM (a separate system) consumes those events and decides. That event contract is what keeps Perception free of the AI state machine and free of dependency cycles: Perception reports what it senses; it never tells a guard what to do.

**Design vs. implementation boundary**: the accumulator/residual rates, thresholds, and confirm-window timing are this GDD's behavior specification. The concrete sensing implementation (which physics cast produces the vision-cone query, the sensing layer-mask separation E20, the FSM-trace ring buffer, the meter widget) is ADR territory at Technical Setup — noted here, not decided here. The provisional source seam in `src/AI/Perception/PerceptionHearingService.cs` covers injected guard snapshots, E20 hearing evaluation, and immutable relay construction only; it does not claim the full bounded scheduler, transactional bus handoff, or runtime composition is complete or tested.

## Player Fantasy

*You are a phantom who reads the machine — never surprised, never guessing.*

The Perception system's player-facing surface is its instrument: the suspicion meter. It is not a game widget; it is a **language the facility speaks**. Cold fill rises when the machine's eye is on you — how long, how close. A warm line sinks when you make it wary — your presence now lives in the guard's memory. The power fantasy is *never being surprised*: every detection is readable (Pillar 2), every escalation legible (Pillar 4), so the facility's watchfulness becomes something you plan inside, not something that ambushes you.

The anchoring moment: a near-miss at the meter's edge — the spike as the guard's cone sweeps past, and you break line of sight just inside the confirm window. Your competence *is* the instrument answering you. And the residual marker sinking after a close call means the world remembers you visibly — your mistakes become an in-world thread, and "none the wiser" is earned, not given.

Tone is cold-noir (Cold Watch VIA): the meter renders as facility hardware — cold fill, warm verdicts. Every meter change answers a fair question; no bar moves for reasons the player cannot name.

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**R1 — Sensing cadence.** Vision LOS and sensing-coupled path queries evaluate on the shared 2–5 Hz tick; hearing is the additive dedicated 5 Hz sub-evaluation defined in R5. No per-frame vision/path sensing raycasts (frame budget; WebGL hitch). LOS gain/break are stateful comparisons between ticks. **Catch-gate path cost (WebGL sync-hitch guard):** the expensive query is the F1 `d` path — `NavMeshAgent.CalculatePath` is a synchronous, spiky call whose per-call latency is **unmeasured** (the old "10–15 ms frames" figure was an unverified premise — calibrated at Technical Setup profiling, cost-story note ADJ-(r)). Three bindings: (1) **Euclidean pre-gate** — a full path query runs only when straight-line guard-to-player distance ≤ `catch_range + 2 m` (path distance is never shorter than Euclidean, so the pre-gate provably skips every out-of-range tick); (2) **d re-sample cap** — inside the gate band the path re-samples at most once per 500 ms (2 Hz) and on LOS gain / segment transition — a per-tick re-query is forbidden; (3) **per-guard stagger** — sensing ticks are phase-staggered across guards (guard *g* offsets by *g* × T_sample / N) so N guards never cast their raycast + near-range bursts on the same frame. **Dual-mode d (ADJ-1):** within the catch band (Euclidean ≤ `catch_range + 2 m`) the charge metric `d` is the path distance; in the Δ-band (`catch_range + 2 m` < Euclidean ≤ R_vis) `d` is the Euclidean distance with reachability decided by the R3 navmesh sample — no path query runs there (F1). Implementation pins: cached `NavMeshPath`, the guard's own `NavMeshAgent` (no hidden helper agent). **Path-status mapping (rev 3.2; discriminator re-pinned rev 3.3 — J7):** `Partial` ⇒ cumulative-corner distance as specified; **the mid-OffMeshLink discriminator is `NavMeshAgent.isOnOffMeshLink` / `currentOffMeshLinkData.valid` — never the path object** (`NavMeshPathStatus.Invalid` is opaque and fires for any unsampleable start or unreachable destination, so a corners-based heuristic mis-routes the off-navmesh-foot-start case): `Invalid` while `isOnOffMeshLink` ⇒ **defer the query, keep the prior tick's path verdict** — a zero-corner `Invalid` must never degrade to `d ≈ 0` (fast-lane wrong-way charge); **any other `Invalid` ⇒ `d = ∞`** (no charge — includes the off-navmesh-foot-start variant). Land in the Technical-Setup sensing ADR. **Unified reachability fact (rev 3.3 — J2):** Perception computes, per tick, ONE **`reachable(player, guard)`** verdict — R3 same-surface sample exists **AND** path-arrival within the catch envelope **AND** `|ΔY| ≤ delta_y_tolerance` (1.0 m starter) — published as a single sensing-fact (R12) consumed by the F2 Chase-cap, the R15 give-up predicate, and (via the FSM) the catch-gate geometry (**ONE verdict set for all three — the |ΔY| ledge livelock (E4) closes on the same predicate**). The fact is computed **ungated by the FSM's nav-goal** — it must bind during Patrol and Investigate (the goal-gated catch gate is FSM-owned and inactive outside pursuit). The Δ-band Euclidean clause (F1) survives only as the derived sufficient consequence of path-arrival-fail. **d metric pin (rev 3.3 — J2):** `d` is guard-feet → sampled-player-feet **center-to-center** path length; the player is **not** a carved `NavMeshObstacle` (blocked-destination corners at the capsule edge would shrink `d` by up to `r_player` against `δ_reach` 0.02); pinned in the Technical-Setup sensing ADR.

**R2 — Vision.** A guard's vision is a raycast cone: FOV angle (yaw), range, and line-of-sight to the player's *perceived position*. Line-of-sight = `Physics.Linecast` from the guard's eye (standing capsule-height offset) to the player's perceived position (current stance-height offset), World layer-mask, `QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore`, sensing mask restricted per E20. Occluded → no LOS → no accumulator charge. Cone edges are authoring softened rendering, not the sensing boundary.
**LOS continuity (committed):** LOS is a *continuous* state, sampled once per sensing tick. Sub-tick flicker aliases — a player rhythmically riding the sensing boundary may be sampled in-phase and read as continuous LOS (charging full rate) even while spending half their time outside the sensing cone. This overcount is deliberate and fair **only because the rendered cone is strictly wider than the sensing cone** (committed bias, Tuning: rendered-cone bias, starter 5°): every sampled-LOS discontinuity lands inside rendered territory the player visibly knows is sensed. This pairing is the Pillar-2 floor for the vision cone — no charge the player cannot read, no sensing verdict that contradicts rendered territory. **Range-side companion (committed):** rendered vision range ≥ R_vis + margin — the rendered cone covers the sensing *range* edge too, not just the FOV edge; a player standing at the sensing-range boundary is inside visibly-rendered territory.
**Phase-locked flicker (Pillar-2 stance, ADJ-5 — accepted + documented, no hysteresis):** a player rhythmically riding the sensing boundary can phase-lock with the 2–5 Hz tick and be sampled in-phase → charged as continuous LOS even while spending half their time outside the sensing cone. This overcount is **accepted and documented** as the R2 LOS-continuity pairing already sets: the overcount is bounded by the rendered cone ⊇ sensing cone bias, so every charged verdict is provably inside visibly-rendered territory (Pillar-2 floor holds — no charge the player cannot read). **Hysteresis (different ON/OFF boundaries) is explicitly rejected (3.1 boundary: no new mechanism)** — it would trade a bounded, readable overcount for an off-boundary the player cannot observe, and it is out of scope for revision 3.1. The accepted stance is pinned here and asserted at AC-R2d.

**R3 — Perceived position (thin-geometry rule, C-6 pin).** The player position used by detection is the **navmesh-sampled feet position snapped to the same walkable surface** the player stands on (the C-6 nearest-snap same-surface rule): stand-off/ledge positions snap to the nearest walkable point on their own surface, never to a different navmesh island. Stance height (crouch/stand) adjusts the Linecast offset. Guarantees thin cover is honored (crouch behind sub-1.0 m cover is not detected over it) and vision never "sees through" a floor edge onto a lower floor. **Same-surface sampling pin (rev 3.3 — J7):** `NavMesh.SamplePosition` runs with a declared `maxDistance = navmesh_sample_maxdistance` ≈ 0.4 m (registry-locked; ≥ max authorable navmesh-free lip 0.30 m + margin, < gap to any non-adjacent walkable surface 0.5 m) — within range the vertical-projection bias is bounded to the feet surface; a `hit == false` outcome maps to the F1 "no reachable sample ⇒ d = ∞" rule.

**R4 — Hide spots.** A player inside a `HideSpot` zone is **not perceptible to LOS** — a genuine LOS break. A guard's *live hide-spot fix* (set on sight-of-entry, or the spot-front hold) persists through the approach; LOS is not needed while the fix is held. Hide spots do not stop hearing, noise, or direct spot-front verification. **Tier selection is FSM-owned (rev 3.6, CR-FSM-01):** Perception's contract ends at publishing the sensing facts — every witnessed dive fixes the spot on sight-of-entry regardless of alert level, and the hide-dive LOS-break fact carries `a_pre_break` + `threshold_applied`; the *FSM* selects the response tier from those two fields (Chase iff `a_pre_break ≥ threshold_applied`, Investigate-commit cause=hide-entry fallback otherwise). Perception never gates its fix-set or its break-fact fields on the threshold comparison — that decision belongs to the consumer.
**HideSpot transition identity and retained state.** Perception consumes only typed `HideSpotOccupied` and `HideSpotEmpty` transitions, each carrying a source `transition_id`; it never infers an occupancy transition from `occupied` alone, absent LOS, proximity, or a stale LKP. After the HideSpot phase, Perception retains the current occupancy state produced by applying the accepted transitions in source order. The retained event payload preserves `session_id`, `attempt_epoch`, `hide_spot_id`, `interior_position`, `occupied`, timestamp, publisher, and `transition_id` verbatim. A duplicate transition identity is deduplicated and cannot allocate an `entry_id`, restart a hold, or create a second derived fact.

**R5 — Hearing.** Hearing is event-based: the Player Noise system emits `NoiseEmitter` events (radius by movement state / tool). The raw emitter publication carries `kind`, `source`, `position`, `radius`, `timestamp`, `publisher`, and emitter-owned **`ulong fact_id`**; it carries no guard-specific consumption or episode id. Perception evaluates that fact for each guard and creates a per-guard `Noise heard` relay by adding `guard_eid`, `consumption`, and the Perception-owned **`entry_id`** reserved at the first eligible noise-heard relay when the previous-boundary liveness snapshot is closed; when that snapshot says an episode is live, the relay carries the existing `entry_id` and allocates none. A guard hears an event if the effective planar radius test AND the occlusion check (wall-blocking, binary — no partial horizontal attenuation) pass. **Hearing distance is planar with a soft vertical leg (rev 2026-08-28):** `d_noise` is the **XZ-plane** distance from guard to noise origin. Perception computes `R_eff(payload, ΔY) = payload.resolved_nominal_radius × max(0, 1 − |ΔY| / hearing_y_hard_cutoff)`; a source at the same height uses the full radius, attenuation falls linearly with vertical separation, and `|ΔY| ≥ hearing_y_hard_cutoff 4.0 m [3, 5]` is silent regardless of XZ distance (closes the atrium vertical hole; distinct from `delta_y_tolerance 1.0`, the vision/catch reachability gate). **Reaction cadence (rev 2026-08-29):** hearing evaluates on a dedicated **5 Hz hearing sub-evaluation** (`T_hearing 0.2 s` — radius + one Linecast per in-range guard) additive to the 2–5 Hz vision tick. It is driven only by the shared injected session virtual clock and a stable scheduler phase; it is not a `MonoBehaviour.Update()` or `Time.unscaledTime` accumulator, and `OnEnable`/`OnDisable` never resets its phase or queued facts. Pause freezes virtual gameplay time and does not buffer input edges across resume. It produces normal staircase latency `t_audible = t_publish + n·T_hearing`, `n ∈ {0, 1}`, bounded by `T_hearing`; exact timestamp equality at commit is valid when the fact lands on a due boundary. Under bounded-work pressure this is a success-or-explicit-failure deadline: an admitted pair that cannot be evaluated in time records `perception-hearing-deadline-missed` and produces no late relay. Hearing never feeds the accumulator — it triggers Investigate/orientation (a Guard AI decision). Noise source is attributed (Burst / movement) for the trace (R12). **Broadcast + per-guard single-consumption (rev 3.3 — J3, closes the R5/R12 single-consumer ambiguity):** a noise event is **broadcast to every in-range guard** — one guard's consumption never invalidates another guard's relay (two guards 3 m from a Burst with clear line both hear, each on its own `Noise-heard` relay); each guard's relay is consumed by that guard's FSM at most once; stale relays after Chase-end are dropped by the FSM (never re-heard). **Two ids, two owners (rev 2026-08-28 — B2):** every relay retains the emitter-allocated `fact_id` (one per published fact, shared by all hearing guards) and carries the Perception-allocated `entry_id`; neither id is re-derived by the FSM.

**Hearing scheduler amendment (Player Noise re-review, 2026-08-29):** the dedicated 5 Hz hearing sub-evaluation is driven by the shared injected **session virtual clock**, not `MonoBehaviour.Update()`, `Time.unscaledTime`, or component enable/disable. Its phase is initialized once at session/scene start and remains stable across pooling and `OnEnable`/`OnDisable`; pause excludes elapsed time. A queued fact is evaluated at `H(ceil_to_next_boundary(t_publish, T_hearing))`, with an exact-boundary queue drain. For an admitted guard/fact pair, `t_audible − t_publish ∈ [0,T_hearing]` is a success-or-explicit-failure deadline: if bounded work cannot evaluate the pair by the deadline, Perception records `perception-hearing-deadline-missed` and emits no relay. `attempt_epoch` is part of the raw fact/relay identity; death, respawn, and segment reset discard older queued epochs before FSM processing. This paragraph supersedes the older Update-accumulator wording in R5.

**R5 contract-hardening amendment (2026-08-28):** the scheduler phase is represented explicitly as `φ ∈ [0,T_hearing)`, with boundaries `b_n(φ) = φ + n·T_hearing`; `n` is the least index for which `b_n ≥ t_publish`. Facts arriving during the boundary drain are evaluated in that boundary; facts arriving after the drain wait for the next boundary. The relay is immutable and carries `{session_id, attempt_epoch, fact_id, entry_id, source_timestamp, terminal_publication_time, t_publish, evaluated_at, residual_at_hearing, consumption}` plus the raw noise fields and `guard_eid`. For Movement, `terminal_publication_time` is not applicable and `t_publish = source_timestamp`; for Burst, `t_publish = terminal_publication_time`, while `source_timestamp` remains accepted-Throw allocation/order metadata only and is never used as hearing or corroboration timing. The base-envelope `timestamp` shorthand elsewhere in this GDD is resolved to the source-specific `t_publish` rule here; it must not introduce a third hearing-time authority. `fact_id` is an emitter-owned `ulong` transport identity; `entry_id` is an opaque Perception-owned string episode identity. `consumption` is one of `{eligible, consumed, ignored, stale}` and is an outcome, not a command. The FSM deduplicates a relay by `(session_id, attempt_epoch, guard_eid, fact_id)` and never queries Perception synchronously. Noise-led `investigate-commit` receives `R_at_commit` and `evaluated_at` in the sensing-fact preamble, so the decision uses the value observed at commit rather than a later readout. Every sensing fact and decision carries the same session/epoch envelope; reset is an atomic barrier that invalidates older queued facts.

**R6 — Accumulator.** The accumulator `A` charges only during **sustained LOS exposure** to a single guard: `dA/dt = min(k/d, rate_max)` where `d` is reachable guard-to-player distance. **Fully resets on LOS break and on segment transition.** `A ∈ [0, A_max]` (clamped). Inert during Chase.

**R7 — Confirm window.** Crossing the Investigate entry threshold `T_entry` starts a **confirm window** (duration `t_win`). An LOS break inside the window **cancels the escalation** (committed-escalation zero — the grade counts nothing) **and may record a witnessed evasion: it increments the guard's cancel counter (R11) when the break is player-caused** (causal class `peek-out` or player-attributed `wall/segment`, R12). **Causer attribution (ADJ-2 + rev 3.2 wall/segment ruling):** a window-cancelled LOS break increments the counter **iff the player caused the break**. Guard-motion breaks (guard turn/sweep rotating the cone off a stationary player — causal class `guard-sweep`) are sweep-excluded; hide-dives (`hide-dive` — player enters a HideSpot during the window) supersede to the hide-entry path (R4) and never double-count; **segment transitions never count** (the break is the level, not the player). **Classifier (rev 3.2; consistency pin rev 3.3 — J4):** a raw LOS break is classified from (a) player displacement during the window, (b) guard-head angular change during the window, (c) hide-entry event, (d) segment event. **Measurement basis (rev 3.3):** player displacement is measured on the R3 perceived position with a dead-zone `cls_displacement_deadzone` = 0.10 m — perceived displacement **≤** the dead-zone counts as stationary; guard-head angular change is the guard-head **cumulative-absolute yaw** during the window (net rotation is rejected — an out-and-back sweep nets ≈ 0 while still rotating the cone off the player), with threshold `cls_sweep_yaw` = 5.0° ("guard-head" = the transform node carrying the sensing-cone yaw — a Perception input, R12/interactions matrix). **Decision rule (the user-chosen displacement-only ruling, rev 3.2; basis pinned rev 3.3):** a break is player-caused iff the player's perceived displacement during the window exceeds the dead-zone — the *counterfactual* clause is a **debugging heuristic, not the rule**; `guard-sweep` iff the player is stationary (≤ dead-zone) and the guard-head cumulative-absolute yaw change exceeds the threshold. **Simultaneous player motion + guard sweep: player-sided motion wins (player-caused).** Ducking behind arbitrary cover mid-window (displacement > dead-zone) counts as a witnessed evasion; only HideSpot entry (the sanctioned counter-play) is exempt. Sustained sight past the window **commits** to Investigate and emits the escalation event. The window gates only the escalation commit, not the accumulator value. A window-commit while an Investigate is already live is absorbed into the ongoing episode (ADJ-4, R13/F7) — no second Investigate event.

**R8 — Chase.** Chase engages when `A` reaches the Chase threshold `T_chase` (distance-inverse rate ⇒ sustained proximity + LOS required). **R8 is the sensing side of the escalation junction (rev 3.3 — J1):** Perception publishes one **`chase-reached` sensing fact** (R12); the FSM publishes the **`Chase-entry` decision** that Euclid grades as the escalation — and the meter's Chase row triggers on it (A/R freeze on every Chase path, incl. hide-entry and carve-out). The accumulator resets at Chase begin (a state transition, not a suppressed reset).

**R9 — Residual.** The residual `R` charges in parallel with `A` during **any LOS exposure outside Chase** at a lower rate; **persists through LOS break**; **decays toward zero** over ~45-60 s; clamped `R ∈ [0, R_max]`. It **never accumulates toward Chase**. It lowers the Investigate **entry** threshold for subsequent exposure by the **same guard**: `T_entry = max(T_base − k_res·R, 0.2 × T_base)`. The drop is player-visible (warm threshold marker sinks — Pillar 4, MVP-visible). Resets to zero at segment transitions (deferred past any in-flight Chase resolution).

**R10 — Residual inputs.** Two sources: (a) LOS exposure (parallel charge, R9); (b) **fruitless investigations** — an Investigate resolving with the player unfound accrues a bounded share of residual, applied when the FSM's investigate-resolution event reports `cause: fruitless` (feedback contract, R12) — **noise-triggered OR witnessed-entry (hide-dive-caused, `cause=hide-entry`) investigations alike (rev 3.9, CR-PERCEPTION-01; creative-director ruling: residual tracks *a search completed with the player unfound*, so being seen diving must never be the safer play)** — including witnessed inspections that end empty after the spot-front dwell (FSM AC-FSM-16(b) empty-spot variant). Camera-trip alerts mirror this (Camera system). Both are in-world consequences; neither is a direct grade input.

**R11 — Cancel-cap self-escalation.** Repeated peek→break→repeat self-degrades the player's position in two independent ways. (1) **Residual sink:** `T_entry` drops with R (F5), so sustained peek-play opens confirm-windows sooner — less hold-time per peek before the commitment clock runs. (2) **Cancel counter (the closer):** every *player-caused* window-opened-then-cancelled episode (R7, causal class `peek-out`) is a witnessed evasion that increments the guard's per-guard counter. **Causer attribution (ADJ-2 + rev 3.2 ruling; basis pinned rev 3.3 — J4):** the increment fires only for player-caused breaks — `peek-out` and player-attributed `wall/segment` (ducking behind arbitrary cover mid-window counts; classify and tie-break per R7's displacement-only rule on the R3 perceived position with the pinned dead-zone; the segment-valued class `segment` never increments). `guard-sweep` breaks are **sweep-excluded** — the machine's own motion never accrues a witnessed evasion; `hide-dive` breaks supersede to the hide-entry path (R4) and never double-count the counter; **segment transitions never count.** At `n_cancel ≥ n_cancel_cap` (starter 3), Perception emits a **cap-forced escalation event at the last perceived position** (trace cause: `cap-forced`); the FSM acts on it as a **forced Investigate** — no further confirmation required. **Legibility (adjudicated 2026-08-18 + ADJ-3 rev):** the counter is surfaced as **pips** on the meter's residual side (one per witnessed evasion). **Pip/counter coupling (ADJ-3 — decay together / waive-out):** each pip fades on the residual-consistent clock (F4); when a pip's fade completes, the counter **drops with it** (waive-out) — the counter never outlives its visible pips. The cap fires only when the counter reaches `n_cancel_cap` **with all cap-pips still visible** — a dark fire (counter ≥ cap but pips faded) is structurally impossible. Segment end clears pips and counter together. **Reset (adjudicated):** otherwise the counter resets **only on a sight-committed Investigate/Chase commit or the cap-forced Investigate itself** — noise/alert-derived Investigates do NOT reset it (a Burst or peer alert cannot wipe a peek-grudge). **Grade disposition (adjudicated):** the forced Investigate is **non-grading** — an extension of the D1 never-count clause (R13); its trace still records for telemetry. Together these deliver the concept's "bounded time under base" promise by a mechanism the player can name ("the guard got fed up with my peeking").

**R12 — Event trace (C-4 pin).** **Publisher ownership split (blocker (f), rev 3.1; escalation-commit junction closed, rev 3.2; junction completed rev 3.3 — J1):** Perception publishes **sensing facts**; the FSM publishes **decisions — including the escalate decision on every episode-opening path**. **Episode-opening re-scope + suppression rule (rev 3.3 — J1):** the FSM publishes **exactly one escalation decision per escalation tier — the episode's first in that tier, never a same-tier duplicate** — `investigate-commit` at the Investigate tier, `Chase-entry` at the Chase tier; a confirm-window commit absorbed into a live episode emits no second record (ADJ-4); an Investigate-tier episode that subsequently reaches Chase **additionally** publishes its `Chase-entry` — the escalate-to-Chase path carries both records, in that order (AC-R8 positive leg / AC-F9 freeze trigger); **"MANDATORY on every path" means every *episode-opening* path publishes its first decision.** Perception publishes: every LOS gain (source, distance, guard, timestamp); every **LOS break (source, distance, guard, timestamp, numeric R at break, break-type: hide / wall / segment, causal class: peek-out | guard-sweep | hide-dive | wall/segment | segment — the cancel counter (R7/R11) consumes `peek-out` and player-attributed `wall/segment` per the rev-3.2/3.3 classifier; ### Pass A — AI behavior-contract amendment (2026-08-29)

This section is normative where an older R5/R12 paragraph leaves ownership or ordering implicit.

#### Response profile and capability gate

Perception receives the immutable `noise_response_profile` from the session configuration and copies it into the fixture/replay envelope; it does not infer or downgrade the profile. `MVP` requires `coordination_service = absent`. `Target` requires an AlertPropagation readiness/capability handshake before the first playable tick. A missing service, a not-ready service, or a contradictory profile/service pair is a validation failure with no fallback to another profile. Perception may publish noise relays in either profile, but it never publishes `peer-recruit` or `locked-zone`; those are Guard AI FSM decisions, and Target decisions are legal only after the successful handshake.

#### Entry reservation and immutable relay contract

For a heard guard/fact pair, Perception creates the immutable relay only after the pair passes the R5 hearing predicate and before FSM behavioral precedence runs. The relay carries `session_id`, `attempt_epoch`, `fact_id`, `entry_id`, `source_timestamp`, `evaluated_at`, `residual_at_hearing`, the raw noise fields, the guard identity, and initial `consumption = eligible`. Perception reserves `entry_id` at this point only when the guard's latest event-sourced FSM liveness snapshot says that no episode is live; if an episode is live, it carries that episode's existing `entry_id`. The liveness snapshot is the latest published state at the previous boundary; Perception never synchronously queries or gates publication on the FSM. When multiple eligible pairs for one guard are admitted in the same boundary, Perception builds a deterministic virtual liveness projection before publishing relays: guard selection is ordered by ascending `guard_eid` only; `stable_guard_snapshot_position` is recorded snapshot data, never a sort operand, facts for each selected guard by `(source_timestamp, source_event_class_rank, source_event_id, fact_id)`, and dispatched guard/fact pairs by `(source_timestamp, source_event_class_rank, source_event_id, fact_id, guard_eid)`; these are three distinct registry-backed keys, not interchangeable aliases. The first candidate that sees no live episode reserves one new `entry_id`, and each later candidate sees that projected live episode and reuses the same id. The projection is local to the boundary, is seeded only from the prior published liveness fact, and is not a synchronous FSM query or a behavioral decision; FSM precedence may still close any reservation as `ignored` or `stale`.

**HideSpot boundary snapshots and bounded re-entry.** HideSpot allocation uses two
separate Perception-owned inputs: (1) an immutable FSM liveness snapshot containing
only events published through the previous tick boundary, and (2) the current retained
occupancy state after the current boundary's typed HideSpot transitions have been
accepted. Perception never synchronously asks the FSM for current state. If a boundary
contains `HideSpotEmpty` followed by `HideSpotOccupied` for the same spot, the Empty
transition first closes the prior occupancy/hold context; the re-entry is not eligible
for a replacement `entry_id` or a new witnessed-entry derivation during that same
boundary. Perception retains the Empty and Occupied facts, including their original
`session_id`, `attempt_epoch`, `hide_spot_id`, `interior_position`, timestamp, publisher,
and `transition_id` values, and defers the Occupied allocation decision by exactly one
boundary. At the next boundary, if the current retained state is still Occupied, the
normal previous-boundary liveness rule may allocate exactly one new `entry_id`; if it is
Empty, the deferred re-entry is closed as stale/ignored without allocation. Replaying a
retained `transition_id` is a duplicate and cannot allocate, re-derive authority, or
publish another fact.

A reservation can remain unused: the FSM may suppress the relay because of HideSpotFront immunity, Chase, cooldown, a higher-ranked sensing fact, or a terminal barrier. The FSM records `ignored` for a live-epoch suppression and `stale` after an epoch/terminal invalidation; an unused reservation is retained in the trace as closed-unused and is never silently reused. A relay that changes the episode or re-anchor is recorded `consumed`. Duplicate identity `(session_id, attempt_epoch, guard_eid, fact_id)` produces no second behavior. The relay and its identity are immutable after publication: its `consumption=eligible` is the admission value, while the final `consumed|ignored|stale` value is a separate keyed outcome record, not an in-place mutation or command. Perception never re-derives `fact_id` or `entry_id`.

**Liveness snapshot lifecycle.** The liveness fact Perception reads to decide the no-alloc-when-live rule is FSM-owned and event-sourced. The FSM **publishes** the liveness fact when it emits an opener (`investigate-commit` for Investigate tier, or `Chase-entry` for Chase tier on any cause) and **closes** it when it emits a closer (`investigate-resolution` for any cause, `Chase-end`, or a witness-Investigate-tier interior-inspection `Capture` per CR-PERCEPTION-01). The liveness window is exactly `[opener, closer]` and never reopens within a single episode; an Investigate→Chase promotion is represented by `op=promote` and retains the same `entry_id` without closing or reopening the episode. "No live episode" means Perception reads the boundary as outside any guard's liveness window — the projection in the deterministic virtual liveness is seeded from the prior published liveness fact at the previous boundary, never a synchronous FSM query.

`LivenessFact.position` carries an explicit `position_source`: `opener_decision` for `op=open` (the matching FSM decision position), `episode_position` for `op=promote` (the last authoritative episode target/decision position), `terminal_decision` for a normal `op=close` (the matching resolution/`Chase-end`/witness-Capture position), and `last_authoritative_episode_position` for `op=close,cause=stale` (the liveness ledger immediately before invalidation). A guard transform, relay arrival, or wall-clock callback is never an implicit position source; missing source data fails closed.

A lifecycle transition (death, respawn, segment reset, full-room restart, scene reload, new playable attempt) is owned by the session/lifecycle service. Before invalidating prior-epoch queues, that owner submits exactly one `StaleLivenessClose` handoff per live prior-epoch key to `GuardAISystem`. The FSM publishes exactly one stale close for each accepted request; the lifecycle owner does not publish a duplicate. The Event Bus retains and deduplicates the handoff with `(session_id, attempt_epoch, guard_eid, entry_id, op, source_timestamp)`, using the exact epoch-transition timestamp for `source_timestamp` and `(source_timestamp, guard_eid, entry_id, op)` for ordering. The epoch barrier commits only after each stale close is retained or a fail-closed barrier record is written; current-epoch processing starts afterward. A late duplicate callback is a no-op. The close is recorded as `stale`, and only then does it free the next eligible relay to reserve a new `entry_id`.

Perception's no-alloc-when-live rule fires only between opener and closer. The read-side closure contract is keyed by `(session_id, attempt_epoch, guard_eid, entry_id)`: every FSM `op=open` must be observed with exactly one matching `op=close` (terminal or stale), duplicate closes and mismatched keys are validation failures, and rejected/disabled emitters never create either record. Perception records the close outcome without issuing a close command or allocating a replacement id.

#### Shared boundary ordering and epoch ownership

The FSM publishes every `LivenessFact` (`open`, `promote`, and `close`) before the next hearing drain. Perception consumes the latest such fact at the following boundary; a liveness publication that occurs after a drain cannot affect that already-running drain and is visible only at the next one. This ordering is mandatory and closes the cross-boundary window in which two eligible relays could otherwise reserve different `entry_id` values for one guard. The same-boundary virtual projection remains required for multiple eligible candidates in one drain.

The lifecycle/session owner alone increments `attempt_epoch` per the canonical lifecycle transition table (entities.yaml / player-noise.md). The transition is atomic: old Event Bus envelopes, raw/deferred hearing boundaries, hearing work items, and FSM relay queues are invalidated before current-epoch work starts. Components never increment the epoch independently and no old record may migrate into the new epoch.

  | Event                              | attempt_epoch behavior                                       |
  |------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
  | Death → capture (or vice versa)    | One atomic transition; exactly one increment                |
  | Player respawn (post-death)        | Part of the same atomic transition; no additional increment  |
  | Segment reset (room transition)    | One atomic transition; exactly one increment                |
  | Full-room restart                  | New session; globally fresh, never-reused `session_id`, with fresh `attempt_epoch = 1` |
  | Scene reload                       | New session; fresh `attempt_epoch = 1`                       |
  | New playable attempt               | New session; fresh `attempt_epoch = 1`                       |
  | Pause / resume                     | No increment; virtual clock freezes and resumes              |
  | Pool / unpool                      | No increment; scheduler phase preserved                      |

All producers and consumers use the same session virtual clock and this boundary order:

**HideSpot outer-phase contract.** The gameplay boundary is canonically ordered
`Physics → HideSpot → Perception → FSM`. Physics publishes the overlap/geometry sample;
HideSpot publishes typed occupancy transitions; Perception consumes and retains those
transitions, derives sensing facts, and performs bounded id reservation; FSM consumes
those facts only in its ordered substeps (occupancy transitions → witnessed-entry
hold/reach-presence → catch timer → outcome). This is the outer mapping for the
hearing/lifecycle order below, not a second clock or a synchronous FSM query.

1. Apply the epoch barrier.
2. Apply death/capture/terminal latches.
3. Resolve gameplay and committed raw publication.
4. Drain the due hearing queue.
5. Publish Perception relays/reservations.
6. Let the FSM apply its fixed fact ranks (Chase-tier, hide-entry gate-pass, cap-reached, sub-threshold hide-entry, confirm-window-elapsed, noise/corroboration, reachability/LOS bookkeeping).
7. Resolve timers after facts; catch completion wins simultaneous give-up expiry, and a qualifying same-boundary corroboration precedes give-up.
8. Flush trace and non-authoritative bookkeeping.

Facts arriving after the hearing drain wait for the next boundary. Facts accepted before the drain at the exact boundary timestamp are eligible in that boundary. Epoch and terminal invalidation are evaluated before hearing-deadline admission: a prior-epoch fact, a `DeathCancelled` Burst, or any terminal state other than `published` is stale and produces no relay even when its timestamp equals the deadline. Only a current-epoch published fact surviving that barrier may be admitted; exact equality at `t_publish + T_hearing` is eligible, while any later evaluation records `perception-hearing-deadline-missed` and produces no relay. If retry exhaustion and the deadline resolve at the same virtual timestamp, the epoch/terminal barrier wins, then the deadline outcome is applied. Pause freezes virtual time, timers, and scheduler phase; pooling and `OnEnable`/`OnDisable` do not reset them.

#### Hearing endpoint and corroboration inputs

Perception derives the listening endpoint from local guard geometry:

```text
guard_eye_world_y = guard_feet_world_y + guard_eye_height
```

`guard_eye_height` is never interpreted as an absolute world Y, and the eye is the **occlusion-ray endpoint only** (rev 2026-08-31) — F12's `ΔY` falloff operand uses the **guard's feet** as its datum: `ΔY = |noise_origin_y − guard_feet_y|`. Anchoring the falloff at the eye would multiply every flat-ground movement pip by `1 − guard_eye_height / hearing_y_hard_cutoff ≈ 0.60` and silently shrink the registered radii. The movement source origin is player feet plus `f12_noise_origin_offset`; Burst uses its published landing contact; each source's origin Y is compared against the guard's **feet** Y for the falloff, while `guard_eye_world_y` feeds only the `Linecast` occlusion leg. (Player Noise E16 carries the effective-radii table.)

Corroboration is evaluated from the first raw `NoisePublished.position` and source-kind publication time `t_publish` that the FSM committed as the live noise episode anchor. `t_publish = source_timestamp` for Movement and `t_publish = terminal_publication_time` for Burst. Candidate facts qualify iff `0 ≤ candidate.t_publish − anchor.t_publish ≤ t_noise_corroborate_window`, their raw XZ origin is within `t_noise_corroborate_radius`, and their `fact_id` differs from the anchor. The interval is inclusive. Duplicate, stale, out-of-order, out-of-window, or out-of-radius facts do not advance the count; evaluation/relay arrival time and jittered target positions are never used. Perception supplies the raw origin, source timestamp, terminal publication time, derived `t_publish`, and `residual_at_hearing`; the FSM owns the count and response.

**F1 — Accumulator charge rate.**

`dA/dt = min(k / d, rate_max)`

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accumulator value | A | float | 0–A_max (A_max = 1.00) | Sustained-LOS suspicion toward one guard (normalized to meter full scale) |
| Guard-to-player distance | d | float | (0, R_vis] ∪ {∞} | **Dual-mode (ADJ-1):** in the catch band (Euclidean ≤ `catch_range + 2 m`) d = catch-gate path distance (agent-parameterized `CalculatePath` to the player's reachable sample; partial paths score cumulative corner distance; no reachable sample ⇒ d = ∞). **Output range:** `dA/dt ∈ [0, rate_max]`, monotone decreasing in d. No division-by-zero escapes (implementation clamps `d := max(d, 0.01)`). **Dual-mode closure (ADJ-1):** the F1 domain and the R1 pre-gate now agree — path distance is defined exactly where the pre-gate runs (catch band), Euclidean exactly where it does not (Δ-band). No undefined band remains. **Worked example:** d = 4.0 m, k = 1.5, rate_max = 0.60 → rate = 0.375 A/s. Time 0→T_base (0.30): **0.80 s**. Time 0→T_chase (1.00): **2.67 s**. At d = 2.0 m: min(0.75, 0.60) = 0.60 → Investigate 0.50 s, Chase 1.67 s (cap kicks in at d ≤ k/rate_max = 2.5 m). At d = 10 m: 0.15 → 2.0 s / 6.67 s.

**F2 — Per-vision-tick integration, clamps, unreachable Chase-cap (same-tick ordering, ADJ-1 rev).**

`A ← min(A_max, A + ΔA)`, where `ΔA = min(k/d, rate_max) × Δt`
then, if the **unified Perception reachability fact is false (rev 3.3 — J2: NOT (R3 same-surface sample AND path-arrival AND |ΔY| ≤ delta_y_tolerance) — the SAME verdict set as the R15 give-up predicate and the catch-gate geometry, ONE set for all three consumers)** or Euclidean is in the Δ-band (`catch_range + 2 m` < Euclidean ≤ R_vis — provably catch-unreachable, the derived consequence of path-arrival-fail, R1/F1): `A ← min(A, T_chase − δ_reach)`

**Same-tick ordering (pinned, 3.1; prose corrected, 3.2):** reachability is evaluated at the top of each sensing tick, before integration. (i) If the player is unreachable (the unified reachability fact false, or Euclidean in the Δ-band), **the tick integrates, then the cap is applied to the post-integration value** (`A ← min(A, T_chase − δ_reach)` afterward) **and Chase is checked on the final post-cap value** — a capped-unreachable player never fires Chase that tick, even if the tick's `ΔA` *would* have pushed the pre-cap value to `A ≥ T_chase`. (ii) If the player is reachable, integration runs normally and `A ≥ T_chase` fires Chase the same tick. (iii) A reachable→unreachable flip within the same tick resolves at the top of the *next* tick — the tick's verdict is the reachability it started with.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tick-integrated charge | ΔA | float | 0–0.40 A/tick (legal corner; starter 0.30 = rate_max 0.60 × T_sample 0.50 — a max-config delta must not be false-failed, AC-F2) | Charge per sensing sample (rate × tick period) |
| Shared vision/suspicion tick period | Δt = T_sample | float | 0.2–0.5 s | 1/cadence of the shared 2–5 Hz vision/suspicion tick (R1); this is not `T_hearing` |
| Chase threshold | T_chase | float | 1.00 | A value that triggers Chase (prototype-validated) |
| Unreachable margin | δ_reach | float | 0.005–0.05 | Strict-< real margin below T_chase while the player is unreachable (C-6 float-precision razor) |

**Output range:** `A ∈ [0, A_max]`, clamped. Max single-tick jump = 0.80 × 0.5 = **0.40 < T_chase 1.00** (starter 0.30) — a single tick can never jump from Idle to Chase. **F4 — Residual decay (first-order).**

**F7 — Confirm window (timing relationship).**

- **Open:** at a sample tick where `A ≥ T_entry(R)` with LOS and no window open (record τ_open and threshold state `base | residual-lowered` for the R12 trace).
- **Commit:** at the first tick where `(t − τ_open) ≥ t_win` **and** LOS present → Perception publishes **`confirm-window-elapsed`** (the sensing fact that IS the threshold-path commit trigger, R12) and the FSM commits its **`investigate-commit`** decision; window closes. **If an Investigate is already live (any source), the commit is absorbed into the ongoing episode (R13/ADJ-4): no second Investigate decision is emitted — the window's only observable effect is its Chase preempt below.**
- **Cancel:** any LOS break while the window is open → cancel (committed-escalation zero), close window, A resets to 0 (R6); the cancel counter increments only for a player-caused break (causal class `peek-out`, R7/R12).
- **Preempt:** if `A ≥ T_chase` on any tick while the window is open, Chase fires directly (the window gates only the Patrol→Investigate transition — never Chase).

**Window-duration relationship (locked):** `t_win ≥ 2 × T_sample_max = 2 × 0.5 = 1.0 s`

| Variable | Symbol | Type | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirm-window duration | t_win | float | 1.0–2.0 s | Grace during which a break cancels; recommended 1.2 s |
| Max sensing sample period | T_sample_max | float | 0.5 s | Worst-case 1/cadence at the 2–5 Hz tick floor |

`t_win < 2×T_sample` is a **contract violation** (undersampled window — a single sample gap swallows the window and the cancel/commit verdict becomes unresolvable). With t_win ≥ 2×T_sample at least two samples observe the open window and "sustained sight past the window" is enforceable. t_win ≥ 2 s inflates free-peek time — keep ≤ 2 s. Window gates only the commit; A keeps accumulating through window/committed states.

**Worked example:** T_sample = 0.5 s, t_win = 1.2 s. Samples at τ₀+0.5 and τ₀+1.0 land inside the window. Break at τ₀+0.9 ⇒ next tick sees no LOS ⇒ **cancelled, committed-zero, A = 0**. Sustain past τ₀+1.2 ⇒ commit at τ₀+1.5 with LOS ⇒ **Investigate escalation emitted**.

**F9 — Chase engagement.**

On any sensing tick with LOS present and `A ≥ T_chase`, engage Chase and emit **exactly one Chase escalation event** per entry. Highest-reached-threshold governs (R13): any entry reaching Chase — base, noise/alert-derived, or residual-lowered — emits the one Chase event; Investigate-level re-entries emit none. The accumulator resets at Chase begin (a state transition, not a suppressed reset). During Chase, A and R are both inert. δ_reach (F2) prevents the equality edge; distance-inverse rate means sustained *proximity* + LOS is what fills the meter.

**F14 — Ordering-chain invariants** (every strict inequality carries a committed real margin, ε_pin = 1e-3 in A units):

1. Sampling: `t_win ≥ 2 × T_sample_max` (1.2 ≥ 1.0 s)
2. Re-sight chain (Chase): `T_sample_max < t_resight_min < t_giveup_chase < t_cap_chase` (0.5 < 1.0 < 8.0 < 30 s), plus `n_resight_cap = 2` — re-sight resets only the give-up window, never the caps
3. Spot-front backstop: `t_catch < t_giveup` (1.0 < 2.45 s at minimum give-up — full-domain floor 3.5 × 0.7; the starter R=0 give-up is 4.0) — spot-front holds terminate catch-or-confirm-empty, never the silent self-resolve (C-2/D4)
4. Window vs self-resolve: `t_win < t_giveup` (1.2 < 4.0) — committed Investigate always pre-dates the self-resolve
5. Catch reach: `catch_range > V_run × T_sample_max + hyst + r_guard + r_player + stopping_distance` (5.5 > **5.05** max-of-band — rev 3.3, J8; floor 5.2)
6. Speed: `V_chase > V_run` via `ρ_chase ≥ 1.10`
7. Grade floor chain: `forgiveness_floor < T_floor` (**T_floor derived** = 0.2 × T_base ∈ [0.04, 0.08]) with `T_floor − forgiveness_floor ∈ [ε, 0.15 × T_base]` (Suspicion/Grade GDD co-owner)
8. Catch-timer span: `t_catch ≥ 2 × T_sample_max` = 1.0 s
9. **Speed coupling (rev 3.3 — J8): `V_patrol < V_investigate ≤ V_run` across legal corners** — the three independent safe ranges admit corners violating the ordering (e.g. `V_investigate` 5.5 with `V_run` 5.0), so the joint constraint is asserted as its own leg (AC-F14(9)); `V_run`'s floor 5.0 is the binding upper bound on `V_investigate`'s range

**F15 — Forgiveness-floor joint constraint (C-6; boundary noted, owned by Suspicion/Grade GDD).** `0 ≤ forgiveness_floor < T_floor = 0.2 × T_base` — **the floor is derived, not a literal**: T_floor tracks T_base, so at T_base ∈ [0.2, 0.4] the floor lives in [0.04, 0.08]. Real margin `(T_floor − forgiveness_floor) ∈ [ε, 0.15 × T_base]` ⇒ `forgiveness_floor ∈ [0.05 × T_base, 0.2 × T_base)`. Recommended `forgiveness_floor = 0.030` (= 0.10 × T_base at starter) — legal across the whole T_base domain (0.02 ≤ 0.030 < 0.08 at T_base 0.40; 0.010 ≤ 0.030 < 0.04 at T_base 0.20); margin 0.030 stays strictly inside [ε, 0.15 × T_base] — even a floor-pinned Investigate entry contributes a real positive integral above the forgiveness floor. **The registry locks the relation, never a 0.06 literal.**

## Edge Cases

| # | Condition | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | LOS gain and break occur within the same sensing tick | Stateful comparison between ticks (R1): the tick observes the post-state. If a gain and break fall within one tick period, only the final state is registered; no charge, no false cancellation. |
| E2 | Player distance exactly at vision range `R_vis` | Inclusive boundary (`d ≤ R_vis` → LOS). Crouch behind sub-1.0 m cover is the R3 same-surface snap case — never detected over the cover. |
| E3 | Player inside a `HideSpot` when the guard holds a live fix from before entry | Fix persists (R4): guard approaches the fix, spot-front verification runs. LOS is not required while the fix is held. Hide spots never stop hearing, noise, or spot-front verification. |
| E4 | Player unreachable by the catch gate (deep off-mesh / failing `|ΔY|` ledge) while staring at guard — or on-navmesh but in the Δ-band (rev 3.2) | `d = ∞` → no charge (F1) for the off-mesh case; the unreachable cap keeps `A < T_chase` over the catch band **and unconditionally in the Δ-band (F2 rev 3.2/3.3)**. **Unified reachability (rev 3.3 — J2):** all three consumers (F2 cap, R15 give-up predicate, catch-gate geometry) read the ONE Perception `reachability` fact — the |ΔY| ledge livelock closes on the same `delta_y_tolerance` predicate. Investigate can still commit from noise/residual sources — staring alone cannot reach Chase against an uncatchable player. Stare-long from an unreachable perch self-resolves the Investigate via the R15 give-up predicate (rev 3.2). |
| E5 | `d = 0` (guard overlaps player capsule) | Implementation clamps `d := max(d, 0.01)` (F1) — rate = rate_max, finite, no division-by-zero. |
| E6 | Player repeatedly peeks in/out of LOS | Each break zeroes A (R6), R accumulates per peek (F3, flat — stays flat), and each **player-caused** window-open-then-cancel increments the guard's cancel counter (R7/R11, causal class `peek-out` or player-attributed `wall/segment` — rev 3.2 ruling). Sweep-exclusion: a break caused by the guard's own turn/sweep (stationary player) does not increment; segment transitions never count. Hide-dive supersedes to the hide path. Two independent pressures: `T_entry` sinks toward windows opening sooner (F5), and at `n_cancel_cap` witnessed evasions a **forced Investigate commits at the last perceived position** — the closer that bounds under-base time (R11). |
| E7 | Residual at `R_max` with `T_entry` floored at 0.06 | A flash-peek still must hold LOS through the full confirm window (F7) — the floor bounds re-entry cost without making one-frame glimpses commit. |
| E8 | Segment transition mid-window (after threshold crossed, before commit) | A → 0 and R → 0 on the same tick (R6/R9); the open confirm window is cancelled, committed-zero — **and the cancel counter is NOT incremented (rev 3.2: a segment break is the level, not the player — it never counts as a witnessed evasion)**. Track state resets with the segment. |
| E9 | `A ≥ T_chase` while a confirm window for Investigate is open | Preempt (F7): Chase fires directly — the window only gates Patrol→Investigate, never Chase. |
| E10 | `A ≥ T_chase` and the window commit evaluate on the same tick | Chase-wins (R13): one Chase event, no Investigate-then-Chase double. Accumulator resets at Chase begin. |
| E11 | Investigate already live and the player re-exposes | No re-investigate re-entry event (R13); A keeps climbing from committed state — only a Chase re-entry emits. New LOS gain still emits the LOS stream for the trace (R12). |
| E12 | Noise investigation triggers while the player is also LOS-exposed | Never both (F6): if the player is visible, the LOS-charge path applies. Noise causes no residual accrual when sight is present — the guard is responding to what it sees, not a guess. |
| E13 | Give-up expiry and a fresh LOS gain land on the same tick | LOS gain wins: the gain is registered before the Investigate self-resolve evaluates. A guard that just re-gained sight does not give up that same tick. |
| E14 | Noise heard exactly at radius boundary | Inclusive effective-radius boundary (`d_noise ≤ R_eff(payload, ΔY)`, F12); the nominal payload radius is never compared directly when vertical falloff applies. |
| E15 | Burst thrown while player crouched | Motion noise is silenced by crouch but the Burst is a thrown object — always emits at full radius (10.5 m). Only the movement-signature component (walk/run) is crouch-silenced. |
| E16 | Wall fully between noise origin and guard | `Linecast_clear` fails (F12) → not heard. This is the only occlusion form — no partial attenuation (hard-radius default, Section-G refinement off). |
| E17 | Guard in Chase when a noise event lands | Perception publishes the event (R5) regardless; the FSM ignores non-LOS inputs during Chase (Guard AI decision). No accumulator change during Chase (F9 — A and R inert). |
| E18 | Player exits a `HideSpotFront` mid-hold (confirm-empty) | Termination is confirm-empty per D4 — never the silent ~4 s self-resolve (R15 note; `t_catch < t_giveup` backstop holds). |
| E19 | Noise event or peer alert during a live spot-front hold | Noise/alert-immune (R14/C-2): no interrupt, no re-route, no catch-timer cancel. Only the spot-front capture or confirm-empty resolves the hold. |
| E20 | Player collider between guard eye and the player's own perceived position (close contact) | **Committed: mask separation.** The sensing mask excludes the player layer, the sensing guard's own layer, **and bystander guards' colliders — a dedicated guard-collider layer removed from the sensing mask (concept line 74: "World geometry only")**. | E21 | Heavy event burst overflows the FSM-trace ring buffer | Ring buffer drops oldest entries (Telemetry-owned; capacity ≥ 256 entries — the longest AC-R12 pairing ≈ 152 is an **estimate** (the legal worst case can approach ≈ 300), so capacity + eviction policy land as a decision in the Telemetry ADR — or a non-evictable escalation class). | E22 | Camera (Target) trip during a segment with an in-flight guard Chase | Mirror semantics: camera suspicion flows through the same accumulator/confirm logic (Shared accumulator — Camera system co-owns); alert propagation relays it, Chase precedence still scoped across all sources (R13). |
| E23 | `NavMeshPathStatus.Invalid` splits a catch-band query (mid-OffMeshLink, or a fully blocked link) | **Mapped (rev 3.2; discriminator re-pinned rev 3.3 — J7):** `Invalid` while **not** mid-link ⇒ `d = ∞` (no charge — same as no reachable sample); **the mid-OffMeshLink discriminator is `NavMeshAgent.isOnOffMeshLink` / `currentOffMeshLinkData.valid` — never the path object** (`Invalid` is opaque; a corners-based heuristic mis-routes the off-navmesh-foot-start case): `Invalid` with `isOnOffMeshLink` true ⇒ **defer the query and keep the prior tick's path verdict** (re-query at the next allowed sample). A zero-corner `Invalid` degrading to `d ≈ 0` (rate_max fast-lane charge against an unreachable player) is forbidden — R1 implementation pin. |
| E24 | Noise source approaches or exceeds the hearing vertical cutoff; `|ΔY|` varies with a clear XZ line | **Effective radius falls linearly** as `|ΔY|` rises; at `|ΔY| ≥ hearing_y_hard_cutoff = 4.0 m`, `R_eff = 0` and the event is not heard (F12, rev 2026-08-28). **Confirm-window legibility contract (this GDD's requirement, visual form owned by the HUD GDD).** The meter must surface, without the player reading numbers: **charging** (cold fill rising), **window-open** (the grace is live — a distinct state the player can act on), **committed** (Inv Investigate escalation fired), **residual-sinking** (warm threshold marker dropping). These four states are the fantasy's readability floor (Pillar 2/4) — no meter movement for reasons the player cannot name. Specific visual language (color/hardware styling) is HUD-GDD territory.

**Cancel-cap pip coupling (rev 3.1, ADJ-3 — this GDD's display-contract requirement, visual form owned by the HUD GDD).** The witness-evasion **pips** (R11) on the residual side and the cancel counter they surface are coupled on **one clock**: a pip's fade and its count drop together (decay together / waive-out). The meter can never show the cap fire while its pips have already faded — a "dark fire" is structurally impossible, and the display must not drift from that (a pip that outlives its count, or a count that persists past its pips, is a legibility failure). Pips use the residual-consistent fade (F4); pips and counter are both cleared at segment-end. This pairing is the readable-face half of the "bounded time under base" promise (R11) — the player can always name which evasion the guard is "remembering".
