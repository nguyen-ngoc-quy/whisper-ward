## Overview

The Guard AI FSM is the guard's decision-making mind: a deliberately small, hard-capped three-state machine — **Patrol → Investigate → Chase** — that turns Perception's raw sensing facts (who is visible, what noise was heard, how much suspicion each event carries) into visible, legible enemy behavior. To the player it is the "thinking enemy" — the opponent that notices, stops, turns, crosses a room to a doorway and searches, holds at a hide-spot front, then either gives up and moves on or breaks into a pursuit. Capture is not a fourth state: it is the terminal Chase-catch outcome when the guard closes the distance to the player during a chase. The FSM never queries Perception and never delegates decisions to it: every *decision input* arrives as a published sensing event (event-not-command, in both directions; execution-time geometry queries are an enumerated exception, C1.0) and every *output* is a published decision record — `investigate-commit`, `Chase-entry`, `Chase-end`, `Investigate-resolution`, and `Capture` — exactly one escalation decision per escalation tier per episode, on the typed event channel that the Suspicion/Grade operator, the Suspicion Attribution telemetry, the VFX telegraph layer, and the HUD all consume. Because everything in the game feeds off this decision trace, the FSM is the project's core-loop heart: getting the transitions right — when a guard commits, pursues, holds, or gives up — is what makes the facility feel alive and the mind-challenge fair.

## Player Fantasy

The player should feel like they are outwitting a mind, not a script. Every guard in Whisper Ward is a disciplined little consciousness with three visible temperaments — deliberate patrol, searching suspicion, and committed pursuit — and the fantasy is in *reading it*. You watch a guard on a moonlit patrol and predict him: where the sweep will take him, how long he lingers at the window, whether that burst of noise was enough to bring him over. The tension lands in the moments taken straight from Metal Gear Solid — crouched in shadow as a guard stops, turns, and walks to your hiding spot, kneels, scans, and you hold your breath waiting for the give-up — and the satisfaction lands Arkham-style: you baited the route, waited for the gap, and slipped past a guard who is still standing exactly where you left him, none the wiser.

Crucially, the fantasy is *fair by construction*: the guard never cheats the player with omniscience or teleportation. When he walks to your spot trailing you, it is because he genuinely perceived you — a lingering line of sight, a footstep heard, a suspicion left over from a glance. The player is never unsure why the guard is doing what he is doing. A guard who gives up after a search and returns to patrol feels like a fair, earned victory; a guard who gives up too early feels like a gift; a guard who latches onto a small mistake and makes you pay feels like the game being *honest*. That honesty — the ability to always answer "why is he doing that?" — is what makes the facility feel like it has a breathing mind in it, and what makes the player believe the mind-challenge is theirs to win. Honesty also cuts both ways at hide spots (rev 3.8): if the guard *saw* you dive, hiding in that spot is not safety — above the entry threshold his pursuit ends with him at your spot's front and capture is near-certain; below it he still comes and checks, and sitting still through his kneel-and-verify is a resolved, knowable gamble: if he saw you dive, he finds you — every time. The escape was never "hold your breath harder" — it was the transit time while he walks over. A guard acting on a mere hunch, by contrast, can be waited out from inside the box: noise alone doesn't bust spots, being seen diving does.

Centerpiece-pillar linked: this is Pillar 1 (Thinking Enemies) and Pillar 4 (Visible Intelligence) expressed through the FSM's trace.

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

#### C1.0 Scope and structure

- One FSM instance per guard; all state (current state, timers, per-episode record, catch timer, counters) is private to that instance. No cross-guard shared mutable state.
- The FSM is **tick-driven on the shared virtual tick clock** (the Update-accumulated authority, Perception H-0(5)): it reads the queued sensing-fact set at each tick boundary, runs its per-tick decision, and publishes any decision records at the end of the same tick. **Determinism (rev 3.5)**: all *decisions and timers* run on virtual time — every verdict (catch gate, give-up clocks, caps, re-sight validation) reads positions only at tick boundaries, and every FSM-owned timer (catch timer, give-up clocks) accrues virtual-tick time only. Guard *movement* is delegated to per-frame `NavMeshAgent` steering (integrates `Time.deltaTime`); steering is execution, never authority — frame-rate-dependent agent position can start/stop nothing and publish nothing. **Scope of the determinism claim (rev 3.6)**: determinism holds for *decisions and timers only* — steering positions (and therefore path lengths and boundary verdicts that read them) are frame-rate-dependent by design; test fixtures step movement deterministically (fixed-Δt or mocked navigator, H.0). Physical wall-clock time is never used for decisions.
- **Event model** (R12, consume only — rev 3.5 clarified): all *inputs to decisions* arrive as published sensing facts; all *outputs* are the FSM's published decision records; neither side ever calls the other. Execution-time geometry queries (the catch-gate `NavMeshAgent.CalculatePath`, `Physics.Linecast`, and movement steering) are the **enumerated exception list** — they execute decisions already made from facts and are bounded by the R1 cost bindings (see C1.4). The FSM never queries *Perception* synchronously.
- **Per-tick fact-processing order (pinned, rev 3.5; fallback re-ranked rev 3.8)** — when multiple escalation-triggering sensing facts queue in one tick, process in this priority: (1) Chase-tier facts (`chase-reached`) → (2) hide-entry sight-of-entry passing the gate (`a_pre_break ≥ threshold_applied` ⇒ Chase-tier; see C1.3) → (3) `cap-reached` → (4) **sub-threshold hide-entry fallback** (`a_pre_break < threshold_applied` ⇒ `investigate-commit cause=hide-entry`; see C1.3) → (5) `confirm-window-elapsed` → (6) `noise-heard` (single-consumption — **E18**: a first noise may open an Investigate; a second same-area noise within `t_noise_corroborate_window` recruits a peer and holds the investigation; a third same-area noise escalates to locked-zone response; unrelated or post-resolution noise follows the recommit cooldown; noise never opens Chase) → (7) reachability / LOS bookkeeping. First matching rank wins the tick's escalation slot; lower-priority facts are still consumed (single-consumption lifecycle) but do not open a competing episode.
- **Fallback re-rank (rev 3.8; bounded supersession of the rev-3.7 disposition pin)**: the sub-threshold fallback previously had no independent rank ("never re-competes"), which granted witnessed-dive immunity whenever any other escalation fact shared the tick — violating Pillar 2 (the loss was unreadable: the guard visibly saw the dive), Pillar 4 at MVP (attribution display is Target-tier; "the pips carry the why" was false), and enabling a dive-on-your-own-cap-tick free-immunity exploit. The creative-director ruling preserves the pin's Chase-tier intent (a failed gate never opens Chase) while giving the Investigate-tier fallback rank 4: it beats `confirm-window-elapsed` and `noise-heard`, so a witnessed dive always publishes a spot-directed record unless ranks 1–3 win. **Remaining supersession cell (documented for triage)**: {graze-dive + `cap-reached`} — rank 3 wins; the spot is not checked this episode and the fallback is consumed with no record. Rationale: reaching `cap-reached` requires `n_cancel_cap` = 3 player-caused window cancels, so timing a dive to your own cap tick is itself a meter-maxing play that receives the visible sharper-intent cap-forced response at the pattern point; playtest must watch this cell specifically. Publish at tick end respecting the tier-scoped suppression rule. When ranks 1–3 win over the rank-4 fallback, the consumed fallback additionally emits the trace-only marker **`suppressed-hide-fallback`** (rev 3.9, precedent `postchase-sweep` — not a decision record, touches neither tier-scoped suppression nor grading; exists so the bounded supersession cell is visible in the trace and triageable in playtest).
- `cap-reached` arriving while an episode is already live (rev 3.5): retarget the live episode's investigation target to the cap fact's last perceived position (+ `r_investigate_error`, C1.2) **without** publishing a second Investigate-tier record — the episode's existing `investigate-commit` stands; grading consequences ride the existing record. If the episode became a Chase in that same tick (priority tier 1 or 2 won), the cap fact is consumed with **no effect** — a Chase has no investigation target (rev 3.6).

- **Suppression rule** (rev 3.4, tier-scoped): the FSM publishes at most one escalation decision per escalation tier per episode. `investigate-commit` is the Investigate-tier decision; `Chase-entry` is the Chase-tier decision. An episode that commits Investigate then later reaches Chase publishes both records, in that order. A confirm-window commit absorbed into an already-live episode publishes no second record.
- **Noise corroboration (CR7, rev 2026-08-28):** noise handling remains an internal behavior of the three-state FSM; it does not add a fourth state or a new escalation tier. The first eligible noise opens the ordinary `Investigate` episode. While that episode is live, a second `noise-heard` fact within `t_noise_corroborate_window` and within `r_investigate_error` of the prior noise commit is consumed as corroborating evidence: the FSM publishes a peer-recruit command/decision to an eligible nearby guard and keeps the original investigation open until peer arrival or the window expires. A third same-area fact in the same window publishes a locked-zone command/decision for the authored patrol area and does not publish a duplicate `investigate-commit`. These positive corroboration outcomes are distinct from the post-resolution `t_noise_recommit_cooldown`; neither outcome opens Chase, and both are driven by pushed Perception facts rather than a Perception query.

#### C1.1 Patrol

- The guard follows its authored **waypoint route**: an ordered list of nodes, each with a world position + dwell time + optional scan arc (span + count). The route consumes nodes in order, loops on reaching the end. Level GDD authors routes; this GDD defines the consumption contract and the timing derivation.
- **Route timing** — each node's serial time is the constant (dwell + scan-span, zero variance in MVP). The patrol period is the sum of node serial times. AC(a)'s start-phase window in the concept is defined against this period.
- Patrol speed is `V_patrol` (2.3 m/s). Dwells hold the guard stationary at the node; scan arcs are a head/cone sweep performed over the scan-span.
- **Legibility**: scan arcs are always at node dwell points, never mid-transit (predictable; Pillar 2/4).

#### C1.2 Investigate

- An Investigate episode has a **target** = (position, radius) determined at commit. **Error radius (defined rev 3.6)**: all origin-derived targets sample their navigation position uniformly within **`r_investigate_error` = 1.5 m [1.0–2.5]** (registered) of the stated origin — one knob, applied uniformly:
  - `threshold` or `sight-committed` commit: target = the player's last perceived position (no error — the guard saw the player).
  - `noise` commit: target = the noise origin + `r_investigate_error`.
  - `alert` commit (provisional): target = the alert's area center + `r_investigate_error`.
  - `cap-forced` commit: target = the cap-reached fact's last perceived position + `r_investigate_error` (rev 3.5 intent, knob defined rev 3.6). An exact-position walk read as omniscience and violated the Player Fantasy honesty contract; the meter's alert pips already surface *why* the guard came (the player's own peek pattern), keeping the penalty legible under Pillar 4. **Penalty disposition (rev 3.6)**: the cap-forced investigate is a **time-and-position tax** — honest but deliberately non-grading and consequence-free beyond the ~4–6 s forced search at a fuzzy point (the counter self-resets on it, R11); the fantasy's "makes you pay" weight rides the residual/grade pressure from the peeking itself, not this commit. If playtesting shows the tax is toothless, the escalation path is a Grade-side disposition change, not an FSM one.
  - `hide-entry` commit (sub-threshold fallback, rev 3.6): target = the hide-spot position, **exact** (no error radius — the guard saw which spot the player entered; see C1.3).
- **Navigation**: the FSM navigates to the target's navigation position (navmesh-sampled), then executes the **look-around sub-behavior** — decomposed per the thoroughness scaling:
  - Base look-around = sweep arcs at scan positions around the target.
  - **Thoroughness scaling**: **sweep count scales** with `tau = 1 + k_thorough·R/R_max` — residual→thoroughness factor (F8); arc geometry per node stays fixed (single scaling axis, D2 rev 3.5). At starter k_thorough, R=R_max ⇒ ~1.5× the arcs of R=0; band-max k_thorough ⇒ 2×.
- **Give-up clock** (F8, FSM-owned; semantics revised rev 3.5, start point hardened rev 3.6): Investigate self-resolves when the give-up budget `t_giveup = t_giveup_base × s_diff × (1 + k_thorough·R/R_max)` expires. The budget is a **search budget**: it starts at **whichever comes first — arrival at the investigation target OR the agent stopping at the closest reachable point to the target (path end)**, never at commit — travel time never consumes it. **Detection predicates (pinned rev 3.7 — both arms read engine signals reconstructed on tick boundaries; the frame-rate-steered agent is execution, never authority):**
  - **Arrival predicate**: guard's XZ distance to the navmesh-sampled target ≤ `eps_arrive` (0.3 m [0.2–0.5], registered) evaluated at a tick boundary.
  - **Path-end predicate** (**arm extended rev 3.8; arming suppression + force-arm fallback added rev 3.9**): agent `remainingDistance` ≤ `stopping_distance` + `eps_stop` (0.1 m [0.05–0.2], registered) AND agent speed ≤ `v_stop_eps` (0.1 m/s, locked) — sustained for `n_pathend_ticks` = 2 consecutive ticks (locked) — evaluated on every tick where the **arrival predicate fails**, **regardless of path status or sample outcome**, subject to the **arming-suppression window (rev 3.9)**: the arm evaluates only while `hasPath && !pathPending && destination == the current episode target`, with a **mandatory force-arm fallback after `t_pathend_suppress_max` (1.0 s [0.5–2.0], registered rev 3.9)** of continuous suppression since the destination re-issue — fail-open toward give-up, never infinite suppression (constraint `t_pathend_suppress_max < t_giveup_base`, band-min 3.5 s). Agent speed reads `NavMeshAgent.velocity.magnitude` (actual velocity), never `.speed` (the desired max-speed setting — reads full on a stopped agent and would permanently disable the arm). (History: the arm's preconditioning/de-preconditioning/suppression evolution — Appendix H-1. The speed+conjunction+sustained-ticks requirements remain the anti-flake pin against `remainingDistance`'s asymptotic jitter.)
  - Arrival and path-end are **distinct** predicates — reached-the-sampled-target vs settled-short-of-it on any path status — and **mutually exclusive by evaluation order (precedence pinned rev 3.8): arrival is checked first; a tick where both hold sets the start-arm state to `arrived`, never `path-end`**. An episode's start-arm state is one of {not-started, arrived, path-end}.
  - **Mid-transit retarget reset**: a cap-reached retarget or hide-entry fallback retarget re-issues the destination mid-transit and resets the start arm to not-started (the clock has not started for the new target). The path-end arm (rev 3.6) closes the unreachable-target hang: a target the guard can never reach (navmesh-sample failure, `r_investigate_error` pushing the sampled point off-mesh, disconnected island) starts the clock at the closest reachable stand-off instead of never (honors Perception R15's self-resolve promise for unreachable targets). The clock ticks iff **NOT (sustained LOS AND reachable)** — stated once here as the canonical predicate and referenced by C1.3: the clock pauses only while the player is both visible-and-reachable; a hidden player, or a visible-but-unreachable one, always lets it run (no livelock on reachable noise origins). **Hold suspension (either tier, rev 3.9)**: the give-up clock is fully suspended during any witnessed-entry spot-front hold (M4, either tier — episodes carrying `witnessed_entry_authority`, C1.5 rev 3.10: Chase-tier holds and witnessed Investigate-tier inspections alike, C1.3) — terminations there are interior-Capture or confirm-empty, never self-resolve. (History: the prior Chase-tier scoping's corner race — Appendix H-2.) On expiry → sweep done → `investigate-resolution cause=fruitless` → Patrol.
- **Resolution**: an Investigate episode that completes without Chase promotion publishes `investigate-resolution`:
  - `fruitless` — search budget expired with the player not found → F6 residual share (**witnessed-entry inspections included, rev 3.9** — creative-director ruling: residual tracks *a search completed with the player unfound*; Perception R10/F6 scope extended in lock-step; a seen-diving player whose spot checks empty seeds the same bounded wariness as a noise hunch — being seen must never be the safer play).
  - `sight-committed` — player re-exposed with LOS and the guard promoted to Chase → absorbed, no F6 share.
  - `cap-forced` — a cap-forced Investigate that completes without finding the player → non-grading, no F6 share.
  - `redirected` — alert-redirected episode closes its `entry_id` without residual share.
  - Reconciliation note (rev 3.5): the R12 schema's `capture` and `abandoned` causes remain valid enum values for schema completeness, but this FSM **never emits** them — spot-front capture is Chase-only (C1.3), and no FSM condition abandons an episode (`abandoned` defined nowhere = never emitted; strike if still undefined at next registry pass).
- **LOS accumulation is state-independent** (Perception R-gate): an Investigating guard who retains LOS keeps charging A → may reach Chase (state 4 below).

#### C1.3 Chase

- **Chase entry** (decision `Chase-entry`): the FSM's Chase-tier decision record. Opens on:
  - `threshold` cause: Perception's `chase-reached` (A ≥ T_chase) — either direct (from Patrol/Investigating) or after an Investigate-committed episode.
  - `hide-entry` cause: Perception's sight of the player entering a hide spot (the one direct Chase path), **tier-gated (rev 3.5; channel + fallback defined rev 3.6)**:
    - **Gate input channel (CR-FSM-01, rev 3.6; extended rev 3.7 = CR-FSM-01b)** — the gate consumes Perception's amended hide-dive LOS-break fact, which carries **`a_pre_break`** (the accumulator value at the *last pre-break sight tick*), **`threshold_applied`** (the `t_hide_entry` value at that tick), **`spot_position` + `hide_spot_id`** (the entered spot's world position and authored ID), and **`entry_id`** (**no-alloc-when-live, rev 3.8**: Perception allocates a new `entry_id` at the hide-entrance open **only when the guard signals no live episode** — via the published live-Investigate fact / GuardGoalMode feed; if the guard already has a live episode, the break fact instead relays that episode's existing `entry_id`, so the witnessed dive retargets the live episode under tier-scoped suppression rather than leaking a second id). **Liveness snapshot (rev 3.9)**: alloc-vs-relay liveness is evaluated against the guard's published state as of the **previous tick boundary** — an episode closing at tick N does not retroactively make a dive fact queued at N allocation-bearing. The pre-break value is the only meaningful carrier: hide-spot entry *is* an LOS break and R6 resets A at the break itself, so post-break A is always 0 — "A at the moment of entry" is pinned to the last pre-break sight tick. Both gate outcomes construct their targets from `spot_position` and relay `entry_id` from the fact — the FSM never derives a spot target from any other source.
    - **Threshold**: the gate is `a_pre_break ≥ threshold_applied`, where **`t_hide_entry` ≡ Perception F5's entry threshold, consumed verbatim** — derived `T_entry(R) = max(T_base − k_res·R, T_floor)`, starter domain [0.06–0.30], **full-knob domain [0.04–0.40]** (rev 3.8 — registry band-checks and corner asserts evaluate against the full knob domain, not the starter slice; see the forgiveness envelope below for the corner this closes). Renamed rev 3.6 from `T_entry` to avoid colliding with F5's Investigate-entry symbol; same value, distinct name.
    - **A ≥ t_hide_entry** ⇒ `Chase-entry cause=hide-entry` (unchanged from rev 3.5).
    - **A < t_hide_entry — sub-threshold fallback (rev 3.6, creative-director ruling D-B; interior-inspection authority amended rev 3.8, user-approved)**: the dive is NOT silently forgiven. The FSM publishes `investigate-commit cause=hide-entry` targeting the spot position — exact (`spot_position` from the break fact, no error radius). This honors the concept's no-immunity pins ("immunity does NOT protect against a guard who saw the player enter"; the spot becomes that guard's LKP): the guard comes and checks where you went — a stronger Visible Intelligence beat than silence. **Witnessed-entry interior inspection (rev 3.8)**: a witnessed-entry Investigate-tier episode carries the same spot-inspection authority as its Chase-tier sibling — it navigates to the `spot_front_anchor` hold position (M4 applies with state = Investigate), runs the same verification dwell `t_spotfront_verify` with the same kneel-and-verify telegraph, and **if the player still occupies the spot when verification completes, the guard captures them there** (`Capture` with `carve_out=true`, terminal and non-promoting — the episode closes on the Capture without ever publishing `Chase-entry`/`Chase-end`; its Investigate-tier commit stands as the episode's one Investigate-tier record, tier-scoped suppression intact). The approach transit **is the relocation window**: a player who vacates the spot (back exit) before the dwell completes escapes — an empty-spot verification resolves `investigate-resolution cause=fruitless`. Pillar-2 rationale preserved: the *Chase* outcome is what graze frames must not earn; zero response was never on offer.
    - **Forgiveness envelope (restated rev 3.8, formula form)**: charge rate caps at `rate_max` 0.60 A/s, so per-tick charge at point blank is ΔA = `rate_max × T_sample` ∈ [0.12, 0.30] across T_sample's [0.2, 0.5] band. Single-tick point-blank Chase therefore requires `threshold_applied ≤ rate_max × T_sample` — true at starter (0.30 ≤ 0.30), false in general; the corner-failure this formula form exposes: at threshold_applied 0.40 (full-knob max) and T_sample 0.2 s, ΔA/tick = 0.12 < 0.40. General form: **n_ticks_pointblank = ceil(threshold_applied / (rate_max × T_sample))**, domain-bounded by **ceil(threshold_max / (rate_max_min × T_sample_min)) = ceil(0.40 / (0.40 × 0.20)) = 5 ticks** (rev 3.9 general bound; history of the prior "worst corner 4" figure — Appendix H-3). Starter: ceil(0.30/0.30) = 1 tick; starter-rate_max corner: ceil(0.40/0.12) = 4. Point-blank dives still open Chase within a bounded handful of ticks at every legal config; forgiveness exists only at range, and this distance-dependence is deliberate (point-blank dives *should* be punished) and must not surprise tuning or playtest.
    - **Reachability defensive gate (rev 3.6)**: the spot-front hold engages only if the spot position satisfies the published reachable fact; an unreachable spot LKP falls back to the non-hide-spot pursuit path (StaleLKP; give-up runs per the canonical predicate) so termination always remains reachable. In certified content this branch is dead by authoring contract — the concept AC(d)/hide-system pins require every hide spot in the certified-route vicinity to be guard-reachable with bounded depth and |ΔY| — pins this GDD now cites as a **load-bearing Level-GDD dependency** (Dependencies table) with a regression AC (AC-FSM-15).
  - Mutually exclusive openers; no episode opens Chase twice.
- **Chase sub-behaviors** (internal, not states):
  - **Live pursuit**: navigate to the player's sampled feet position (`NavMesh.SamplePosition` at the shared 2–5 Hz throttle), `GuardGoalMode = LivePursuit`. Re-path on the same cadence; the raw-transform Euclidean backstop is the only per-frame pre-check.
  - **Spot-front hold**: when the episode's LKP is a hide spot reached via a **witnessed entry** (either tier, rev 3.8 — formally: episodes carrying `witnessed_entry_authority`, C1.5 rev 3.10: a Chase-entry `cause=hide-entry`, a witnessed sub-threshold Investigate-tier commit, or a converted retarget episode), the guard approaches the spot front and holds there — `GuardGoalMode = HideSpotFront`, catch-timer-live. **Spot front defined (rev 3.7)**: each hide spot authors a **`spot_front_anchor`** (Level/hide-system-authored world position on the approachable face of the spot zone; registered as an authored datum) and the guard's hold position is the anchor offset outward along the guard's approach vector by guard-radius standoff. "Reached the spot front" = arrival predicate (C1.2, XZ ≤ `eps_arrive` to the hold position). Catch timer runs (never pauses) during the hold; give-up + caps suspended. **Presence semantics (pinned rev 3.7 — resolves the R4 LOS-invisibility conflict and the telegraph-preemption hole):**
    - **Sight-presence** (the guard's own vision cone sees the player outside/at the spot) during the hold ⇒ M5 flip → `LivePursuit`, dwell abandoned, normal pursuit resumes.
    - **Reach-presence** (catch-gate pass against the spot interior — the only presence channel for an R4-invisible occupant) does **not** fire capture immediately. **Single-clock capture condition (pinned rev 3.8)**: capture fires on the **first tick where all three hold simultaneously — (1) the catch gate passes (reach-presence), (2) the catch timer's accrued elapsed ≥ `t_catch`, and (3) the verification dwell has completed**. The catch timer runs continuously from hold engagement (catch-timer-live, one clock), so condition (2) is typically satisfied long before the dwell ends and the dwell is the binding constraint — but no "dwell-end + `t_catch`" offset exists; that arithmetic double-counted time against the already-running timer. The kneel-and-verify telegraph always plays in full before any carve-out capture.
    - **Confirm-empty operationalized (rev 3.6; refined rev 3.7; single-clock restated rev 3.8)**: the hold terminates empty when the guard has reached the spot front and completed the **verification dwell `t_spotfront_verify` = 1.5 s [1.0–2.5]** (registered rev 3.6) with no reach-presence gate pass at any tick up to dwell end. Chase-tier holds publish `Chase-end` → Patrol; witnessed Investigate-tier inspections resolve `investigate-resolution cause=fruitless` → Patrol (rev 3.8). Never inferred from "player has no LOS" (that would clear a hidden player by definition).
  - **Catch cycle**: run the catch gate when `GuardGoalMode` is not `StaleLKP`/`None` AND a live player fix is held; on pass, start/run the catch timer (see C1.4).
- **Chase escape contract** (pinned): LOS break → move to LKP → valid re-sight (sustained LOS ≥ `t_resight_min`) restarts pursuit. The Chase give-up window runs on the **same canonical predicate as C1.2**: it ticks iff NOT (sustained LOS AND reachable) — i.e. suspended only while the player is visible-and-reachable; first tick at the first moment that condition fails (LOS break, or LOS retained against an unreachable position). Give-up expiry OR duration cap (`t_cap_chase`) OR re-sight-count cap (`n_resight_cap`) ends the chase — and by the predicate, termination can never fire while staring at a geometrically-reachable player. Re-sight resets only the give-up window, never caps. No instant re-Chase via the reachability carve-out.
- **Chase termination**: Chase ends on give-up expiry / duration cap / re-sight cap / Capture. On termination without capture → **post-chase sweep** (`t_sweep_postchase`, fixed behavior) at the LKP → Patrol. The sweep publishes a **trace-only behavioral marker** `postchase-sweep` (rev 3.5 — not a decision record, does not touch tier-scoped suppression or grading; exists so the Visible Intelligence showcase beat is visible in the trace and measurable by H.0).

#### C1.4 Catch contract (FSM execution)

- **Nav-goal gate** (**state conjunct added rev 3.9; property-scoped rev 3.10**): the catch check runs only while `GuardGoalMode ∈ {LivePursuit, HideSpotFront}` (never `StaleLKP`/`None`) **AND the FSM state is Chase, with one predicate exception: an engaged witnessed-entry HideSpotFront hold keeps the interior check active regardless of Investigate/Chase classification** (Patrol cannot host M4). Formally: `witnessed-entry-hold-engaged := (goal_mode == HideSpotFront) AND (episode carries `witnessed_entry_authority`, C1.5 rev 3.10)` — the property, not the immutable opening cause, is what makes converted retarget episodes eligible (an authority test on opening cause would dead-code the interior check for exactly the retarget path AC-FSM-20 covers). Without the state conjunct, a sight-presence peek-out during a witnessed Investigate-tier dwell flips goal to LivePursuit while state lags in Investigate until `chase-reached` commits — an active gate purely on goal mode lets the retained catch timer (~`t_catch` 1.0 s) beat legitimate Chase accumulation (~1.7 s to T_chase from A = 0 post-dive), producing an out-of-taxonomy capture with no `Chase-entry`. The peek-out pursuit-without-catch window runs until Chase commit — commit-bound at ~T_chase/rate_max ≈ 1.25–2.5 s from A = 0 at close range (rate band [0.40–0.80] per registry; rev 4.0 errata — the rev-3.10 print "[0.60–0.80]" understated both the band and its bound "1.25–1.67+"; the prior "≤ t_resight_min" figure was corrected rev 3.10) — and is the universal Chase-entry commit ritual applied consistently; once Chase commits, the retained timer resolves proximity promptly (chained-hop retention semantics). Timing note (rev 4.1, R8-L1): the "commit-bound > t_catch max 1.2 s" comparison AC-FSM-11(c) cites holds at the pinned T_chase 1.00 (D0a) and is config-derived across the full registry bands — at the band-min corner (T_chase 0.85 / rate_max 0.80 ≈ 1.06 s < 1.2) the ZERO-capture-before-commit assert is enforced structurally by the catch-gate state conjunct (`witnessed-entry-hold-engaged` collapses on the M5 flip), never by this inequality alone. The gate reads the named FSM-owned goal-mode variable (D0c transition table) — never an engine `destination`/`pathEndPosition` query.
- **Interior-check target (datum disambiguation + sampling pin, rev 3.10 R6-B2)**: during a HideSpotFront hold each metric names its own datum — never a player placement sample (an R4-invisible occupant has no current-tick placement fact, Perception R4; reach-presence IS the gate pass against the spot interior, C1.3):
  - **Arrival predicate** → the **hold position** (authored `spot_front_anchor` + guard-radius standoff, C1.3) — unchanged;
  - **Path-arrival metric** → the **same-surface navmesh proxy of `spot_position`** (the CR-FSM-01b break fact's zone datum), sampled once at M4 engagement via `NavMesh.SamplePosition(spot_position, navmesh_sample_maxdistance)` per the Perception R3/J7 convention — never the raw zone center: hide-spot interiors are routinely off-navmesh, and an unsampled `CalculatePath` into an enclosed zone returns `PathInvalid` every tick, dead-coding the chain. Sample failure ⇒ the shallow off-mesh fallback branch below governs (backstop-only, occlusion-gated);
  - **Backstop** → raw `spot_position`: XZ ≤ `catch_range` + `margin` AND |ΔY| ≤ `delta_y_tolerance`, passing only with a geometry-clear shared-config `Physics.Linecast` guard→`spot_position` (the same occlusion gate as the shallow off-mesh fallback — an enclosure wall blocks it).
  The two spot datums differ by standoff + anchor-to-zone depth (up to ≈0.9 m vs margin 0.5) and are never interchangeable; fixture fabric must author the interior off-navmesh as production geometry does (H.0(6), AC-FSM-16(b)).
- **Path-arrival metric** (rev 3.5 — ownership + cost bounds; restatement drift struck rev 3.7): the FSM runs its own agent-parameterized `NavMeshAgent.CalculatePath` (instance) as its sole path source under its **own FSM-local enumerated bounds** — an Euclidean pre-gate (query only if raw XZ distance ≤ `catch_range` + 2 m), at most one query per guard per tick, and the **round-robin stagger (pinned rev 3.10, locked invariant)**: when ≥2 guards simultaneously hold live fixes, fsm-catch-gate query slots rotate so that **no two guards ever emit a catch-gate query on the same virtual tick** (zero-overlap invariant; asserted exactly by AC-FSM-19's same-tick-pair count == 0). **Queryless-tick verdict semantics (pinned rev 4.0 — consolidation completing R6-B3's induced semantics)**: on a tick where a guard's slot grants no query, the path-arrival metric holds that guard's **last emitted verdict (carry-forward)** for as long as the same live fix persists — accrual continues on carried pass-verdicts under the normal pause/reset list (goal-flip, LOS break without hold, range-exit beyond `catch_range` + `hyst`), so stagger never stretches capture beyond the single-clock dwell bound (C1.3); **fail-by-default is forbidden** — it collapses gate-pass accrual to one tick per K-tick cycle, breaking the locked `t_catch ≥ 2×T_sample_max` floor at legal multi-guard configs (asserted by AC-FSM-8's stagger-silence legs). The carry-forward scopes to the queried path metric only; the Euclidean backstop still evaluates live on every tick (it consumes no query slot). These are *distinct from and additive to* Perception R1's d-metric cap (once per 500 ms inside its gate band) — R1's binding is never restated here; this document only claims its own. Combined worst case ≈ Perception 2 Hz + FSM ≤ 5 Hz = ≤ 7 synchronous path queries/s/guard, documented against the Technical-Setup sensing calibration story (ADJ-(r)). **Simultaneous-expiry precedence (pinned rev 3.7)**: if give-up expiry and catch-timer completion land on the same tick, **catch completion wins** (capture precedes termination). A `PathInvalid` result mid-OffMeshLink defers (keep the prior tick's verdict, Perception E23); any other `Invalid` ⇒ unreachable this tick. Path distance from guard to the player's current reachable sample ≤ `catch_range` + `margin` (**margin = 0.5 m starter**, ≤ `hyst`). Partial paths (`PathPartial`) scored by cumulative corner distance to the closest reachable corner, **restricted to genuine stand-offs** (corner-to-live-transform segment geometry-clear via `Physics.Linecast` with capsule-height offsets, `QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore`, World/solid layer-mask). An obstructed partial path never passes.
- **Backstop**: XZ-plane distance to live transform ≤ `catch_range` + `margin` AND |ΔY| ≤ `delta_y_tolerance` — ANDed as a hard gate; never an XZ+ΔY L1 sum. **Dual path-query divergence rule (rev 3.6)**: Perception's embedded path-arrival (inside the published reachable fact) and the FSM's own catch-gate `CalculatePath` may diverge within a tick; the published fact governs the give-up predicate, the FSM-local query governs the catch metric only — a divergence never publishes anything and never feeds the other verdict. **Shallow off-mesh fallback (rev 3.5; occlusion-gated rev 3.6)**: if no reachable sample exists within `navmesh_sample_maxdistance` but the player's XZ position lies within `catch_range` of the guard and |ΔY| ≤ `delta_y_tolerance`, the backstop alone may pass (path metric waived) **only with a geometry-clear `Physics.Linecast` (the shared sensing config: capsule-height offsets, `QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore`, World/solid mask) between the guard's and the player's positions** — |ΔY| reaches 2.0 m at band max, so without the occlusion check a guard could capture vertically through a catwalk or grate (rev 3.6). Deep off-mesh remains governed by the concept AC(d) rule (Edge Cases row).
- **Hysteresis boundaries (rev 3.5)**: catch-timer accrual engages when path/backstop distance ≤ `catch_range`; it pauses only when distance exceeds `catch_range` + `hyst`. Between the two thresholds, prior state holds (no flicker resets).
- **Catch timer** (rev 3.5; **live-fix definition FSM-localized rev 3.8**): accrues virtual-tick time only while the gate passes and a **live fix** is held. *Live fix* (FSM-local verdict, rev 3.8): a current-tick sensing fact places the player at a sampled position **and the FSM's own continuity evidence keeps that fix alive until it is relinquished per the reset list below** — a stale LKP being pursued after LOS break is NOT a live fix. **Hide-spot persistence (pinned rev 3.9)**: during a witnessed-entry hold, the sight-of-entry fix REMAINS a live fix while held — an R4-invisible occupant never generates a current-tick placement fact, so requiring one per-tick would dead-code the entire carve-out chain; continuity evidence operationally means *nothing on the reset list below has occurred*. The definition deliberately makes no reference to Perception's published Reachability fact: the prior wording ("reachable-or-backstop-passing") smuggled Perception's throttled path query back into the catch chain — exactly what the rev-3.7 D-x3 ruling struck from `catch_gate_pass` (D0b); the divergence rule (below) already governs how the two verdicts coexist. Pauses on: goal-mode flips (the flip tick counts as paused — the catch-gate `CalculatePath` instance call is synchronous; the steering layer's `SetDestination` is a separate async pipeline whose pending span the path-end arm's suppression window handles, C1.2 rev 3.9), LOS break without HideSpotFront hold, and range-exit beyond `catch_range` + `hyst`. Elapsed time is retained, never reset by flickers. Resets only when the fix is fully relinquished (stale-LKP arrival, confirm-empty, or Chase termination) — except the HideSpotFront hold, where **only the LOS-break pause is suspended** (catch-timer-live; pause-scope ruling rev 3.9, concept-parent-wins); range-exit beyond `catch_range` + `hyst` retains its nominal pause — timing-moot during the hold itself since accrual gates on catch-gate pass (early accrual is possible at close-witness corners, where the metric can pass against the interior proxy at M4 engagement pre-arrival, but it is masked by the single-clock dwell bound, C1.3 — corrected rev 3.10).
- **Capture** = the catch check succeeding, not an animation. On a **Chase-tier** Capture: publish `Capture` (cause + carve_out flag) + `Chase-end` (Capture implies Chase-end — Chase-tier episodes only, rev 3.8). On a **witnessed Investigate-tier** interior-inspection Capture (C1.3 rev 3.8): publish `Capture` alone with `carve_out=true` — no `Chase-entry`/`Chase-end` exist in that episode and none are emitted; tier-scoped suppression stays intact. Capture is Terminal either way — the episode ends.
- **Live pursuit catch point**: on any LOS contact, the goal flips to `LivePursuit` immediately; catch check is live for any player within reach even for a sub-min-duration peek.

**Transition summary — all legitimate edges:**

- **Patrol → Investigate**: commit decision from a sensing fact (threshold-elapsed, noise-heard, alert, cap-forced, **sub-threshold hide-entry fallback** — C1.0 rank 4, rev 3.8). Publishes `investigate-commit`.
- **Patrol → Chase**: threshold Chase (chase-reached) or hide-entry chase. Publishes `Chase-entry`.
- **Investigate → Chase**: chase-reached (A ≥ T_chase) or a witnessed hide-entry passing the gate (`a_pre_break ≥ t_hide_entry`) while investigating — exactly two openers, both publishing `Chase-entry` (C1.3); the Investigate episode closes via `investigate-resolution cause: sight-committed`.
- **Patrol/Investigate → Investigate (sub-threshold hide-entry fallback, rev 3.6; re-ranked rev 3.8)**: witnessed dive with `a_pre_break < t_hide_entry` ⇒ `investigate-commit cause=hide-entry` targeting the spot (no silent forgiveness; C1.3). The fallback holds escalation-rank 4 (C1.0) — it beats `confirm-window-elapsed` and `noise-heard`, so the witnessed dive always publishes its spot-directed record unless ranks 1–3 win. If already investigating, the episode retargets to the spot under tier-scoped suppression (no second Investigate-tier record). A witnessed-entry Investigate episode that finds the player inside at verification completes captures there (interior inspection, C1.3 rev 3.8); an empty one resolves `fruitless`.
- **Investigate / Chase → Patrol**: give-up expiry, sweep done, capture-following-tail.
- **Chase → Patrol** (behavioral post-chase sweep, in-line): Chase gives up without capture → fixed-length sweep at LKP (**no new decision record**, trace-only `postchase-sweep` marker), then → Patrol. The sweep is Chase-termination behavior — it never opens an Investigate episode and publishes no `investigate-commit`.

**Never-edges** (anti-pillars): no state other than the three above; no transition *from* Capture (Capture is terminal — the episode is over); no `Chase → Chase` self-loop; no transition that violates the hard 3-state cap.

### D0b — Reachability: published fact vs FSM-local gate (rev 3.5)

Two distinct verdicts — never conflated:

1. **`reachable` (Perception-owned, published)**: Perception R1/J2's *un-gated* per-tick unified verdict — **consumed verbatim as the three-conjunct fact it is**: R3 same-surface sample (`NavMesh.SamplePosition` within `navmesh_sample_maxdistance`) **AND** path-arrival within the catch envelope **AND** |ΔY| ≤ `delta_y_tolerance`. Published as the Reachability fact (with `path_arrival_m`, `delta_y_m`). This ONE fact feeds Perception's F2 Chase-cap and R15 give-up vocabulary, and is consumed by this FSM. It knows nothing about FSM state. (History: the struck two-conjunct restatement — Appendix H-4.)
2. **`catch_gate_pass` (FSM-local, never published as a sensing fact)**: the FSM computes locally, per tick. **Rev 3.7 (D-x3 ruling): the published `reachable` conjunct is STRUCK from this formula** — its embedded Perception-side path leg gated catch on Perception's throttled 500 ms query, contradicting the divergence rule (C1.4). The gate is now purely FSM-local: `catch_gate_pass = live_fix_held AND goal_mode ∈ {LivePursuit, HideSpotFront} AND (state == Chase OR witnessed-entry-hold-engaged) AND path_arrival_metric_pass` — *(state conjunct added rev 3.9, property-scoped rev 3.10: `witnessed-entry-hold-engaged` := goal-mode HideSpotFront ∧ episode `witnessed_entry_authority` (C1.5/C1.4) — the M4 predicate on either tier, covering converted retarget episodes; without it a peek-out during a witnessed Investigate-tier dwell captures out-of-taxonomy on the retained timer)*. Safety is preserved without it: C1.4's *live fix* definition (FSM-localized rev 3.8) is itself a purely FSM-local continuity verdict — it never consults the published Reachability fact — and the local path metric governs the catch verdict alone. The goal-mode conjunction lives HERE only — folding it into the published fact would make the fact false during Patrol/Investigate (goal mode None) and break the C1.2 give-up predicate. The `live_fix_held` conjunct mirrors C1.4's catch-cycle precondition ("the catch check runs … AND a live player fix is held") so the formula matches the prose.

The canonical give-up predicate (C1.2/C1.3) consumes ONLY the published `reachable`, never `catch_gate_pass`.

**Worked example** (starter config): guard at LKP transit after LOS break → goal_mode = StaleLKP. Case (a), player sampleable but on a path-disconnected ledge: the path-arrival conjunct fails ⇒ published `reachable = false`; give-up clock ticks iff ¬(sustained LOS ∧ reachable); no LOS here ⇒ **ticks** ✓. Catch side: with no live fix and StaleLKP, `catch_gate_pass = false` on the live-fix/goal-mode legs regardless of what the local metric would say ✓. Case (b), player fully reachable but no live fix (stale LKP): `reachable = true`, `catch_gate_pass = false`. Case (c), within-tick divergence (rev 3.7): published `reachable = false` (Perception's throttled query last failed) while the FSM-local metric passes in LivePursuit mid-Chase with a live fix — state == Chase satisfies the rev-3.9 conjunct — ⇒ `catch_gate_pass = true` (catch timer accrues — the local metric governs catch only), while the give-up predicate still reads the published fact and ticks. Neither verdict feeds the other; if give-up expiry and catch completion land together, catch wins (C1.4 precedence pin) ✓.

### D0c — GuardGoalMode enum + transition table
```
guard_goal_mode = { LivePursuit, HideSpotFront, StaleLKP, None }
```
Used by C1.4 (catch-gate) to decide if the catch check runs:
- {LivePursuit, HideSpotFront} → catch active
- {StaleLKP, None} → catch inactive

GuardGoalMode is NOT an FSM state (Patrol/Investigate/Chase) — it is a parallel mode variable owned by the catch contract. It is published as an informational fact on the bus each tick it changes (R14; Perception consumes it informationally) — never exposed as a synchronous getter across the event boundary.

**Transition table (rev 3.5 — the mode's own dynamics):**

| # | FSM state | Trigger | Mode set |
|---|-----------|---------|----------|
| M1 | Patrol / Investigate | default at state entry | `None` |
| M2 | Chase | Chase-entry (any cause), live player fix held | `LivePursuit` |
| M3 | Chase | LOS break mid-pursuit (non-hide-spot LKP) | `StaleLKP` for the transit; on arrival at LKP without re-sight, stays `StaleLKP` until re-sight/termination |
| M4 | Chase **or Investigate (episode carrying `witnessed_entry_authority`, rev 3.8/3.10)** | episode LKP is a hide spot from a witnessed entry AND published-reachable → spot-front hold engaged | `HideSpotFront` |
| M5 | any hold/pursuit | valid re-sight (sustained ≥ t_resight_min) or fresh live fix | `LivePursuit` |
| M6 | Chase ends (give-up/cap/Capture) | termination processed | back to `None` |

**Precedence pin (rev 3.6; table repaired rev 3.7, either-tier + mid-Chase pin rev 3.8 — this note follows the full table)**: M4 is evaluated **before** M2 — a hide-entry Chase whose LKP spot is published-reachable enters in `HideSpotFront` directly (never a LivePursuit flash against the spot interior, which could pass the path metric through the front with no verification telegraph). M2 governs all other Chase entries. If the spot LKP fails the published reachable fact, M4 is skipped entirely and the M3 non-hide-spot path applies (defensive fallback, C1.3). **Mid-Chase second hide-dive (pinned rev 3.8)**: a new witnessed dive during a live Chase re-engages M4 on the new spot — fresh approach, fresh verification dwell, fresh telegraph; the catch timer is **retained** (continuously-held live pursuit, same rule as the chained-hop Edge Case), and **no new decision record publishes** (there is no `Chase → Chase` self-loop; the episode continues under its existing `Chase-entry`). Gate tier-selection (`a_pre_break` vs `threshold_applied`) is an **episode-opening decision only** — mid-Chase break facts are consumed and retarget without re-running the gate.

The interval between LOS break and LKP arrival is `StaleLKP` — exactly when the catch gate must be closed and the catch timer paused (C1.4 pause rule reads this).

### D1 — Investigate give-up clock (F8, FSM-owned execution; semantics rev 3.5)

The FSM is primarily a state machine; its "formulas" are mostly the FSM-owned clocks and the consumption of registered couplings. All values below are **consumed verbatim from `design/registry/entities.yaml`** (never re-declared); any new FSM-owned knob is flagged for registry registration.

```
t_giveup = t_giveup_base × s_diff × (1 + k_thorough × (R / R_max))
```

| Variable | Type | Value | Source |
|----------|------|-------|--------|
| t_giveup_base | float | 4.0 s [3.5–5.0] | registry |
| s_diff | float | 1.0 [0.7–1.6] | registry |
| k_thorough | float | 0.50 [0.25–1.0] | registry |
| R | float | 0–R_max | Perception residual |
| R_max | float | 1.0 | registry |

**Semantics (rev 3.5; start point hardened rev 3.6; detection predicates pinned rev 3.7 — see C1.2)**: `t_giveup` is a **search budget** — it starts at whichever comes first: arrival at the investigation target OR the agent stopping at the closest reachable point to the target (path end; both via the C1.2 pinned predicates; unreachable targets can never stall the clock — Perception R15 self-resolve honored), and ticks iff ¬(sustained LOS ∧ reachable) (canonical predicate, C1.2).

**Expected output**: R=0 → 4.0 s; R=R_max → 6.0 s; starter band **[4.0, 6.0]**; full domain [2.45, 16.0]. Verified by ai-programmer against the registry.

**Worked example**: starter config, R = 0.4 → t_giveup = 4.0 × 1.0 × (1 + 0.50 × 0.4) = **4.8 s** of visible searching after arrival. Full-domain minimum 2.45 s (base 3.5 × s_diff 0.7 × τ 1.0 at R = 0) still > `t_catch` max 1.2 s (D7 leg holds).

### D2 — Look-around thoroughness factor tau

```
tau = 1 + k_thorough × (R / R_max)
```

The look-around sub-behavior scales **sweep count only** by `tau`; per-arc duration stays fixed at `t_sweep_base` (rev 3.6 resolution of the D3 contradiction: count-scaling + duration-scaling would be O(τ²) against an O(τ) budget — the τ² bug this section was written to kill):

```
n_sweep(R) = ceil(n_sweep_ref × tau)      # sweep arcs in the look-around sequence
t_sweep    = t_sweep_base                  # FIXED per-arc duration (no × tau)
```

with **`n_sweep_ref` = 4 (locked, registered rev 3.6)** — the R=0 base arc count (cardinal offsets around the target). At starter k_thorough: R=0 → 4 arcs; R=R_max → ceil(4×1.5) = 6 arcs; band-max k_thorough → 8 arcs. `tau` is the same sub-expression as the F8 scaling factor — extracted and reused, not re-derived. *(rev 3.6 note: the prior text claimed "arc geometry per node fixed" while D3 scaled arc duration — both cannot fit the budget; count is the surviving axis because more sweeps reads clearly under Visible Intelligence.)*

### D3 — Sweep duration budget (bridges tau → time)

The Investigate look-around consumes sweep time within the give-up budget; the per-sweep arc duration is **fixed** (rev 3.6 — the tau axis is count, D2; value lowered rev 3.7, user-approved):

```
t_sweep = t_sweep_base              # FSM-owned knob t_sweep_base = 0.8 s (registered rev 3.7); no × tau
n_sweep = ceil(n_sweep_ref × tau)   # DEMANDED arcs (D2); n_sweep_ref = 4 locked
n_perf  = min(n_sweep, floor(t_giveup(config) / t_sweep_base))   # PERFORMED arcs after truncation
```

**Venue resolution (rev 3.7 — fixes the config-time/per-episode contradiction)**: the ONLY config-time assert is the per-arc bound `t_sweep_base < t_giveup_base` (band-min 3.5 s). The per-episode product containment is **runtime enforcement via truncation, not a config assert** — a runtime R makes it non-evaluable at load time, and a worst-case config-time reading would force `t_sweep_base ≤ 0.306 s` (unsatisfiable). When the demanded sequence exceeds the remaining budget, it truncates at the budget edge (`n_perf` arcs play) and the episode resolves `fruitless`. **Expected performed arcs**: `min(ceil(4τ), floor(t_giveup / 0.8))` derived at config time — starter: R=0 → min(4, 5) = 4 arcs (3.2 s of 4.0 s budget); R=0.5·R_max → min(5, 6) = 5; R=R_max → min(6, 7) = 6 (4.8 s of 6.0 ✓); band-max k_thorough R_max → min(8, 10) = 8. With t_sweep_base 0.8 the thoroughness mechanic now manifests monotonically across the whole residual range at starter (the 1.0 s value truncated demand>budget over most of R∈(0, R_max)); truncation remains live code at band-min corners (t_giveup(R=0) 2.45 s ⇒ floor gives 3 arcs — below the 4-arc cardinal baseline, an accepted documented corner, not silently hidden). ACs assert against `n_perf`, never raw `n_sweep`.

Post-Chase sweep ties to `t_sweep_postchase` (2.0 s starter, [1.5–3.0], registered Phase 5 2026-08-18; satisfies the family `≥ 2×T_sample_max` floor and `< t_giveup_chase`).

### D4 — Chase speed coupling (consume F11)

```
V_chase = rho_chase × V_run(runtime-max)
```

**Invariant**: `V_chase ≥ 1.10 × V_run(runtime-max)`. Starter: 1.20 × 6.25 = **7.50 m/s** (≥ 6.875 ✓). Band-max floor: 1.10 × 7.2 = **7.92 m/s**. Consumed, never re-derived.

### D5 — Catch-range invariant (F10)

```
catch_range > V_run × T_sample_max + hyst + r_guard + r_player + stopping_distance
```

**Binding floor: 5.05 m** (max-of-band: V_run 7.2 × T_sample_max 0.5 + hyst 0.7 + 0.40 + 0.35 = 5.05). Starter-only evaluation (V_run 6.25, hyst 0.5) = 4.375 m — *not* the operative floor; the invariant must hold at the worst legal corner. catch_range 5.5 > 5.05 → margin 0.45 m. catch_range's own safe-band floor 5.2 > 5.05 (redundant 0.15 m).

### D6 — Patrol period derivation

```
T_patrol_loop = Σ_i ( edge_i / V_patrol + t_dwell_i + t_scan_i )
```

where `edge_i` is the navmesh path length from node i to i+1, `t_dwell_i` the dwell at node i, `t_scan_i` the scan-span at node i. This defines the deterministic patrol period the concept's AC(a) start-phase window (≤ half a period) is measured against. `t_dwell_i` / `t_scan_i` are Level-authored waypoint-node knobs (**registered** in entities.yaml, Phase 5 2026-08-18).

**Route-period guard (rev 3.5; provenance fixed rev 3.6)**: node count, dwell, and scan-span have no engine-side cap, so a config-time validation assert requires `T_patrol_loop ≤ T_route_budget_max` — registered rev 3.6 as a **provisional** constant (`t_route_budget_max` = 90 s [45–180], registry; the Level GDD owns its final value and supersedes the provisional band on landing). A violating route fails config validation loudly; MVP's hand-authored one-room routes are far under any sane budget. *(rev 3.6 correction: this section previously claimed the existing registry dwell entries already carried this budget — they did not; false provenance struck and the constant actually registered.)* A minimum-period guard also applies (rev 3.6): a nonempty route is required — a zero-node/zero-edge route fails validation instead of passing vacuously with T_patrol_loop = 0.

### D7 — Constraint chain (verified at band corners, rev 3.5)

`T_sample_max < t_resight_min < t_giveup_chase < t_cap_chase` — worst corners: 0.5 < 1.0 < 6.0 < 20 ✓ · `t_catch ≥ 2×T_sample_max` (band-min 1.0 ≥ 1.0 ✓) · `t_catch max 1.2 < t_giveup min 2.45` ✓ · `rho_chase > 1.0` (band-min 1.10 ✓) · `catch_range band-min 5.2 > binding floor 5.05` ✓ · `t_sweep_postchase max 3.0 < t_giveup_chase min 6.0` ✓.

**Joint-coupling legs (rev 3.6 — enforcement venue pinned)**: two relational constraints must hold in EVERY FSM config and are enforced by a **config-time joint assert in the FSM config loader** (the registry AC-F14(9) CI leg is its mirror, not its venue): (1) `V_patrol < V_investigate ≤ V_run` — independent bands admit violating corners (V_investigate 5.5 > V_run 5.0; registry J8 note); starter values (2.3 < 5.0 ≤ 6.25) satisfy it but are not themselves the proof. (2) `margin ≤ hyst` — margin's band [0.25–0.5] overlaps hyst's band-minimum 0.3 below it, so a legal-looking config pairing margin 0.5 with hyst 0.3 violates the concept's "margin committed ≤ hysteresis" pin; the joint assert catches it at load (registered rev 3.6). A violating combination fails validation loudly.

**Termination-precedence note (rev 3.5; arithmetic corrected rev 3.6)**: worst-case chase length ≈ (n_resight_cap + 1) × t_giveup_chase — the +1 counts the window running when the final re-sight lands, charged at full length as an upper bound (window-count semantics pinned rev 3.6). This can exceed or undercut `t_cap_chase` depending on config: band maxima give **(3+1) × 10 = 40 > 20** (vs t_cap_chase band-min); band minima give (1+1) × 6 = 12 < 20 — i.e. WHICH designed termination fires first flips across legal configs. (History: the corrected "3×10 = 30" misprint — Appendix H-5.) Pinned precedence: on simultaneous expiry, `t_cap_chase` wins (duration cap is the outer guard); document per-config which path dominates in the fixture log so tuning changes of behavior class are visible, not silent.
