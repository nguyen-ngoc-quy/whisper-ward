# Game Concept: Whisper Ward (working title: "The Phantom")

*Created: 2026-08-12*
*Status: Approved (conditional — 2026-08-17, review #24)*

---

## Elevator Pitch

> It's a third-person stealth/adventure game where you infiltrate a moonlit,
> guard-patrolled facility and outwit an intelligent AI — guards who see, hear,
> investigate, and coordinate to hunt you. Your mission: steal the artifact and
> escape without ever being caught.

---

## Core Identity

| Aspect | Detail |
| ---- | ---- |
| **Genre** | Stealth / Adventure (third-person) |
| **Platform** | PC (Windows), with WebGL demo build for portfolio sharing |
| **Target Audience** | Mid-core PC gamers who enjoy mastery-style stealth (see Player Profile) |
| **Player Count** | Single-player |
| **Session Length** | 45-60 minutes for a first full run (~35-40 for quicker players with minimal observation — a stated sub-×1.3 case); ~20-30 minutes mastered |

| **Comparable Titles** | Dishonored, Splinter Cell, Alien: Isolation |

---

## Core Fantasy

> You are the ghost in the machine — the unseen planner who reads a living,
> thinking security system and threads through it with surgical precision. The
> guards aren't obstacles on a rail; they're an intelligence you learn, bait,
> divert, and exploit. The reward isn't loot — it's the moment a guard walks
> past your hiding spot, none the wiser, because *you* set up the situation.

---

## Unique Hook

> It's like Splinter Cell with AI-driven enemies, **AND ALSO** the guards think
> for themselves — a self-written AI: raycast vision + event-based hearing, a
> suspicion meter that fills fast and lingers after breaking line of sight, coordinated alert propagation, and a
> last-known-position chase that retargets on re-sight. The AI is a living
> adversary you play against, not a scripted patrol.

| **Fantasy** (make-believe, role-playing) | 2 | You are the phantom infiltrator — a ghost in the machine, outwitting a living security system |
| **Narrative** (drama, story arc) | N/A | Light layer only — guard routines, diary fragments. Story is not the focus |
| **Challenge** (obstacle course, mastery) | 1 | Fair mind-challenge — learn and exploit the AI; early-warning suspicion meter + S/A/B room-grading mastery loop |

### Key Dynamics (Emergent player behaviors)
- Players will *watch* guards to learn their patrol routes and tells before acting.
- Players will experiment with bait/distraction to reshape a room's guard flow.
- Players will attempt "clean runs" — completing the facility without ever being spotted.

### Core Mechanics (Systems we build)
1. **Guard AI controller** — FSM (Patrol → Investigate → Chase) with per-state action lists; a *minimal self-written Behavior Tree* drives one guard end-to-end as the portfolio showcase (built after the FSM is proven). Hard 3-state cap.
2. **Perception system** — raycast vision cones (FOV angle + range + line-of-sight), event-based hearing (radius + occlusion), early-warning suspicion meter with a committed two-component semantic model: **(a) A sustained-LOS accumulator** that fills quickly — **charge scales inversely with distance to the guard**, with a hard per-tick cap (`rate = min(k/d, rate_max)`: closer fills faster **up to the cap** — within catch range the rate is capped so no single instant can dump the meter) (prototype-validated shape-level, N=1 internal; closer = fills faster, so risk is readable at the moment it matters) — crossing the Investigate threshold within a brief confirm window (an LOS break inside that window cancels the escalation — only sustained sight past the window commits to Investigate) and reaching Chase when the accumulator reaches the Chase threshold (which, given the distance-inverse fill rate, requires sustained continuous LOS). The accumulator **fully resets on LOS break and on a segment transition** — the fixed early-warning grace that makes a single clean break survivable, and the mechanism that realizes "carried-in exposure does not penalize the new segment" (a partial carry is penalized where it accrued, then cleared). **(b) A separate residual** — the designated within-run tension lever — that **charges in parallel with the accumulator during any LOS exposure outside of Chase at a lower rate**, persists after LOS break, and decays slowly to zero (bounded, fully de-escalating, never a permanent ratchet). The residual lowers the Investigate **entry** threshold for **any subsequent exposure episode by the same guard** — the first entry uses the base threshold; every later entry uses the residual-lowered value — not merely Investigate-state re-entry, so repeated peeks self-escalate rather than being exploitable via peek→break→repeat (a player can spend only a *bounded* time under base before the residual makes the next peek bite). The threshold drop is **player-visible** (a threshold marker that sinks on the meter as residual builds — Pillar 4) and **MVP-visible** (a meter element in the tester build, not the Target-tier attribution HUD). The residual is **clamped** (`R ∈ [0, R_max]`) and the effective Investigate entry threshold is **floored**: `T_entry = max(T_base − k_res·R, 0.2 × T_base)` (with `k_res` the residual-to-threshold coupling coefficient, distinct from the distance-falloff gain `k`) — a residual-wary guard tightens the window, but the threshold can never reach zero or go negative (that would break the crossing semantics and read as random detection, violating Pillar 2). The residual's decay rate is a first-order tuning knob with a target scale — decays to zero over ~45-60 s, so wariness persists within a segment but guards reset between encounters. It also makes the exposed guard **investigate more thoroughly** (longer hold, broader look-around), and **never accumulates toward Chase**. In addition, **fruitless noise investigations** (a noise-triggered Investigate that resolves without LOS exposure) accrue a bounded share of residual wariness — so repeated tool use carries a real in-world consequence and "none the wiser" must be earned, not given. Noise never feeds the accumulator — only LOS does. **Chase entry is the accumulator reaching the Chase threshold** (the meter reading, not a separate timer), with **one explicit direct path: personal sight of the player entering a hide spot** — the guard who sees the player dive into a spot enters Chase immediately (LKP = the spot, live fix on the spot's front), the mechanism that makes a hide spot never capture immunity (see the hide system) — and **the accumulator resets when Chase begins**, so the LOS break that starts last-known-position pursuit is a state transition, not a suppressed reset event, and post-Chase the meter starts at zero. **During Chase both the accumulator and the residual are inert** (no accumulation, no reset). **Chase escape contract**: LOS break → guard moves to the last-known-position → re-sight restarts the pursuit (re-sight requires sustained LOS ≥ a minimum duration — a single-tick occlusion at a corner does not reset the hunt) → no valid re-sight within the chase give-up window OR a total chase bound reached (a chase-duration cap and a re-sight-count cap bound the hunt: the chase ends when the give-up window expires with no valid re-sight, **or** the duration cap is reached, **or** the re-sight-count cap is reached; **a valid re-sight is re-acquisition of sustained LOS after a LOS break — a maintained sight is a continuation, not a new re-sight; the give-up window is suspended during any sustained continuous LOS on the player — initial acquisition or re-acquired — and first starts ticking at the first LOS break; the duration and re-sight-count caps never terminate a chase while the player is in sustained continuous LOS** — a chase ends only on a valid re-sight's break or give-up-while-hunting, never while staring at a ***geometrically reachable* player — "geometrically reachable" is evaluated by the **full catch gate — reachable ⟺ the catch gate can fire: the path-arrival metric AND the |ΔY|-gated Euclidean backstop, one rule — never path-arrival alone, so a |ΔY|-failing ramp/landing player is unreachable for both the give-up resume and the accumulator cap; the path-arrival metric (navmesh path distance from the guard to the player's reachable sample ≤ catch range + margin, with partial paths scored by cumulative corner distance restricted to Linecast-clear genuine stand-offs — the same gated rule the catch gate fires on) is the primary check**, so reachability and catchability are one rule and the runtime qualifier can never disagree with the AC(d) certification sweep (if the player remains in sustained continuous LOS but is geometrically unreachable — the path-arrival metric fails, i.e. navmesh path distance from the guard to the player's reachable sample exceeds catch range + margin or the partial-path Linecast is not clear, OR the |ΔY|-gated Euclidean backstop fails — the give-up window resumes ticking whenever the catch gate cannot fire (catch = path-arrival AND backstop, so its negation is OR: either half failing terminates the chase rather than livelocking it; for a **hide-spot** LKP this resume is overridden by the hide-spot suspension below — the give-up is suspended through the approach and spot-front verification, so the resume applies to **non-hide-spot** LKPs only), and the chase terminates on its expiry, the guard returning to Investigate at the LKP, with no instant re-Chase because the player is unreachable; the window re-suspends if the player becomes reachable before expiry), and the accumulator cannot reach the Chase threshold while the player is geometrically unreachable by the catch gate's metric — while unreachable, only Investigate-level accumulation proceeds, so a give-up-expiry → Investigate transition cannot instantly re-Chase through the accumulator path on a staring-but-unreachable player (the 'no instant re-Chase' guarantee is a mechanism, not an annotation), so there is no give-up-while-staring / instant re-Chase loop; re-sight resets only the give-up window, never the duration cap; both caps are per-Chase-episode; sub-threshold LOS contact does not reset the give-up window; re-sight min-duration < give-up window < chase-duration cap — so a corner-stalemate can't be won for free; **and guard-chase-speed > player-run-speed is a hard invariant of the catch contract (else a player could kite an un-catchable guard indefinitely under the sustained-LOS carve-out — no catch, caps suspended; the speed ratio is a Perception-GDD tuning knob)**) → Chase ends → a brief Investigate sweep at the LKP → Patrol. **Capture** is a catch check — guard within catch range of the **player's live position** for a short timer — not a literal animated body-catch. There is **no LOS component**: a catch reads as "the guard got within reach," not "the guard saw you" (a single-frame raw-LOS gate would read as random, violating Pillar 2). The operative catch proximity for on-mesh-reachable players is **navmesh path distance from the guard to the player's *current reachable* position** — the navmesh-sampled position nearest the live transform (never the sampled chase target — that is the chase *target*, not the catch reference — never the destination, never the stale LKP) — so the catch **cannot resolve before the guard's path has arrived** and never fires through intervening geometry; the **live-transform Euclidean distance in the XZ plane with a vertical-delta tolerance** (`|ΔY| ≤ the committed |ΔY| tolerance` (≈1.0-1.5 m is authoring guidance; the single committed value is asserted at the milestone-0 gate), covering feet-to-navmesh offset and slope; never 3D straight-line, which fires through floors/ceilings) is demoted to the **off-mesh/backstop fallback**. The guard's chase target is the player's **NavMesh-sampled feet position** (`NavMesh.SamplePosition` with a **bounded `maxDistance`**, refreshed at a throttled cadence ~2-5 Hz, never every frame), with the catch check's navmesh path-arrival metric and reachable-sample lookup refreshed on the same shared 2-5 Hz tick (never every frame — a per-frame path query is a synchronous hitch source, worst on single-threaded WebGL; the raw-transform Euclidean backstop is the only per-frame pre-check), and the catch check runs **only while the guard holds a live player fix AND the nav-goal gate passes — the guard's *current navigation goal* is the player's vicinity (live pursuit — Chase with sustained LOS, or a valid re-sight) or the spot front (a hide-spot fix the player is believed to occupy; **the spot front is the navmesh-sampled point nearest the hide spot's entry/cover boundary where the guard halts to verify — geometry enumerated in the Level GDD**); it is never active during travel to a stale LKP with no player-ward goal — **the nav-goal gate reads a named FSM-owned goal-mode variable (e.g., `GuardGoalMode { LivePursuit, HideSpotFront, StaleLKP, None }`), never a `NavMeshAgent` engine query (`destination`/`pathEndPosition` do not change when a fix goes stale, so an engine-keyed gate would reproduce the through-wall false positive)** — AND the path-arrival metric passes — the navmesh path distance from the guard to the player's *current reachable* position is ≤ catch range + margin, **computed via the agent-parameterized `NavMeshAgent.CalculatePath` (instance) as the sole path source — respecting the guard's radius and areaMask, never the static `NavMesh.CalculatePath`, which takes only an areaMask and ignores the agent's radius/height — an unparameterized centerline path can understate reachability, passing a path the guard cannot physically execute** — with the **margin committed ≤ hysteresis** (a small path-distance jitter tolerance, never an open knob; **a partial path (`NavMeshPathStatus.PathPartial` — the player's reachable sample unreachable, e.g. on a navmesh island or ledge) scores the gate by cumulative corner distance up to the closest reachable corner, passing if ≤ catch range + margin, **restricted to genuine stand-offs only: the corner-to-live-transform segment must be geometry-clear — occlusion-tested by a physics `Physics.Linecast` from the guard's corner to the player's live transform — **both endpoints offset by each actor's current capsule height — the guard's standing height at the corner, the player's live crouch/stand height at their transform — never a fixed +1.0 m, so the ray never lies in the floor collider plane and never passes over sub-1.0 m cover a crouched player genuinely occupies (a floor-level ray returns a PhysX hit at distance ≈0 and always reads blocked by the floor itself), and cast with `QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore` (level triggers — camera LOS zones, hide-spot zones — would otherwise block the ray under the default `queriesHitTriggers = true`)** (guard and player colliders excluded via dedicated collider layers + a layer-mask filter on the Linecast — World geometry only — never `Physics.IgnoreCollision`, which does not affect raycasts; PhysX auto-skips only the collider containing the ray origin, so a bystander guard's collider must also be layer-excluded), which must be clear of intervening solid geometry (`NavMesh.SamplePosition` is a nearest-point query and does not test occlusion, so it cannot serve as the geometry-clear test); a partial path whose obstruction is intervening solid geometry (a wall or a gap through it) does NOT score and cannot pass the gate — preserving the 'never through intervening geometry' commitment**, with the backstop ANDed on top — the shallow-off-mesh "capture still fires" claim is reachable exactly through this scoring**) — so a catch fires only where the guard is actively pursuing the player and physically able to reach them along the navmesh: never through intervening geometry, never on a passing-by/passing-away approach to a stale position** (set on first LOS gain — during Chase pursuit, any LOS contact on the player flips the goal to `LivePursuit` immediately, so the catch check is live for any player within reach, including a point-blank peek shorter than the re-sight min-duration; only the give-up-window reset is gated on a valid re-sight ≥ the min-duration, so a brief corner peek still cannot reset the hunt — on "saw the player enter a hide spot", or on Chase entry with sustained LOS on the player — the accumulator-crossing path, where the guard is actively pursuing a visible player; cleared when the guard arrives at the LKP and the player is not there — **for non-hide-spot LKPs, arrival with no LOS** — or the chase give-up window expires with no valid re-sight, or the guard redirects to a *new* **stale** LKP **with no live player-ward goal (a redirect during a live pursuit — `LivePursuit` or `HideSpotFront` — re-sets the fix target without clearing the catch timer)**; when the LKP is a hide spot, the fix persists through the guard's approach and spot-front verification, so the catch check is live at the spot until the guard confirms it empty — **for a hide-spot LKP, the give-up window and chase caps are suspended through the approach and spot-front verification (a hide-spot fix is never cleared mid-approach by give-up expiry or a cap; those terminations apply only to non-hide-spot LKPs)** — where **"confirm empty" is operationalized as the spot-front reach check completing with the player not present — never inferred from "the player has no LOS," which would clear the fix on a hidden player by definition; the catch also fires at a hide spot's front whenever the guard holds a live fix on the spot via a **witnessed entry** (CR-CONCEPT-01: via the Chase sight-of-entry path, or via a sub-threshold witnessed dive opening an Investigate-tier spot inspection — never via a noise/alert-directed hunch)**) — so a catch can never fire when the guard is traveling to a stale last-known-position: the nav-goal gate is inactive for stale-LKP travel with no player-ward goal — the "walks past, none the wiser" moment is preserved, and a player hidden near the guard's route to a stale position is never captured through cover. The composition has **no LOS component** — proximity-only capture is preserved — and a hide-spot approach catch still fires because the guard's nav goal *is* the spot front (the guard-reachability pin), with the path-arrival metric measuring the guard's approach to it. Chasers use `stoppingDistance = 0` (well below catch range); the catch timer **pauses** during `pathPending` and across goal flips and LOS breaks while the guard holds a live fix — **retained, never reset** — including across a redirect to a new hide-spot LKP during a continuously-held live pursuit (a chained hide-spot hop re-sets the fix target to the new spot without clearing the catch timer); **with one explicit exemption: while the guard holds a live `HideSpotFront` fix — the spot-front approach and verification hold — the LOS-break pause does not apply; the hold is a catch-timer-live state, a suspension of the pause rather than an LOS-break pause, so the spot-front catch always resolves via the short timer (a hide spot is never capture immunity)**; the catch timer resets only when the live fix is fully relinquished: arrival at a stale LKP with no LOS, a hide spot confirmed empty, or the Chase episode terminating via give-up-window or cap expiry (a chained hide-spot-hop sequence thus accumulates catch time rather than restarting it) — and tolerates intermittent range exits (hysteresis, never a flicker that resets the timer); catch range exceeds guard + player capsule radii + stopping distance **plus the sampled-target lag and hysteresis — `catch range > V_run × T_sample + hysteresis + guard/player capsule radii + stopping distance` is a hard invariant of the catch contract (**hysteresis is a distance in meters — the invariant adds it to a pure distance sum, never a time value**) (`V_run` = max player run speed, `T_sample` = the maximum chase-target sample *period* — the time between sampled feet-position refreshes, `1/cadence`, so `V_run × T_sample` is a distance in meters, 0.2–0.5 s at the 2–5 Hz cadence; both Perception-GDD tuning knobs): the chase target is the 2–5 Hz-sampled feet position while the catch metric is the live transform, so a strictly-faster guard trails a runner by `V_run × T_sample ≈ 1.0–3.6 m`; the catch range must absorb that lag, or a full-speed runner is permanently uncatchable — the exact kiting the guard-chase-speed invariant exists to prevent**. A **Euclidean sanity backstop** (the AND-form Euclidean check — XZ-plane distance to the live transform ≤ catch range + margin AND |ΔY| ≤ the committed |ΔY| tolerance — a hard AND-gate on the player's actual transform; **the authoritative backstop form, matching the primary backstop statement above — never an XZ + |ΔY| L1 sum, which is a different bound with a different pass set and contradicts the committed |ΔY| tolerance — and never reusing the `NavMesh.SamplePosition` output or `remainingDistance`**) prevents a stale or invalid path from ever triggering a catch — **the backstop inherits the nav-goal and path-arrival gates, so it cannot fire the through-wall catch when the primary check is blocked** — and is the fallback when the player is off-mesh (path-partial). **"Genuinely within reach" is consistent across both checks: for on-mesh and shallow off-mesh players (a reachable navmesh sample exists within the bounded `maxDistance`) the backstop's Euclidean test is the same bound the path-arrival metric gates, so capture still fires when the guard is genuinely within reach; for deep off-mesh players (no reachable sample within the bounded `maxDistance` — the nav-goal/path-arrival gates have no input) the catch contract does not apply, and the position is governed by the committed deep-off-mesh definition and AC(d)'s authoring rule — no player-reachable deep off-mesh stand-off exists within the certified-route vicinity — so there is no off-mesh catch immunity; the chase give-up window is reserved for the legitimate eluded case, never off-mesh immunity.** A scripted grab is animation/IK scope, deliberately out of the concept. **Capture is a terminal outcome of Chase** (the catch check runs within Chase), **not a fourth FSM state** — the hard 3-state cap is preserved — **with one explicit carve-out (re-scoped by concept amendment CR-CONCEPT-01, 2026-08-22, to witnessed-entry authority): a guard who *witnessed* the player enter the hide spot captures them there without promoting to Chase — on either tier: the Chase sight-of-entry path at the spot front, or a sub-threshold graze-frame dive that opens a witnessed Investigate whose interior inspection finds the player still inside when verification completes (a physical verification, not a pursuit); hunches never break spots — a noise/alert-directed investigation ending at an occupied spot resolves fruitless and walks away** — preserving the single direct Chase path and the alert-derived-never-chases rule — **the carve-out capture is telegraphed before it resolves (the guard's commit-to-verify-this-spot reads as intent — an approach-intent/alert telegraph committed under Pillar 4, mechanism pinned in the Perception GDD alongside the sight-of-entry telegraph) and appears on the grade card as an escalation event (per the grade-legibility commitment)**. LOS accumulation toward Investigate/Chase is **state-independent across Patrol and Investigate** (a guard who retains LOS while Investigating keeps charging). Investigate **self-resolves** to Patrol after a base give-up timeout (~4 s, prototype-validated) if its target is unreachable — the base is scaled up by difficulty and by the residual-lowered thoroughness of an exposed guard. **While the guard holds a live `HideSpotFront` fix — the spot-front approach and verification hold — the self-resolve is suspended (the hold is a catch-timer-live state, mirroring the catch-timer pause exemption); its terminations are catch or confirm-empty, never the ~4 s self-resolve, so a carve-out spot-front capture cannot be ended early — a hide spot is never capture immunity through the Investigate self-resolve either (`catch-timer < Investigate-timeout` commits as a Perception-GDD ordering backstop alongside the existing ordering chain).** The meter is an early warning because accumulation is visible and the residual lingers, not because there is a long grace period. The strengthened residual (thoroughness + noise-wariness) is to be validated in the engine milestone-0 prototype. Exact rates and thresholds are Perception GDD tuning knobs.
3. **Player noise system** — `NoiseEmitter` that broadcasts noise events scaled to movement state (crouch/walk/run), consumed by guard hearing. Noise events do **not** feed the LOS accumulator — they trigger Investigate/orientation, and suspicion rises only via subsequent LOS. Fruitless noise investigations seed a bounded share of residual wariness (see Perception system), so tool use carries an in-world consequence without ever **directly** grade-penalizing clean tool use.
4. **Alert propagation** — when one guard detects the player, a radius-based alert event brings nearby guards to Investigate. Alerts carry *imprecise* information (an area, not the exact position): **a guard acting on alert-derived information investigates the area with error and never chases on rumor alone — but any guard with personal sustained LOS always promotes to chaser** ("only the detector chases" applies to alert information, not personal sight). Guards ignore self-alerts. **A guard in Chase ignores peer alerts entirely — Chase is the exclusive-highest state; an alert can neither escalate nor redirect an active pursuit (alert redirection applies to Patrol/Investigating guards only).**
5. **Player movement & hide system** — three movement states (crouch/walk/run) with distinct speed + loudness, `HideSpot` zones that make the player **not perceptible to LOS while inside — a genuine LOS break** (immunity does NOT protect against a guard who saw the player enter (a **witnessed entry**, either gate tier — CR-CONCEPT-01, 2026-08-22: a hunch-directed noise/alert investigation does not break spots; only a witnessed entry grants spot-inspection/capture authority) — the spot becomes that guard's last-known-position **and the guard carries a live fix on the spot's front under the capture contract, so a hide spot is never capture immunity within the guard's reach-and-verify envelope (the AC(d) certified-route vicinity), and unreachable deep off-mesh positions are governed by AC(d) geometry verification, not the AI catch contract; **every hide spot within the certified-route vicinity is guard-reachable (a NavMesh path from the guard to the spot front exists) — a Level-GDD authoring pin, along with **each hide spot's interior depth ≤ catch range + margin (the backstop-depth pin: a guard at the spot front can always resolve the reach check against a player inside, so false "confirm empty" cannot occur) and each hide spot's interior vertical offset above the guard's navmesh floor is **strictly below** the committed |ΔY| tolerance (the single committed value asserted at the milestone-0 gate; ≈1.0-1.5 m is authoring guidance) (the backstop vertical pin: an elevated spot beyond the vertical gate would resolve "empty" and become capture immunity, so hide spots sit at or near floor level — swept in AC(d))** — so a chase whose LKP is a hide spot always resolves at the spot (catch or confirm-empty) rather than suspending indefinitely: the give-up window and caps are suspended through spot-front verification, so an unreachable spot would hang the chase forever; guard-reachability closes that hole and is verified as part of AC(d)****). Hiding composes with the confirm-window and re-sight rules: a hide is a real break, never a corner-stalemate the player can exploit for free.
6. **Camera system** (low-scope, Target tier) — static LOS-cone cameras with a disable interaction; they add pressure events without adding guards, supporting the segment/encounter budget. Camera detection follows the guards' early-warning philosophy — **never an instant capture**: camera LOS charges a suspicion analogue at a readable rate, and detection is **visually telegraphed** (a brightening scan / lock-on indicator) before it resolves. On resolution, a full camera meter **routes to the segment's guards as an alert-derived Investigate at the camera's location** (imprecise area, not position) — never a separate camera capture, never instant. **A camera alert that resolves without LOS exposure accrues a bounded share of residual wariness (mirroring fruitless noise investigations), so a camera trip is never a free, unlimited, consequence-free distraction — repeated trips carry an in-world consequence without ever **directly** grade-penalizing a clean crossing.**

## Player Motivation Profile

### Primary Psychological Needs Served

| Need | How This Game Satisfies It | Strength |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| **Autonomy** | Meaningful choice in how to approach each guard: hide, bait, divert, or time it. You create the situation | Core |
| **Competence** | Suspicion meter + AI reactions teach you; skill grows room by room, visible via clean runs | Core |
| **Relatedness** | Minimal by design — you feel *opposed* by the guards; a light pathos layer (guard routines, diary fragments) keeps the world human | Supporting |

### Player Type Appeal (Bartle Taxonomy)

- [x] **Achievers** — the suspicion meter and clean-run feedback give a measurable "did I do it perfectly?" mastery loop.
- [x] **Explorers** — the game rewards observation and system understanding: reading patrols, learning the AI's tells.
- [ ] **Socializers** — solo experience; no social layer.
- [ ] **Killers/Competitors** — no combat, no PvP; a future speedrun community is a passive bonus, not a target.

### Flow State Design

- **Onboarding curve**: First room teaches the core loop gently — one guard, a clear route, and the suspicion meter introduced via a near-miss that *rewards* a hidden approach.
- **Difficulty scaling**: *Parametric* AI tuning (patrol speed, scan rate, suspicion-fill rate, investigate duration) is the primary independent difficulty lever — under the hard 3-state cap — which applies to the **top-level FSM states only**; sub-behaviors within a state (Investigations look-arounds, sweep patterns) are the sophistication lever and live under the same cap — "sophistication" means tuning and sub-behavior detail, not new states. Coverage — the share of a critical corridor's route time swept by the 2-3 guards — is a bounded spatial-redistribution variable (raising it in one segment draws route time from elsewhere; the feasible envelope is committed in the Level GDD), escalating along the player's path. The player's growing mastery is the difficulty curve.
- **Feedback clarity**: Legible AI states (vision cone, "alert" animation, investigation look-around), suspicion meter, and sound feedback — the player always knows why they were spotted.
- **Recovery from failure**: Fast respawn at segment/checkpoint start; failure is educational (you saw the AI react) and never a long restart. A capture taints your clean-run grade for the run and raises the facility's **alert level** — guards scan faster and investigate longer (parametric tuning, not authored route rewrites), with at most one authored spatial contingency per segment (e.g. a door that locks), never a topology overhaul. The facility alert level (a global parameter modifier, **not a guard FSM state**) **de-escalates on respawn at a segment-start checkpoint** — capture raises the alert; the retry that follows returns to the learnable baseline patrol schedule — **the residual resets to zero at any segment transition — on a successful clear as well as on the segment-start respawn — alongside the alert de-escalation, so a retry practices the same schedule with no carried wariness and a cleared segment carries no wariness into the next; a Chase in progress is exempt from this reset (during Chase the residual is inert — no accumulation, no reset) — the transition reset is deferred to the chase's resolution, at which point the residual clears to zero if the segment changed** (and since heightened alert is a **Target-tier** system, the MVP room's fast restart always returns to baseline). To prevent a survived chase from leaving the facility hot with capture as the only reset, the alert also **de-escalates after [N] seconds without incident, or on segment clear** (Target-tier) — a bounded tension spike, never a permanent ratchet. This keeps a retry practicing the same observed schedule segment by segment; the run continues, but stakes persist via the permanently tainted grade.

## Core Loop

### Moment-to-Moment (30 seconds)
Move through the environment → watch for guard vision cones / hearing ranges → watch your early-warning suspicion meter (brief clear sight *briefly* crosses the Investigate line but cancels if you break LOS fast within the confirm window; sustained exposure commits to Investigate and reaches Chase; a residual lingers after breaking LOS) → bait or divert when you need an opening → slip past → repeat.

### Short-Term (5-15 minutes)
A *segment* of the facility — a **designer-authored spatial-functional zone** delimited by geometry and sightline choke points inside one continuous space, not a sealed room — with a goal (disable a camera, reroute coolant — non-gating objectives that reshape pressure without a physical door). The level's 2-3 guards patrol continuously across boundaries; boundary crossings are scheduled and learnable, not free drift. Verbs are **structurally significant** — the facility guarantees **≥1 route reachable only via a manipulation verb per major area** (a **major area = a contiguous group of 1-2 segments sharing a functional identity, delimited by a major sightline choke** — enumerated in the Level GDD; not by pure waiting), so manipulation is never decorative — **which requires every major area to retain ≥1 guard (guard-poor = ≤1 guard-driven beat per patrol cycle, never zero guards: a manipulation verb acts on a guard, so a guard-free area would make the guarantee un-satisfiable; camera-disable is a route-opener, never a manipulation verb)** — but a manipulation verb is **never the only route in any crossing**: pure timing remains a possible crossing at base residual (a residual-wary guard tightens timing windows — a readable, player-visible consequence, never a silent lockout) — **with one sanctioned exception: static cameras enforce "never clear by timing" chokes in guard-poor segments — a guard-poor segment carries ≤1 guard-driven pressure beat per patrol cycle (enumerated in the Level GDD) — bounded at ≤1 camera beat per segment, no consecutive guard-poor segments, and ≤2 guard-poor segments facility-wide out of 7–9, **with a facility-wide camera-beat cap of ≤4 total, a per-segment guard-beat minimum (any segment carrying a camera beat also carries ≥2 guard-driven beats in that segment — except guard-poor segments exercising the sanctioned camera-choke exception, each ≤1 camera beat and ≤1 guard beat per patrol cycle, bounded by the ≤2 guard-poor facility-wide cap — the round-13 exception, suspended minimally and deliberately, never silently) — **camera-bearing non-guard-poor segments additionally require ≥4 min (line 199: the ≥2 guard beats + 1 camera beat need ≥3 pressure events, at the ≥2-min cadence floor a ≥4-min span)** — and ≤2 guard-poor segments facility-wide** (a strict minority, so guard-driven beats stay dominant facility-wide — Pillar 1, enforced both per-segment and globally; the pure-timing guarantee is suspended minimally and deliberately, never silently)** — and verb-only routes often open optional rewards (shortcuts, diary fragments, camera disables) — so verb use is a chosen, bounded trade, not a tax. The choice: *steal through silently, or bait a guard away and slip the gap.* "One more segment" psychology: each segment is a clean, readable challenge you want to thread invisibly, inside a facility that stays alive around you.

### Session-Level (30-60 minutes)
Infiltrate deeper → reach the objective → grab the artifact → escape. Natural stopping points at each segment cleared / checkpoint reached. The hook: "can I get a fully clean run of that segment?"

### Long-Term Progression
The *player* grows — knowledge of the AI, its tells, and the level — not the character. No XP bar; mastery IS the progression. Each segment is graded **S/A/B** on *suspicion accrual* and *time*. Suspicion accrual uses a defined operator: the **time-integral of committed LOS exposure above a forgiveness floor** — only exposure that committed (crossed the Investigate entry threshold, base or residual-lowered) feeds the integral; sub-threshold exposure below the entry threshold never grades at all (isolated partial fills are forgiven outright, and the floor is pinned strictly below the residual-lowered entry-threshold floor — `forgiveness_floor < 0.2 × T_base` — so no committed Investigate/Chase escalation, base or residual-lowered, can contribute zero to both grade inputs, and a segment can never grade S while a guard committed an escalation on the player — **S is the zero-input band: S requires zero committed exposure (a null integral over committed exposure) AND zero escalation events — any committed exposure or any committed escalation event moves the grade below S (the band thresholds themselves remain deferred to the Systems/Perception GDDs, pinned here as: S ⟺ no committed exposure and no escalation event)**) **plus a compounding penalty per Investigate/Chase escalation event** — an escalation event is **fresh LOS exposure crossing a committed BASE Investigate/Chase threshold**: noise-triggered Investigates, alert-derived Investigates, and residual-lowered **Investigate-level** re-entries never count **at the Investigate level** — they are consequences of noise, alert, or prior exposure, not new LOS events — **but any of these that reaches Chase emits one Chase event** for that episode (the never-count rule is scoped to entries that never rise above the Investigate level; an entry that crosses the Chase threshold — base, noise/alert-derived, or residual-lowered — emits one Chase event; highest-reached-threshold governs, so an alert-derived guard who gains personal sustained LOS and promotes to chaser grades as the Chase it is, and the harshest in-world outcome never grades clean through the never-count rule) — **the direct-path hide-entry Chase (personal sight of the player entering a hide spot) counts as one Chase escalation event for the grade (unless already counted via a threshold-crossing Chase in the same episode), **and a witnessed-entry capture at a hide spot — the Chase sight-of-entry spot-front capture, or the witnessed Investigate-tier interior-inspection Capture (CR-CONCEPT-01 completion, 2026-08-24: noise/alert-directed investigations never gain spot authority and never capture — hunches resolve fruitless and walk away) — likewise counts as one Chase escalation event, attributed to the segment where the spot-front confirmation or interior capture completes — **one spot-verification incident emits exactly one Chase escalation event total, regardless of which guard-events triggered it (a hide-entry Chase + a carve-out capture, a witnessed inspection capture alone, or multiple witnessed Chases/inspections at the same spot), attributed to the confirmation segment; incidents are episode-scoped (CR-CONCEPT-02, 2026-08-24): an incident closes when its episode(s) resolve — a later witnessed dive into the same spot opens a new incident that emits its own event, so repeated witnessed dives are penalized per attempt, not merged into one** — so the harshest in-world outcome never grades clean through either route**** — so well-spaced repeated exposure is penalized, not just one large fill, and the harshest in-world outcome never grades clean. **Each exposure episode generates exactly one escalation event at its highest reached threshold** (a sustained exposure that reaches Chase is one Chase event, not Investigate+Chase; the confirm window gates only the Patrol→Investigate transition — a sustained exposure reaching the Chase threshold within the window proceeds directly to Chase). Exposure is **aggregated over exposure episodes, not per guard**: an exposure episode is a maximal continuous interval of LOS exposure to a single guard, closed by an LOS break ≥ [X] s or a segment transition; overlapping episodes merge before the per-episode max; simultaneous same-exposure is counted once (max within an episode) and exposure in disjoint episodes — **same or different guard** — sums. **Non-committing episodes — sub-threshold exposure and confirm-window-cancelled episodes alike — are excluded from the integral and count no escalation event** — a sub-threshold or cancelled peek is genuinely free, and repeated peeks are caught by the residual rather than the grade. The segment grade **accumulates all exposure across attempts within the segment, including failed/captured attempts** — a respawn retry cannot wash the grade clean (the basis of the permanently tainted grade). The compounding growth law and event-counting rules are owned by the Systems/Perception GDDs. The **residual is deliberately excluded** from the graded quantity — it is the in-world memory, not a grade input. *Time* is a wide band / soft bonus, so careful observation is never punished. Noise is not graded as a separate input; clean tool use that resolves without LOS exposure never **directly** penalizes the grade (the residual wariness it seeds is an in-world consequence, not a grade input). Consequences use **exposure-source attribution**: a chase that begins in segment A and resolves in B still penalizes A, and a residual or trailing guard carried in from a previous segment does not penalize the new segment. The grade measures consequences of your presence, not the verbs of the fantasy. **Grade legibility is committed (Pillar 4)**: grade inputs are the same visible meter and visible escalation events, and a post-segment **grade card** (Target-tier HUD) shows the meter trace, the escalation-event list, and the penalty breakdown — a player can always name the behavior that cost them the S. A **cross-run best record** (best segment grade / best full run) is a Target-tier HUD element, giving the mastery aesthetic a persistent surface for improvement. The **MVP build emits no S/A/B output** — it collects grade-input telemetry (LOS-exposure integral + escalation-event counts) only; the S/A/B labels are placeholders until the forgiveness-floor value, compounding law, and band thresholds are defined in the Systems/Perception GDDs and validated at the Vertical Slice playtest. The game is "done" when you can thread the whole facility invisibly.

### Retention Hooks
- **Curiosity**: Learning the AI's behavior, finding alternate routes, uncovering diary fragments.
- **Investment**: A clean-run score/consistency you want to maintain; the facility as a place you've *mastered*.
- **Social**: Not a design target — but clean runs are naturally shareable for a future portfolio/speedrun community.
- **Mastery**: The core draw — improving from "spotted often" to "invisible."

## Game Pillars

### Pillar 1: Thinking Enemies
The guards are an intelligent system the player learns, reads, and exploits — never a simple patrol-on-a-rail obstacle.

*Design test*: When deciding between "one more AI behavior" and "one more enemy type," this pillar says we choose the AI behavior. When a feature competes with AI depth, AI depth wins.

### Pillar 2: Fair Mind-Challenge
Difficulty comes from *understanding* the AI and planning — never from twitch reflexes or unfair surprise.

*Design test*: If a player is spotted, the loss must always be readable ("I stayed in view too long") rather than cheap. If a detection feels random, it's a bug, not a feature.

### Pillar 3: You Create the Situation
The player is the architect of their infiltration — they bait, divert, and manipulate guards, not just observe them. Two committed manipulation verbs, both reusing the `NoiseEmitter` system: **Burst** — a throwable noise-maker (MVP) that spawns a noise event where it lands, drawing a guard to investigate; **Lure** — a placed noise source (Target tier) that draws a guard to a specific point and holds their attention.

*Design test*: When a moment has only one possible approach, we add another — burst or lure — so the player can always *do something* to reshape the room. Anti-puppeting is explicit: tool use is a limited resource, the grade penalizes compounded exposure, and fruitless noise investigations accrue a bounded share of residual wariness (an in-world consequence, never directly grade-penalized) — so manipulation is a meaningful choice with consequences, not a free action.

### Pillar 4: Visible Intelligence
The AI systems — perception, suspicion, coordinated alert, chase — are designed to be *seen and understood* by the player (and the portfolio viewer).

*Design test*: Every AI behavior must be legible through feedback (gizmos in dev, "alert" animations/UI in play). If a behavior isn't visible to the player, it doesn't count. Every suspicion-meter gain displays its source and the affected guard (Suspicion Attribution) — player-facing attribution is a **Target-tier HUD feature**; the MVP tester build shows **no live attribution** (dev-gizmo verification only — the tester run itself shows none), so the capture-cause claim stays testable.

### Anti-Pillars (What This Game Is NOT)

- **NOT 2D/puzzle-box levels**: We will NOT make levels feel like disconnected puzzle rooms — this is a continuous, living facility. Protects Pillar 1 & 3 — the world must be *simulated*, not scripted.
- **NOT more than 3 AI states**: We will NOT add a 4th/5th state (Combat, Fleeing, etc.) no matter how tempting — Patrol/Investigate/Chase carry the whole game. Hard scope cap.
- **NOT combat or reflex checks**: We will NOT add a weapon or quick-time reflex challenge. Protects Pillar 2 — mind-challenge, not twitch.
- **NOT movement feature creep**: We will NOT add jumping, climbing, or lean/cover beyond the committed set (WASD camera-relative movement + crouch/walk/run). Protects the 2-3 month timeline — the third-person controller is the silent time sink and gets the same scope discipline as the AI.
- **NOT large level sprawl**: We will NOT expand past one level. Keeps the 2-3 month timeline; depth over breadth.

## Visual Identity Anchor — "Cold Watch"

**One-line visual rule: *Every light is a verdict.***
Whenever any light enters the scene, it answers a question — "Can I be seen here?" / "What is the machine attending to?" / "Is this prize or risk?" No light is decoration.

**Supporting visual principles:**

1. **The AI's attention is the building's light.** Vision cones and scan sweeps render as faint cold-white volumetric shafts; camera telegraphs are brightening warm scans; the suspicion meter is facility-hardware-styled with cold fill and the residual-sinking threshold marker as a warm line descending into it. Cognition renders in the world's own light grammar — the player learns one language, and the machine's thinking is literally visible (Pillar 4).

2. **Saturation is reserved currency.** Near-monochrome blue-grey world; the only warm family is gold-amber — the artifact, the objective, and committed alert attention. Cold = the institution, the machine, neutral watchfulness; warm = value and commitment. Objective text and grade card use the same pairing: cold states, warm verdicts.

3. **The building is a sightline first, a place second.** Hard, disciplined rectilinear institutional geometry — sightline-notched walls, extruded light-strip rails, sliding security gates — architecture doubles as vision-cone geometry; cover and hide language are legible at any angle. ~15% human residue (rounded low-poly counterpoint: tipped mug, cardigan, diary fragments) carries the light pathos layer. Guard silhouettes use heavy slab shoulders and short necks so head-turn, glance, and approach telegraph at distance.

4. **Lighting is the primary communicator.** 3D stylized, lighting-driven URP; restricted palette and affordable PBR assets do the work under a strong grade; vision cones are lightweight gradient meshes (draw-call cheap, WebGL-safe). Every frame answers "can I be seen?" — the rule doubles as the Fair Mind-Challenge fairness contract (Pillar 2).

**Mood:** The facility after closing time — a building still breathing on standby. Cold-noir moon wash; tension through stillness and anticipation, never spectacle; quiet dread with surgical precision. Every corridor reads as a sightline first.

**Color:** Near-monochrome blue-grey — steel-lit surfaces `#8FA3B8`, deep shadow `#2E3B4E`, pale rim `#C7D8E8`; warm-gold reserved — `#F2A33C` objective, `#D98E2B` alert. One temperature language, two poles.

## Risks and Open Questions

### Design Risks
- **Risk**: A lone "outsmarting" loop can feel flat if detection isn't tense — needs strong feedback moments (the suspicion meter, guard reactions, near-miss relief).
- **Risk**: Deterministic patrols can become "memorize the solution" — the AI must still react to keep the world alive.

### Technical Risks
- **Risk**: The 3D third-person controller + NavMesh + AI integration on a *first* project — the "first-time 3D plumbing" is where time silently disappears.
- **Risk**: Chase fairness tuning (retargeting, give-up timer) — mitigated by last-known-position + re-sight retargeting (position prediction is a stretch experiment only).

### Market Risks
- **Risk**: Stealth is a niche genre, but the "thinking AI" hook has a proven, passionate audience (Alien: Isolation). As a portfolio project, commercial reach isn't the goal.

### Scope Risks
- **Risk**: AI scope-creep ("perfect" AI) — mitigated by the hard 3-state limit and anti-pillars.
- **Risk**: Level size creeping past one facility — mitigated by the single-level anti-pillar.

### Open Questions
- **Question**: Is the early-warning suspicion meter + noise-maker loop fun in isolation? → Prototype-1 answered **PROCEED** (HTML, N=1 internal, 2026-08-13); tuning ranges and the strengthened-residual behaviors to be validated in the engine milestone-0 prototype.
- **Question**: Does the S/A/B room grading make the mastery loop compelling, or does it need another retention hook? → Answer via playtest.

## MVP Definition

**Core hypothesis** (directional — the MVP validates the loop's **fairness and learnability**; the "engaging" verdict is deferred to creative-director sign-off at the playtest, and the S/A/B grade-loop validation is scheduled for the Vertical Slice playtest): the one-room loop reads as fair and learnable to first-time players — evidenced by: after each capture, a first-time player can state at least one *specific* true cause of the detection; after observing a guard's complete patrol cycle once, a first-time player can state the correct crossing plan and then complete a run on the **first post-observation attempt** in which the guard never *visually detects* them (scored as two sub-bars — comprehension and execution — with an explicit scoring rule: the execution sub-bar is scored on the first post-observation attempt; an attempt in which the guard visually detects the player per the operational definition — an actual LOS-suspicion escalation to Investigate or Chase, including residual-lowered threshold entries, excluding confirm-window-cancelled peeks and noise/alert-only Investigates — is a design signal (fail); this fail bar is independent of the grade's base-threshold 'escalation event' definition, so a residual-lowered visual detection fails the claim even though it does not count as a grade escalation event; an attempt that records no committed LOS escalation but fails to complete is an execution miss that does not consume the attempt, up to a bound of K=3 attempts beyond which the sub-bar fails; the trace logs residual level and threshold state at each attempt start/end, so residual carryover between attempts is recorded rather than silently misattributed; **a scored execution attempt must start at residual ≈ 0 (R ≤ ε_residual — the claim-2 residual floor, distinct from the grade's forgiveness floor) — an attempt that starts above the floor is not scored against the execution sub-bar (recorded as secondary evidence) and does not consume the K=3 bound, so the execution bar measures geometry + plan, not carried wariness — **a non-scored above-floor start triggers a residual-decay wait (or protocol reset) until R ≤ ε_residual before the next scored attempt, and [2] consecutive above-floor starts cap the sub-bar, so the protocol terminates (the K=3 scored-attempt bound then decides); ε_residual's numeric value commits in the playtest plan, not here****); and players choose to use the noise-maker at least once — **unprompted** (no tester demonstration), within the session, and **only after the build has presented the tool** (a **placed pick-up guarantees discoverability — no HUD hint**, consistent with the no-full-HUD MVP exclusion; "unprompted" means volitional adoption, not instruction-following).

**Operational definitions** (so the claims are testable): *Visually detected* = the guard escalates above Patrol due to line-of-sight suspicion of the player (to Investigate or Chase); a noise-triggered Investigate that does not result in LOS suspicion is NOT a detection. *Capture* = the guard reaches Chase and the player enters a catch check (guard within catch range of the player's live position for a short timer — the guard-to-player metric, never distance-to-destination); the attempt ends. *True cause* = a contributing **decision input from the gizmo's logged FSM decision trace** — more specific than "the guard saw me" — per the committed rubric of acceptable/rejectable statements (acceptable: "Guard A's LOS from the east window while I crossed," "I lingered in the light past the confirm window"; reject: "the guard saw me") in `design/qa/prototype-playtest-plan.md`; any contributing event from the trace counts (not necessarily the first). It shows **no live attribution during the tester run**, revealing the capture cause **only after** the player states it; the measurement protocol (tester count, pass thresholds, screening, attempt bounds, cohort separation for claims 2 and 3) is committed to `design/qa/prototype-playtest-plan.md`, **which must additionally specify: the claim-2 comprehension rubric (what "correct crossing plan" means, mirroring the claim-1 rubric commitment) and the claim-2 aggregate pass rule (≥60% both sub-bars, N≥5, INCONCLUSIVE below); scenario ordering so the claim-1 trace reveal cannot contaminate claim-2 for shared-cohort testers; operational attempt boundaries (start/end, abort, timeout); the claim-3 denominator above and its **numerator rule** (a tester counts in the numerator iff their pick-up-reached event is followed by a Burst use on a later attempt — the competency is measured against attempts after the pick-up was presented, never the attempt that reached it; the numerator is committed to the playtest plan, not derived here); a colloquial→trace mapping for the claim-1 rubric (so raters judge the cause, not the player's vocabulary); and a no-`Debug.Log` trace flush (Player.log would leak the trace to disk)** — anchored at concept tier as: claim 1 passes if ≥80% of scored captures yield a specific true cause, where scored captures are limited to the FIRST capture per tester (later captures are reveal-contaminated and recorded as secondary evidence, not scored against the pass rule); minimum viable sample = 5 first-captures; verdict INCONCLUSIVE below that; **to make claim 1 jointly satisfiable with claim 2's clean-pass bar, the tester session continues past a clean objective grab — a tester whose first post-observation attempt completes without detection does not end the session; later attempts remain playable, and a capture on any later attempt is that tester's first capture and is scoreable against claim 1, so the 5-first-captures floor is not structurally capped by claim 2's ≥60% clean-pass requirement**; The HTML prototype's N=1 internal result is NOT this protocol.

**Required for MVP**:
1. One well-crafted room with one guard running the full AI loop (Patrol → Investigate → Chase, driven by the suspicion meter) as an FSM. The room must support **both** a pure-timing crossing (no Burst) **and** a route reachable only via Burst, so noise-maker use is a genuine decision (anchoring claim 3). Four geometry acceptance criteria: **(a)** *existence* — a spawn-to-objective route exists whose full transit at **crouch and walk speed** (the committed slowest states — the states testers actually use) accrues **zero LOS-initiated committed Investigate/Chase escalation** when transited within a start-phase window of the patrol cycle — the window is a strict non-empty subset **bounded to at most half of one full patrol period** (the period, including the guard's scan sweeps, bounds it), so a start phase outside the window always exists and the AC stays timing-gated, never always-clear and never nearly-always-clear — with the transit's noise state verified to produce no noise-driven Investigate that re-covers the route; "zero" is a committed-escalation measure, with the tolerance bounded to committed exposure only — the only tolerated transient accumulator samples are those that never commit: a transient that crosses the Investigate entry threshold (base or residual-lowered) and survives the confirm window commits, accrues graded integral, and disqualifies S; a window-cancelled crossing is committed-escalation zero and does not fail the AC, so a certified-clean transit implies a null graded exposure integral and therefore S-eligibility; the route is **timing-gated**, not always-clear — the same transit begun outside the window accrues suspicion, and the verification must include **≥1 sampled start phase outside the window that accrues committed escalation at base residual (the negative sample — otherwise an always-clear route passes the AC as verified and the "correct crossing plan" comprehension is vacuous)**; **(b)** *universal* — any sampled full-transit attempt of the Burst-only route without a noise event ends in a committed LOS escalation (outcome-based — the transit fails, not merely that the sightline is "clear"); **(c)** *investigation-arc* — split: **(c-1)** AC (b) is verified against whatever investigate hold-position/look-around behavior ships in the MVP build (trace-verified), so the Burst route cannot be broken by the shipped behavior; **(c-2)** when the strengthened-residual look-around is implemented (engine milestone-0 / Vertical Slice), the Burst-only route must remain crossable-by-Burst — any full-transit of the route **with a noise event** completes without a committed LOS escalation under the strengthened look-around — **'committed LOS escalation' here matches the claim-2 operational definition (any actual LOS-suspicion escalation to Investigate or Chase, including residual-lowered threshold entries, excluding confirm-window-cancelled peeks and noise/alert-only Investigates), not the grade's base-threshold-only escalation-event definition** — re-verified at that tier **by outcome (trace-verified), with a stated failure consequence: if a Burst crossing ends in committed escalation, the route geometry is re-authored or the look-around arc re-tuned until the AC passes (the AC gates that tier)**; **(d)** *no capture-immunity* — no reachable off-mesh, shallow off-mesh, or path-partial position within the certified routes' vicinity permits evading a catch beyond the reachable catch+backstop envelope of a guard with a live fix (no stand-off; verified by an **editor-time sweep — exhaustive per-walkable-cell centroid sampling** of all player-reachable positions within a committed **"certified-route vicinity" radius** — a bake alone proves walkability, not the absence of reachable stand-off pockets. **The sweep requires committed inputs before it executes: the certified-route vicinity radius value, **catch range + margin (the reachable catch+backstop envelope the sweep bounds against, with margin committed ≤ hysteresis)**, the player-position sampling granularity = the navmesh cell itself (exhaustive per-cell centroid sampling, superseding a fixed lattice — a stand-off pocket narrower than the ≤ ½-catch-range grid must still be caught); the guard-position grid spacing ≤ margin (unchanged), and the bounded `NavMesh.SamplePosition maxDistance` that defines deep off-mesh, **and a single committed |ΔY| tolerance value (the ≈1.0-1.5 m band is authoring guidance, not an executable bound — the sweep uses the committed single value), and the sweep Linecast's committed stance/offset set (the conservative lowest-ray rule: both actors' committed capsule heights, `QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore`, World layer-mask)** — the milestone-0 gate asserts these are committed in the Level GDD / qa plan before the sweep runs, **along with committed *provisional* grade values (`forgiveness_floor`, `0.2 × T_base`, the confirm-window duration, and the AC(a) start-phase-window, sourced from the Perception/Systems GDDs) before AC(a) can be reported passing — re-validated at the Vertical Slice playtest.**** **The sweep scores path-partial positions by the same rule as the runtime catch contract: cumulative corner distance to the closest reachable corner, restricted to genuine stand-offs where the corner-to-live-transform segment is geometry-clear via `Physics.Linecast` — **with the same parameterized ray as the runtime clause: both endpoints offset by each actor's committed capsule height, testing the conservative lowest ray (the player's crouch offset), cast with `QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore` on the World layer-mask, so the sweep and the runtime cannot disagree**; a partial path obstructed by intervening solid geometry does not score and cannot pass.** **The sweep's path queries use the same agent-parameterized `NavMeshAgent.CalculatePath` instance API as the runtime — a warped hidden agent placed at each sampled guard position carries the guard's radius and areaMask — never the static `NavMesh.CalculatePath`, so sweep and runtime can never disagree on a path the guard can actually execute.** The sweep asserts, **geometrically** (per sampled player-reachable position, **THERE EXISTS at least one guard — a sweep-sampled guard position within the certified-route vicinity — **sampled only at on-navmesh (walkable) points at a grid spacing ≤ the committed margin (the path-distance jitter tolerance, itself committed ≤ hysteresis — finer than the player-position sample grid, because the sweep must not fail a guard position the runtime legitimately catches when its nearest walkable lattice neighbor falls more than one margin outside the catch+backstop envelope), with |ΔY| measured from each guard position's navmesh-sampled reference floor, never from an off-navmesh grid point** — whose navmesh path distance to the position is ≤ catch range + margin (the reachable catch+backstop envelope, the envelope check including the vertical delta — a sampled position whose |ΔY| from the guard's reference exceeds the |ΔY| tolerance (≈1.0-1.5 m) fails the existence requirement); otherwise the position is a stand-off and the AC fails — the existence requirement makes failure non-vacuous: a position unreachable by every guard fails, never passes vacuously** — the reference bound is that envelope, never the suspended give-up window, which does not bound sustained-LOS stand-offs by design), that **no non-hide-spot sampled position holds a catch-check evasion beyond that envelope** (hide spots are governed by the guard-reachability assertion below plus spot-front verification completing — the give-up window is suspended through the approach and spot-front verification by design, so the evasion bound applies only to non-hide-spot positions), **and every hide spot within the certified-route vicinity is guard-reachable (a NavMesh path from the guard to the spot front exists), no deeper than catch range + margin (the backstop-depth pin), and its interior vertical offset above the guard's navmesh floor is strictly below the committed |ΔY| tolerance (the backstop vertical pin, mirroring the line-77 hide-spot pin) — so a chase whose LKP is a hide spot always resolves at the spot (catch or confirm-empty) rather than hanging forever, and an over-elevated spot can never resolve "confirm empty" as capture immunity — the give-up window and caps are suspended through spot-front verification, so an unreachable, over-deep, or over-elevated hide-spot LKP would never terminate. These hide-spot assertions run at the first hide-spot build (the milestone-0 bake ships no hide spots, so they are vacuous there) and are re-verified at that tier.** **A shallow off-mesh authoring rule mirrors the deep off-mesh commitment: no player-reachable shallow off-mesh pocket within the certified-route vicinity exceeds the committed |ΔY| tolerance relative to its navmesh-sampled reference floor — verified by the same geometric off-mesh probe sweep that verifies the deep off-mesh rule, so no |ΔY|-gated backstop evasion is authorable anywhere in the certified-route vicinity.**). Also required: a **safe observation vantage** (from spawn, with **zero LOS exposure and therefore zero residual accrual** — a true safe spot, so observing the patrol does not contaminate the claim-2 crossing with a residual-lowered threshold) from which a full patrol cycle including scan sweeps is visible; **AC(a)/(b) are verified at the residual level the first claim-2 crossing faces** (the residual reset between verification cycles measures geometry, not accumulated wariness). "Patrol schedule" explicitly includes the guard's scan sweeps; ACs are verified against the **event-complete FSM decision trace** (every LOS gain and LOS break, every escalation with its threshold state — the same event-complete trace the gizmo already logs), **never a lossy sampler: a committed escalation whose confirm-window is shorter than a sample period cannot be missed because verification reads the trace's escalation events, not discrete samples; the ≥10 Hz overlay sampling is a display/spot-check rate, never the verification source** — across ≥3 consecutive cycles, with the guard's residual **reset to zero between cycles** — verification measures geometry, not accumulated wariness.
2. Player movement (crouch/walk/run) + vision-cone and hearing perception + early-warning suspicion meter.
