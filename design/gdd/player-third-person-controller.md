## Overview

The Player Third-Person Controller is the player's body in the facility — and the data layer every detection system reads. On the surface it is deliberately small: camera-relative WASD movement on the ground plane plus three movement states (**crouch / walk / run**), each with its own speed and loudness signature. Beneath that, it is a **movement-state authority**: it owns the stance/movement state machine and publishes state facts (binary stance flag, live speed, capsule geometry, armed noise radius, movement-state transitions) that Perception consumes for sensing-ray offset selection and distance metrics, Player Noise consumes for its loudness signature, and the Guard AI's chase/catch contract consumes for its speed invariant (`V_chase ≥ 1.10 × V_run(runtime-max)`) and capsule operand (`r_player` 0.35 m, locked). The controller is the player's half of the fairness contract: crouch is the committed silent state, walk is heard at 4 m and run at 6 m once your first stride lands (F5) — every risk the player takes with speed is legible, because the states that move you faster are exactly the states that make you louder. Scope is hard-capped by the anti-pillar: **no jump, no climb, no lean, no cover system** — the committed set is WASD + three states, nothing more, because the third-person controller is the project's named silent time sink and every hour not spent on it is an hour spent on the thinking enemies that are the actual centerpiece.

## Player Fantasy

The controller's fantasy is **being a phantom with a body** — weightless precision under tension. You are the ghost in the machine, and the movement set is your instrument of invisibility: three gears on one dial. **Crouch** is the committed state of the ghost — silent, slow, coiled; the feeling of holding your breath behind a crate while a guard's cone sweeps past. **Walk** is the craftsman's pace — deliberate, unhurried, quietly audible; the feel of threading a route you've already read. **Run** is the admission that something went wrong — the fastest gear is also the loudest confession, and pressing it should always feel like spending a resource you'd rather keep. The player never fights the controls to express intent: camera-relative WASD means the character goes where the eye looks, instantly, with no acceleration mush and no input buffering surprises — because in a game where detection is the failure language, *the controller itself must be fair* (Pillar 2). A missed input that gets you seen reads as the game cheating; the controller's contract is that it never is.

The reference feel is Splinter Cell's tactile third-person stealth — close camera, readable silhouette, weight that answers — minus everything that isn't movement: no vaults, no grabs, no acrobatics. What remains is deliberately austere, because the fantasy isn't athletic prowess; it's *restraint*. The mastery aesthetic lives here too: a clean run is one where the player spent the whole segment in the two quiet gears and never needed the third — and the controller makes that restraint feel skillful rather than sluggish.

## Detailed Design

### Core Rules

**C0 — Scope cap (anti-pillar enforcement).** The committed verb set is exactly: camera-relative WASD ground movement + **crouch / walk / run**. Explicitly excluded, permanently: jump, climb, vault, lean, cover system, roll, interact-grab — the artifact-grab input verb is owned by Win/Lose & Respawn (#9); this controller exposes no interact channel (revision round 1, B9 routing). Any addition requires a concept-level revision, not a tuning change.

**C1 — Movement model.** Input is a 2D vector from WASD, rotated into world space by the camera's yaw (camera-relative movement). The vector is normalized; diagonal input is never faster than cardinal. Movement is confined to the ground plane — the controller never applies vertical velocity from input, and NO airborne state exists: certified routes forbid unguarded drops (Level-authoring pin, #8 dependency); gravity and ground-probe implementation rules belong to the physics ADR / #18 (E11).

**C2 — Stance latch & movement-state machine (B8 revision: toggle crouch).**
- **Stance is a toggle latch**: pressing **C** (edge-triggered) latches Crouched; pressing C again latches Standing. The latch persists regardless of movement input — crouch is a posture, not a held key.
- While latched **Crouched**: movement state is **Crouch** when move input is held, **Idle (crouched)** otherwise. Run is unreachable while crouched — Shift + move under the crouch latch stays Crouch speed.
- While latched **Standing**: **Run** = Shift + move input; **Walk** = move input without Shift; **Idle** = no move input.
- State changes are immediate on the frame the input edge registers — no buffering, no queued transitions. Same-frame multi-input changes resolve to the single priority-winning state and publish exactly ONE transition event naming the net from→to pair (never intermediate hops — C7 pin).

**C3 — Speeds.** Each state has a fixed target speed: `V_crouch` (new knob), `V_walk` (new knob), `V_run` = 6.25 m/s (registry design value; runtime-max declared here — see F1). **Backpedal rule**: when the move direction opposes the character's facing (`dot < −eps_d`), the effective speed is `state_speed × backpedal_factor` (0.70, new knob). At `dot ≥ −eps_d`, including exact zero, full state speed applies. Sideways (|dot| small) uses full state speed.

**C4 — Facing.** The character's mesh turns toward its velocity direction at a proportional rear-damped rate defined by F3 (new knobs `turn_rate` and `rear_damp_floor`); the collider is rotation-independent (capsule). During backpedal, facing turn authority is reduced continuously by `w(dot)` rather than held or suspended — the silhouette stays readable to guards while a full reversal remains a deliberate cost (Pillar 4: the guard sees a body walking backward, not a spin). With zero move input (Idle), facing **holds its last value** — no camera tracking, no autonomous turn; heading changes only under active movement input through F3 (Round-3 normative pin).

**C5 — Responsiveness contract (Pillar 2).** Acceleration and deceleration blend over ≤ 0.1 s (new knob `accel_time`) — effectively instant, with just enough smoothing to avoid animation popping. No momentum, no inertia, no input buffering. The character is always where the player commanded on the same frame the state resolves.

**C6 — Capsule geometry & stance publication (B1/B3 revision).** Player capsule radius is **0.35 m — LOCKED** (registry; catch-contract operand). Two heights: `h_stand` (~1.8 m) and `h_crouch` (~0.95 m), mutated only through the F4 gamma blend with **feet anchored**: `center.y = h_cap / 2` is co-moved every tick — a height change never lifts or sinks the character. **Stand gate**: before gamma may increase toward standing, a clearance probe on the feet-anchored TARGET-pose capsule (player radius, target height) must report no hit — **overlap-query semantics (OverlapCapsule-class), NOT a swept capsule cast**: casts skip colliders the cast shape initially overlaps, manufacturing false clearance at thin ceiling lips (Round-3 pin). Margin inflates the **TOP extent only** (`ε_probe ≥ skin_width` beyond the standing capsule tip) — whole-capsule inflation would push the probe's lower hemisphere into the guaranteed-present ground collider and permanently block stands. **Ground inset (Round-4)**: symmetrically, overlap queries report colliders that merely TOUCH, and the feet-anchored target pose rests tangent ON that guaranteed-present floor collider — so the probe's QUERY SHAPE insets its lower hemisphere ≥ `skin_width` above the feet plane (implemented by raising the LOWER sphere center `point1`; `point0`'s separate raise implements the TOP-extent margin — both are query-shape edits ONLY, never a uniform lift of the whole shape; the published `h_cap` and `center.y` are never modified). Without this inset a verbatim implementation reads `stand_blocked` permanently, or flickers with float drift after movement. **Probe XZ anchor**: the probe samples the target-pose capsule at the TICK-ENTRY (pre-move) position — state resolution precedes movement application in the C7 pipeline, the stand completes on the first tick whose START position is clear (one-tick lip-crossing latency, imperceptible), and no circularity exists between the probe result and this tick's displacement. Triggers are excluded via an explicit `QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore` parameter (never the global `queriesHitTriggers` setting); the mask excludes the player layer; production uses a preallocated `Collider[]` hit buffer whose overflow direction fails safe (truncated hits read blocked, never clear). Overflow detection pin: an overlap NonAlloc query returns AT MOST `buffer.Length`, so `count == capacity` is treated as possible overflow ⇒ blocked; only `count == 0` reads clear. Physics-state staleness relative to this tick's transform move (autoSyncTransforms / fixed-step sync policy) is a designed exception OWNED by the physics ADR / #18 gate list — same routing as gravity (E11). On probe failure the controller freezes the current crouch/stand blend pose (at gamma 0 this is fully crouched) despite the Standing latch, **and Reading A applies: `stand_blocked` gates the movement state as well as the pose**. With move input held, the effective state remains Crouch (silent `V_crouch` crawl); with no move input it is Idle (crouched). The latch remains Standing, `stand_blocked = true` publishes, and the probe re-checks each tick until clearance returns (E10 — the one non-total transition). Publication is split by consumer:
- **`stance` (binary enum {Crouched, Standing})** — THE operand Perception vision rays consume for offset selection; flip rule: Crouched iff `gamma < 0.5`. Perception's pinned eye offsets (crouch 0.5 / stand 1.6) are Perception-owned constants selected BY this flag — this GDD publishes no ray geometry.
- **`h_cap` (live height)** — consumed ONLY as the Guard AI catch-gate Linecast endpoint operand (live-capsule contract) and by the debug overlay; NOT used for vision-ray offsets.
- **`r_noise`** (armed radius incl. stride gate — F5) for the DEBUG OVERLAY AND TELEMETRY ONLY — never a gameplay channel (F5g consumption pin: guard hearing and footstep audio consume `step_event`s exclusively).

**C7 — Publication contract (B7 revision; G1 Round-6 adds the visual-only animation feed).** Two GAMEPLAY channels, both one-directional (the controller never queries consumers); plus ONE visual-only polled feed (final bullet) that is not a gameplay channel. In an ordinary tick the fixed order is: resolve input/state, apply movement and capsule/noise updates (including ledger remainder and `audible_armed`), rebuild the cached snapshot, then emit the net transition FIRST and at most one `step_event` second — deterministic intra-phase order, asserted DIRECTLY by AC-P14's ordered-tap publication-order test (a replay canary alone cannot distinguish a deterministically reordered publication) — from that completed snapshot. **Movement sub-order pin (Round-5)**: within "apply movement", the `f_back` dot evaluation reads TICK-ENTRY facing; F1 then slews `v_eff`; F3 advances facing second (its own `w(dot)` likewise evaluated once per tick against tick-entry facing); displacement applies LAST as `Δposition_i = v_eff(i)·dt` (post-slew, explicit forward Euler — the billing convention shared by the F5 ledger and AC-P9's canonical oracle). Two faithful implementations cannot diverge. No event observes a half-updated readout.
- **Transition events** (discrete, event bus): emitted when the resolved movement state changes. Exactly ONE event per frame maximum — same-frame multi-input changes collapse to the net transition (e.g., latch flipped mid-run: single Run→Crouch event, never Run→Walk→Crouch hops). Payload: timestamp (injectable clock seam H.0.9), from-state, to-state, publisher (mirrors the registered schema entry). Capture ingress is a **polled sever flag consumed at `Tick` entry**, before input sampling, movement resolution, or any publication. Ordering pin: sever-preempts-publication — a capture frame republishes the frozen baseline snapshot and emits neither a transition nor `step_event`, so a would-be same-frame state change or noise pulse cannot leak (AC-P14/P15).
- **`step_event` payload** (discrete, event bus — Round-3 pin): {`session_id`, `attempt_epoch`, `step_id` (monotonic source identity), `stride_target_state` (the latched source state), `previous_position`, timestamp (same H.0.9 clock seam), resolved audible state at fire time, world position POST movement resolution of the emitting tick (the C7 pipeline order makes post-move the only truthful reference for distance attenuation), `stride_length_m` consumed, `ledger_remainder_m` after consumption, publisher}. `step_id` is unique within `(session_id, attempt_epoch)`; `previous_position` is the prior post-resolution position, allowing consumers to validate positive XZ displacement without storing movement history. `stride_target_state` and `stride_length_m` remain fixed for the stride; NoiseEmitter selects `R_walk`/`R_run` from the latched target, preventing a Run→Walk change just before commit from reducing the cost of the already-started stride. Field set mirrors the registered schema entry in `design/registry/entities.yaml` verbatim — this GDD consumes that registration; delivery is typed and non-boxed like transition events. Duplicate delivery of the same source identity is not a second gameplay event.
- **Cached live readout** (polled): a readonly snapshot struct rebuilt once per tick — {state, stance, stand_blocked, v_eff, gamma, h_cap, r_noise, facing}; consumers read a stable copy, never live internals. On a capture frame the last pre-tick snapshot is republished bit-for-bit as the frozen baseline; no post-sever mutation is visible. Zero-allocation: preallocated struct, no per-tick heap allocation (WebGL GC budget). Poll-vs-push mechanism ownership → ADR (contract semantics fixed here).
- **Animation feed (polled, visual-only — G1, Round 6)**: a dedicated production accessor returning {`AnimState` (the resolved eight-value movement×variant enum), `v_eff`, `gamma`} computed AT CALL TIME from controller internals — the SOLE delivery seam to the animator adapter (Visual/Audio). Polled at render rate; the adapter change-detects and drives crossfades, so a mid-run camera-flick variant flip delivers on the next poll with NO movement-state change required (transition events do not carry the variant — they fire on movement-state change only). NOT part of the eight-field gameplay snapshot (AC-P22 intact), NOT a gameplay channel (visual tier, like the debug readout) — no consumer may derive detection-relevant facts from it. Idle-pair discrimination is pinned: `IdleCrouched` iff the published binary `stance` reads Crouched (pose-truthful under E10 `stand_blocked`); otherwise `IdleStanding`.

**C7b — Movement/Burst boundary (Player Noise co-sign, 2026-08-31; stationary-throw amendment 2026-09-01).** A Burst throw and landing never enter the movement ledger or the controller's `step_event` path. The Throw edge is sampled in the same authoritative input phase as movement resolution, but it never advances `L_audible`, changes the current `stride_target_state`, changes `stride_length_m`, or creates a movement pip. `ThrowSnapshot` and `flight_handle_id` are independent Burst identities; the controller supplies only its completed movement/stance/facing snapshot to the Burst owner. **The Burst owner accepts the edge only when the completed snapshot's planar speed is zero (Idle or Crouch-Idle); an edge during Walk or Run is rejected before spend and produces no snapshot or flight.** A throw, landing, death-cancelled flight, or restart cannot reset or replay a rustle. Rustle `armed`/`fired` state is discrete and controller-owned; only a committed movement stride may publish the audio cue.

**C8 — Capture interaction.** Win/Lose raises a capture/sever flag; the controller consumes that flag only at `Tick` entry, severs input, and freezes the last pre-tick snapshot for the remainder of the capture frame and all later ticks (the Win/Lose system owns the flow). The controller re-enables on respawn. Respawn is an authoritative reset that bypasses the ordinary upward stand gate: #9 returns control only at a spawn point certified clear by #8, then the controller resets to **Idle (Standing latch, standing pose)** (`gamma` = 1, `v_eff` = 0, `facing` = the spawn transform's forward — delivered by #9), with the internal from-state tracker reset so the first player input publishes `Idle→<state>` (never a stale pre-capture from-state), clears the global audible-distance ledger and armed flag, and clears `stand_blocked` (F5/E7). The INITIAL session spawn applies the identical authoritative reset (first spawn = respawn semantics; delivery owned by #9).

### States and Transitions

| # | State | Entry Condition | Exit Condition | Behavior |
|---|-------|-----------------|----------------|----------|
| 1 | **Crouch** | Latch Crouched + move input, **or** latch Standing + move input while `stand_blocked` | Input released (→ Idle-crouched); latch flipped to Standing → Walk/Run only when the stand probe is clear; while blocked, remains Crouch | Speed `V_crouch`; silent (radius 0); capsule blends toward `h_crouch`; `stand_blocked` therefore gates movement, not just pose |
| 2 | **Walk** | Latch Standing + move input, Shift up, and stand probe clear | Shift+move (→ Run) / input released (→ Idle) / latch flipped (→ Crouch); a new block returns to Crouch-speed branch | Speed `V_walk`; audible signature uses the global F5 ledger and `R_walk` 4.0 |
| 3 | **Run** | Latch Standing + Shift + move input and stand probe clear | Shift released (→ Walk) / input released (→ Idle) / latch flipped (→ Crouch); a new block returns to Crouch-speed branch | Speed `V_run`; audible signature uses the global F5 ledger and `R_run` 6.0 |
| 4 | **Idle** | No move input (either latch state) | Move input; Standing-latch movement is gated by a stand probe before leaving crouched pose | Stationary; `r_noise = 0` immediately; pose is crouched while `stand_blocked`, otherwise follows the latch |

Transitions are total across these edges with **one documented exception**: a failed stand-gate ceiling probe (E10) freezes the current blend pose and holds the Crouch movement branch while the latch reads Standing — published via `stand_blocked`, re-checked every tick, auto-completing when clearance returns. No other forbidden edges, no transitional sub-states; the latch and `stand_blocked` are independent facts.

## Formulas

**F1 — Effective speed (linear slew model).**

```
v_target = S(state) × f_back
f_back   = backpedal_factor   if dot(d_move, facing) < −eps_d
f_back   = 1                  otherwise
a_max    = V_run / accel_time                      (derived, not a knob)
v_eff(t) = approach(v_eff, v_target, a_max · dt)   (linear slew, clamped at v_target)
```

| Symbol | Type | Range | Meaning |
|--------|------|-------|---------|
| `S(state)` | lookup, m/s | {0, V_crouch, V_walk, V_run} | Idle 0 / Crouch / Walk / Run base speed |
| `d_move` | vec2 unit | — | Camera-relative WASD input direction, normalized |
| `facing` | vec2 unit | — | Character forward |
| `eps_d` | registry constant | 1e-4, locked | Float-determinism guard for the C3 backpedal speed boundary; shared by implementation and tests |
| `f_back` | float | [backpedal_factor, 1] | Backpedal multiplier |
| `a_max` | m/s² | 78.125 at starters | Slew rate; worst case (Idle→Run, 6.25 m/s) completes exactly in `accel_time` |
| `dt` | s | frame delta | Time step |
| `v_eff` | m/s | [0, V_run] | Published effective planar speed |

The slew replaces a multiplicative blend scalar: a blend multiplied into the target snaps velocity discontinuously when the state changes mid-blend; the slew simply swaps `v_target` and continues at `a_max`. Clamped linear approach cannot overshoot — this is what makes F2 structurally safe.

*Example:* Idle→Run with `accel_time` 0.08 s ⇒ `a_max` = 78.125 m/s². At t = 0.06 s, `v_eff` = 4.69 m/s; ramp completes at 0.08 s. Full-speed backward run: 6.25 × 0.70 = **4.375 m/s**.

**F2 — Runtime-max V_run declaration (this GDD owns it).**

```
V_run_runtime_max = sup{ v_eff(x) : x ∈ reachable action combinations }
                  = V_run × prod(m_i)        over all speed modifiers m_i
```

(B6 revision: the former `ceil(v_eff)` discretization was struck — literally evaluated it returned ⌈6.25⌉ = 7 and breached the very declaration beneath it. The runtime-max is the exact supremum of the continuous speed function; tick-grid quantization is test-harness bookkeeping, not part of the declared bound.)

The committed action set contains **no speed modifiers**: diagonal input is normalized before any other logic, `f_back ≤ 1`, the slew cannot overshoot, and no external buff channel exists → empty product = 1.

**Declared value: `V_run_runtime_max = 6.25 m/s`** (equal to design value). Diagonal normalization is load-bearing for this declaration — it is asserted in tests, not assumed.

*Forward rule:* any future feature that can raise achievable player speed (sprint-burst, slope boost) MUST update this declaration AND re-run the chase/catch invariant checks in the same changeset. *Solo-tuning ceiling:* because V_chase is pinned at its 7.50 starter, V_run may rise to **6.81 m/s** (= 7.50/1.10) without touching the Guard AI GDD; above that requires a coordinated V_chase retune re-passing the chase invariant.

**F3 — Facing turn rate (rate-limited damped turn with proportional rear damping — B2 revision).**

```
w(dot)       = 1                                        if dot(d_move, facing) ≥ 0
w(dot)       = 1 − (1 − rear_damp_floor) × (−dot)       if dot < 0     (linear down to floor at dot = −1)
delta_theta  = wrapped_angle(target_heading − facing)
facing(t+dt) = wrap(facing + sign(delta_theta) × min(turn_rate · w(dot) · dt, |delta_theta|))
```

For a fixed 180° rearward target, the ideal continuous reversal duration is `T_reverse = ∫₀^π dθ / (turn_rate · w(cos θ))`, evaluated in degrees with the same shortest-angle convention as the ticked implementation. At starters (`turn_rate = 360`, `rear_damp_floor = 0.25`) this is **0.83 s**; sweeping the safe bands `[300, 480] °/s × [0.15, 0.40]` yields **[0.52, 1.24] s**.

Turning is **never suspended** — rearward move directions scale the turn RATE by `w`, smoothly from full rate at `dot ≥ 0` down to `rear_damp_floor` (starter 0.25) at full-rear. `w` is a continuous pure function of `dot`: no hysteresis boundary exists to flicker across (the former ±eps_d suspension gate and its hover trap are struck), and a fully-rearward target still turns at the floor.

| Symbol | Type | Range | Meaning |
|--------|------|-------|---------|
| `target_heading` | deg | [0, 360) | Heading of `d_move` |
| `delta_theta` | deg | [−180, 180] | Shortest signed angular error |
| `w` | float | [rear_damp_floor, 1] | Turn-weight multiplier; continuous through dot = 0 |
| `turn_rate` | deg/s | [300, 480], starter 360 | Max angular speed (at w = 1) |
| `dt` | s | frame delta, bounded by the ADR max-dt clamp/substep policy (OQ1) | Time step |

90° forward resolves in 0.25 s as before; a straight-back full reversal is deliberately slower but bounded at **~0.83 s for the starter config** (derived band **[0.52, 1.24] s** across the safe tuning ranges). That price IS the design, and its honest shape (Round-3 ruling) is a **transient damped window, not a sustained verb**: because facing converges toward the movement direction, sustained straight-back input self-cancels — within ~T_reverse (~0.83 s at starters) the character faces its velocity and moves forward at full state speed. No held-key retreat verb exists or is claimed. The verbs that sustain are **active arc-steering** (orbiting the input vector ahead of the facing holds `dot` near the strafe band while the turn restores most of the rate) and **turn-and-sprint**, which remains strictly better for pure escape — backpedal never kites (F1 penalty prices only the window). The old 2.5–3 s narration was based on the rejected binary turn-suspension model and is not a target.

*Example:* tap A then W while advancing — 90° error clears in 0.25 s. Hold S and flick the camera 180°: facing swings lazily at first, then snaps up to full rate as `dot` passes 0.

**F4 — Capsule height & stance publish.**

```
gamma(t) = clamp(gamma ± dt/accel_time, 0, 1)      (1 = standing; C-latch target drives direction)
gamma increase gated by the stand probe (C6)        (probe failure freezes gamma, holds Crouch branch, raises stand_blocked)
h_cap(t) = h_crouch + (h_stand − h_crouch) × gamma(t)
center.y = h_cap / 2 every tick                     (feet anchored — B3)
stance(t) = Standing iff gamma ≥ 0.5 else Crouched  (BINARY flip at blend midpoint — B1)
publish(h_cap, stance) every frame
```

| Symbol | Type | Range | Meaning |
|--------|------|-------|---------|
| `gamma` | float | [0, 1] | Height blend scalar |
| `stance` | enum | {Crouched, Standing} | Perception's vision-ray offset selector (flip at gamma 0.5) |
| `h_stand` | m | [1.70, 1.90], starter 1.80 | Standing capsule height |
| `h_crouch` | m | [0.85, 0.95], **hard < 1.0**, starter 0.95 | Crouched capsule height |
| `h_cap` | m | [h_crouch, h_stand] | Published live height (catch-gate operand only) |

**Hard constraint: `h_crouch < 1.0`** keeps the crouched capsule below sub-1.0 m cover geometry with margin (hide-spot interiors, cover heights — Level soft dep); the safe range is capped at **0.95** — the honest working-range top — so even a band-top config retains ≥ 0.05 m real margin below sub-1.0 m cover (a 0.96–0.99 value sits inside contact-offset scale, i.e., effectively zero margin — Round-4 repair of a Round-2 carry-over). Vision exposure is NOT derived from `h_cap`: Perception selects its pinned eye offsets (crouch 0.5 / stand 1.6) from the published binary `stance` flag, so end-to-end concealment behavior is verified in integration tests against the live perception stack — not asserted from height arithmetic here (the former k_eye-ratio ray guarantee is struck: Perception uses pinned offsets, not a ratio of this capsule). The crouch blend shares `accel_time`; split into a dedicated `crouch_time` knob only if playtesting demands it.

*Example:* latch flipped to Standing, t = 0.05 s (blend 0.625): `h_cap` = 0.95 + 0.85 × 0.625 = 1.48 m — but exposure flipped earlier, at gamma 0.5 ⇒ t ≈ 0.04 s, when `stance` published Standing.

**F5 — Noise signature mapping (global audible-distance ledger — Round-2 blocker 1).**

```
L_audible(t+dt) = L_audible(t) + |Δposition_XZ|  if resolved state ∈ {Walk, Run}
                  L_audible(t)                    otherwise (Idle/Crouch: freeze, never reset)
step_event        fires when audible and L_audible ≥ stride_length(state)
                  at most once per Tick; consume one current-state stride and retain remainder
audible_armed     = 1 after the first step_event since capture/respawn; state changes do not reset it
r_noise           = 0                                  Idle and Crouch — forced immediately on entry
                  = N(stride_target_state) × audible_armed           Walk → R_walk (4.0) / Run → R_run (6.0), registry-consumed
```

| Symbol | Type | Expected range | Meaning |
|--------|------|----------------|---------|
| `L_audible` | m | [0, ∞) until capture/respawn reset | Global actual XZ displacement ledger; freezes in silent states |
| `Δposition_XZ` | m | [0, ∞) per tick | Actual planar displacement after movement resolution |
| `stride_length(state)` | m | Walk [1.5, 2.4] / Run [2.0, 3.2] | Current audible state's threshold |
| `N(stride_target_state)` | m | Walk 4.0 / Run 6.0 / silent 0 | Registry-owned audible radius |
| `audible_armed` | bool | {false, true} | Set on first `step_event`; cleared only on capture/respawn |
| `step_event` | count | {0, 1} per Tick | Threshold event; one maximum per tick |

`L_audible` is one global ledger, not one accumulator per state. The controller evaluates displacement after movement resolution and uses the resolved state for the tick. When an audible threshold is crossed, exactly one `step_event` is emitted; one stride length for the current audible state is subtracted and any remainder stays in the ledger for a later tick. A large displacement never creates a same-tick event burst. If an audible state is re-entered after the ledger is already armed, its registry radius returns immediately; if it is not yet armed, the retained ledger determines the remaining distance to the first event.

**Cross-state stride-latch amendment (Player Noise re-review, 2026-08-28):** the first positive Walk/Run displacement after a stride boundary latches `stride_target_state` and its `stride_length_m`; state changes cannot replace that target until the stride commits. The emitted radius is selected from `stride_target_state`, not from a later resolved state at commit. Idle/Crouch freeze progress and the latched target. This prevents a near-threshold Walk→Run switch from postponing a pip or a Run→Walk switch from forcing one, while preserving the global displacement ledger.

Rules:
(a) **Displacement honesty**: only actual ground-plane displacement (`|Δposition_XZ|`) made in Walk or Run advances the ledger; keys held against a wall do not. The ledger bills meters actually traveled, never input duration.
(b) **Silent states silence instantly and freeze history**: entering Idle or Crouch forces `r_noise = 0` on the same frame, but neither clears `L_audible` nor `audible_armed`. This prevents a phantom pulse while preserving the global distance already traveled.
(c) **Fresh-session commit-to-audible latency**: after capture/respawn, the first step event occurs at the time needed to cover the current stride — **≈0.551 s Walk** for 1.9 m and **≈0.456 s Run** for 2.6 m at starters (F5c). A prior silent interruption does not grant a new free stride.
(d) **Tap-inching is not guaranteed silent**: repeated Walk↔Idle hops preserve the ledger, so sub-stride hops eventually arm the ring when their total audible-state displacement crosses the threshold. This is deliberate global-distance honesty; Crouch remains the only reliably silent movement state. An idle blip cannot erase progress or manufacture a pulse.
(e) **Respawn/capture clears the global ledger and disarms it** (C8/E7); this is the only reset.
(f) **Footstep audio plays exactly on `step_event`** — what the player hears coincides frame-exact with what guards hear (fairness language; Visual/Audio Requirements).
(g) Backpedal inherits its current audible state's radius (direction-independent once armed); **`R_burst` (10.5 m) stays out of this GDD's scope** — C0 defines no burst action. **Consumption-channel pin (Round-3, sole-audio-exception added rev 2026-08-27): the `step_event` stream is the SOLE gameplay channel — guard hearing (#3) AND footstep audio consume `step_event`s only; the polled `r_noise` readout is debug/telemetry and must never drive hearing or audio. Sole audio exception: the `rustle_event` under rule (h) — per-stride audio-only, NEVER a hearing event, never a gameplay or trace channel.** This pin makes rule (f)'s frame-exact fairness identity true rather than aspirational: armed re-entry returns `r_noise` immediately while the next `step_event` can wait up to one stride (~0.53 s Walk at starters), so a consumer polling `r_noise` would react to a presence the player experiences as silent — forbidden (Pillar 2). Contract co-signed when #3 is designed. **Consumer-side evidence owner (Round-4)**: compliance of BOTH consumers (guard hearing #3, footstep audio) is verified at #3's design gate and exercised by the first Integration story under `tests/integration/` — same routing pattern as the stand-probe pins (B5).
(h) **`rustle_event` (per-stride audio-only, rev 2026-08-27)** — in Walk and Run only, exactly ONE `rustle_event` fires per stride at `rustle_threshold 0.70 [0.60, 0.80]` (registered; Player Controller-owned, Player Noise co-sign) of the current stride. Rustle has two discrete controller states: `armed` after positive audible-state displacement begins for the stride, and `fired` when the current ledger progress first crosses the threshold. With the global ledger, intra-stride progress is `p = (L_audible mod stride_length_m) / stride_length_m`; the rustle fires on the first tick `p ≥ rustle_threshold` after each `step_event`/stride boundary, and re-arms on capture/respawn. It carries `rustle_id = (session_id, attempt_epoch, stride_id)`; `stride_id` remains stable across Walk↔Run state changes until the next committed stride. The warning is sticky for that stride: entering Idle or Crouch after the threshold does not cancel it, and resuming audible movement does not replay it; exactly one rustle is emitted for the stride. If capture, reset, or another terminal sever occurs after the rustle is fired but before the stride's `step_event` commits, the fired rustle is cancelled as an uncommitted audio cue: it produces no hearing event, no `NoisePublished`, no phantom `step_event`, and no replay on resume. It is **audio-only** — it is NEVER a hearing event, publishes no perception fact, emits no `step_event`, and appears in NO trace schema (not a C7 gameplay channel; audio-tier delivery only, same tier discipline as the animation feed). It plays a quiet cloth/gear swish re-timed with the stride so Walk and Run carry a rhythmic fabric layer with zero gameplay consequence. F5g's sole-channel pin stands: guard hearing and footstep audio still consume `step_event`s exclusively. Crouch and Idle emit no rustle for a stride that has not crossed the threshold.

Radii remain registry-owned verbatim. The STRIDE lengths are new knobs owned here (`stride_length_walk` / `stride_length_run`, Player Noise #3 co-sign — they shape the shared event-stream timing). The former per-state accumulator and "tap-inching stays genuinely silent" doctrine are retired; they would contradict the global ledger and make audible distance depend on key segmentation.

**F5c — Fresh-session first-event timing.** For a stride length `L` larger than the acceleration distance, the first-event time is `t_first = L / V_state + V_state / (2a_max)`, where `a_max = V_run / accel_time`. Starters produce Walk: `1.9/3.6 + 3.6/(2×78.125) = 0.551 s`; Run: `2.6/6.25 + 6.25/(2×78.125) = 0.456 s`. ONE joint-sweep convention (Round-5): corners sweep ALL owned axes simultaneously — stride × state speed × `accel_time`, each across its own band (Run's speed axis spans the registry band [5.0, 7.2]) — giving Walk **≈ [0.35, 0.78] s** and Run **≈ [0.30, 0.69] s**; worst legal slowest arming 3.2/5.0 + 5.0/(2×50) = **0.69 s** (a_max = 50 m/s² at the `accel_time` band-worst 0.10 and the V_run band floor — the Round-4 print "5.0/125 = 0.68" had silently pinned `accel_time` at its starter while other corners swept it). Within the SOLO-tuning sub-domain (V_run ≤ 6.81) the Run envelope narrows to ≈ [0.32, 0.69] s. The earlier single-convention "[0.34, 0.56] s" held V_run fixed at starters entirely (Round-4 repair; Round-5 repair names every swept axis under one convention). Because stride and speed are independently tunable, `stride_length_run > stride_length_walk` does not mathematically guarantee a shorter Run latency at every corner; the starter ordering is the intended feel and any reversal at a tuned corner is a balance-review signal.

*Example:* from a fresh respawn, walking out from cover accumulates the first 1.9 m; the moment that stride lands, the 4.0 m ring goes live AND the footstep sound plays. If the player stops at 1.0 m, idles, then resumes Walk, the 1.0 m remains banked — there is no reset and no phantom event. Holding Shift from a fresh standstill needs ~2.6 m before the 6.0 m ring opens.

### Joint constraint: `V_walk < V_investigate` (full safe bands)

If `V_walk ≥ V_investigate`, a walking player in an investigating guard's path can never be caught up to — walk becomes a dominant strategy (near-run mobility, smaller noise ring than run, zero escalation risk). Keeping walk slower than investigation speed guarantees walking exposure carries genuine risk, preserving the ladder: crouch safe-but-slow / walk fast-but-heard / run loud-and-fast / chased-always-caught. Binding form: `V_walk ≤ 4.4` given V_investigate band-minimum 4.5 (registry J8-style bands-pinned constraint).

**Joint form of `V_crouch < V_patrol` (B6 revision).** The independent safe bands [1.4, 2.2] × [2.0, 2.8] admit violating corners (V_crouch 2.2 ≥ V_patrol band-min 2.0) — the former "across full bands" claim was unsatisfiable as stated. The constraint therefore lives as a **config-time JOINT assert** in the H.0.7 validator (AC-P18 leg): every shipped config asserts `V_crouch < V_patrol`, `V_walk < V_investigate`, `h_crouch < 1.0`, `h_stand > h_crouch`, `stride_length_run > stride_length_walk`, positive stride lengths, `rear_damp_floor ∈ (0, 1)` — the floor keeps full-rear turn alive, the ceiling keeps `w ≤ 1` so rear turns never exceed `turn_rate` — `backpedal_factor ∈ (0, 1]` — the ceiling stops backward run pushing past the F2 runtime-max declaration (a value > 1 silently falsifies the sup), the floor keeps backward movement alive and `v_target` non-negative (≤ 0 silently kills backpedaling or inverts speed outside the declared `v_eff` range), and `0.04 ≤ accel_time ≤ 0.10` at load; a violation fails loudly, never clamps silently. The validator also rejects the wider mathematical domain `accel_time ≤ 0` even though the shipped safe band starts at 0.04 s.

**Cadence amendment (2026-08-28):** the load validator rejects `V_walk / stride_length_walk > V_run / stride_length_run`; the inversion corner is not a legal shipped configuration. M9 remains a feel check for legal boundary configurations, not a waiver of this hard rejection.

## Edge Cases

**E1 — Diagonal input normalization (load-bearing).** `d_move = normalize(camera_rel_input)` happens BEFORE any state, facing, or backpedal logic. Diagonal run is exactly `V_run` (6.25 m/s), never 6.25×√2 ≈ 8.84. Without this, F2's runtime-max declaration is false — normalization is an invariant asserted in tests, not a polish detail.

**E2 — Exact dot = 0 (pure lateral strafe).** The SPEED penalty requires `dot < −eps_d` (`eps_d` = 1e-4, fixed for float determinism in tests): at dot in [−eps_d, 0] and exactly 0, full state speed applies (`f_back` = 1) — pure side-step at full speed. Deterministic tie-break, no jitter: the penalty needs strictly negative dot and releases crossing back above −eps_d. Turning has NO such boundary — `w(dot)` is continuous through dot = 0 (F3), so no discontinuity exists between strafe and rear arcs.

**E3 — Proportional rear damping (B2 revision — replaces binary facing hold).** Turning is never fully suspended: rearward directions scale the turn RATE by `w(dot)` from 1 at dot ≥ 0 down to `rear_damp_floor` (0.25) at full-rear. No flicker loop exists (`w` is a continuous pure function of dot — the old ±eps_d suspension gate is gone); no hover trap exists (a fully-rearward target still turns, at the floor). Straight-back reversals cost **~0.83 s at starters**, with a derived safe-range band of **[0.52, 1.24] s**; shallow-arc retreats restore most of the rate as `dot` rises through the turn. "Turn-and-sprint" and "active arc-steering" are the intact *sustained* verbs; straight-back retreating is an honest transient window (~0.83 s at starters before facing converges and full speed resumes — Round-3 reframe: no held-key retreat verb is claimed); the silhouette stays guard-readable throughout (Pillar 4).

**E4 — State change mid-slew.** Under F1 there is no stored blend scalar to corrupt: `v_target` swaps and `v_eff` continues slewing at `a_max`. Run (6.25)→Walk (3.6) mid-sprint decelerates in (6.25−3.6)/78.125 = **0.034 s**; Walk→Run accelerates identically. Nothing snaps. Full stop from run takes exactly `accel_time` (0.08 s), satisfying C5's ≤ 0.1 s rule for every transition since max delta-v is V_run itself.

**E5 — Noise follows a global audible-distance ledger (Round-2 blocker 1).** The former per-state accumulator and immediate-on-switch rule are retired. Actual XZ displacement advances one ledger only in Walk/Run; Idle and Crouch force `r_noise = 0` but freeze both ledger progress and the armed flag. A first fresh-session stride takes ≈0.551 s in Walk / ≈0.456 s in Run at starters; an idle interruption cannot reset that progress, so repeated tap-inching eventually becomes audible when total audible displacement crosses a stride. Audio keys off `step_event`, never measured speed or raw state changes; guards hear through #3 consuming the SAME `step_event`s (consumption-channel pin, F5g) — never via polling `r_noise` — so player-perceived loudness equals guard-heard truth frame-exact.

**E6 — Mid-blend stance exposure (binary boundary — B1 revision).** Exposure is keyed to the published BINARY stance flag, not `h_cap`: standing up behind cover, Perception's rays use stand-pinned offsets the frame `stance` flips to Standing — gamma crosses 0.5 ⇒ ~0.04 s into the stand blend at accel_time 0.08. Effectively immediate. Correct behavior (the player chose to stand); stated so nobody "fixes" it later. Symmetric case: crouching protects the frame the flip returns to Crouched (~0.04 s after the C press) — a valid panic verb. The remaining sub-frame window between flip and blend completion is cosmetic animation only; end-to-end concealment is an integration-test concern against the real perception stack, not an assertion here.

**E7 — Capture during any state/blend.** Win/Lose sets the capture/sever flag; at the next `Tick` entry, input severs and the state machine freezes before any input, movement, probe, or publication work, regardless of current state, mid-slew velocity, or mid-blend capsule height — no graceful ramp-down. The controller republishes the last pre-tick snapshot as the capture-frame baseline, then emits no trailing transition event; subsequent ticks republish that same frozen snapshot. The frozen `h_cap`, `stance`, and `v_eff` are whatever they were before severance; Win/Lose owns everything after. Respawn resets to Idle (Standing latch, standing pose) with `gamma` = 1, `v_eff` = 0, **global audible ledger = 0, audible_armed = false, `stand_blocked` = false** — and emits ZERO transition events between respawn and first player input (AC-P16).

**E8 — Zero-length input vector during backpedal.** Input released while backpedaling: `d_move` becomes zero → dot test undefined → the guard clause returns before ANY dot evaluation — BOTH consumers (the speed-path `f_back` test AND the variant resolver) sit downstream of the Idle short-circuit, so neither evaluates an undefined dot (no move input means Idle per C2's transition table, not "backpedal at zero speed"). `f_back` never applies to a zero vector; `v_eff` slews to 0 normally.

**E9 — WebGL frame cost.** F1–F5 per frame: ~2 normalized dots, ~8 multiplies, 2 clamps, 2 table lookups (radii + stride), 1 global-ledger accumulate-and-compare, 1 snapshot rebuild into a preallocated struct, and at most one `step_event`; trig in F3's angle wrap — atan2 + wrap recomputed EVERY tick while a turn is in progress (up to ~T_reverse ≈ 0.83 s), plus camera-yaw sin/cos per moving tick (computing owner pinned at the #19/#20 boundary). The stand probe runs only while a stand is requested and uses a preallocated `Collider[]` hit buffer; it excludes self and triggers. Zero per-tick heap allocation (including the probe and event payloads) — trivially inside the 16.6 ms budget; recorded so nobody adds allocations later.

**E10 — Stand refused under low ceiling (B3, Reading A).** Latch reads Standing but geometry above is lower than `h_stand`: the upward probe fails, gamma freezes at its current value and the pose does not advance, while the controller remains in the Crouch movement branch (silent `V_crouch` crawl when moving, Idle-crouched when stationary), and `stand_blocked = true` publishes in the readout. The latch REMAINS Standing and is independent of `stand_blocked` — no re-press or semantic strain/re-press rule: each tick re-probes, and walking out from under the obstruction auto-completes the stand on the first clear tick. `stand_blocked` clears when the latch returns Crouched or the probe succeeds. The probe follows the C6 clearance semantics — overlap query on the feet-anchored TARGET-pose capsule with top-extent-only margin ≥ one skin width AND lower-hemisphere ground inset ≥ one skin width (query shape only), sampled at the tick-entry (pre-move) XZ anchor, explicit trigger exclusion (`QueryTriggerInteraction.Ignore`), player-layer mask exclusion, and the preallocated fail-safe buffer with `count == capacity ⇒ blocked` (H.0.13). This is the documented exception to transition totality (States table).

**E11 — No vertical play (ledge/fall ruling).** There is NO airborne state and NO fall handling in this controller: certified routes forbid unguarded drops (Level-authoring pin — #8 dependency); gravity application and ground-probe implementation rules belong to the physics ADR / #18 gate list. If a route violation drops the character anyway, behavior is UNDEFINED by this GDD and owned by that physics gate — recorded so the gap is visible rather than hand-waved.

## Visual/Audio Requirements

- **Animation (B4 revision — in-place clips ONLY)**: the controller exclusively owns transform translation; the animator is a follower, never a mover — zero root motion. The required clip set is standing idle, crouch idle, crouch-forward, **crouch-backward**, walk-forward, walk-backward, run-forward, and run-backward (backward clips may be authored or explicitly mirrored at import, but the choice must land before M4). Animator parameters are `AnimState` (int enum carrying the EIGHT-value movement×variant pair: IdleStanding, IdleCrouched, CrouchFwd, CrouchBack, WalkFwd, WalkBack, RunFwd, RunBack — named `AnimState`, distinct from the four-value movement `state` field in the C7 snapshot), `SpeedRatio` (float), and `Gamma` (float, drives the crouch posture layer); the forward/backward half of `AnimState` resolves controller-side from the same C3 threshold `dot < −eps_d` — no visual-only hysteresis. **Variant-input delivery (Round-5 resolution; Round-6 G1 delivery seam)**: the variant rides IN the `AnimState` enum, resolved controller-side at state-resolution time from the C3 threshold (`dot < −eps_d`) against TICK-ENTRY facing per the C7 sub-order pin — deterministic at zero velocity (an Idle→Walk onset publishes a directed variant on its first moving tick; no NaN/zero-division window) and immune to deceleration-tail flicker (the variant half only changes when the resolved state or the C3 threshold outcome flips). DELIVERY: the resolved enum reaches the adapter exclusively through the C7 **animation feed** (polled — mid-run camera-flick flips deliver on the next poll with NO movement-state change required; transition events do not carry it). The adapter derives NOTHING from transform deltas (the Round-4 adapter-side derivation named an input no seam publishes — struck); the eight-field snapshot gains NO field (AC-P22 intact) and the adapter reads ONLY published controller seams — the animation feed and the snapshot — never internals (AC-P14 intact). Rig type (generic vs humanoid) is pinned in the SAME pre-M4 decision as backward-clip authoring/mirroring — it changes the mirroring workflow, retargeting, and WebGL import cost. `SpeedRatio = v_eff / cruise_speed(active_variant)`, clamped to [0, 1]: forward variants use `V_state`; backward variants use `V_state × backpedal_factor` (backward run cruise = **4.375 m/s**), so a full-rate backward clip does not look artificially slow. Playback rate follows F1, not raw input — the 0.08 s ramps read as smooth weight, not pops.
- **Clip boundary and crossfade**: the logic state and visual variant change on the same resolved tick; the animator receives the new parameters immediately and uses a crossfade of `min(accel_time, 0.08 s)` (starter 0.08 s; expressed through `Animator.CrossFade` as a NORMALIZED duration — seconds ÷ destination clip length, converted per variant adapter-side; Normal update mode matching the render-rate tick — not `CrossFadeInFixedTime`, whose parameter is seconds and couples to AnimatePhysics). The crossfade cannot delay movement, capsule geometry, stance publication, or `step_event`; repeated state/variant changes restart the same bounded crossfade rather than creating a second movement state.
- **Capsule blend**: no separate "stand up" animation — the gamma blend (F4) IS the crouch/stand transition; animation must neither override the published `h_cap` nor drive translation.
- **Footfall synchronization**: the controller owns `step_event`; animation owns only the visual footfall marker. Each forward and backward clip's authored full-rate cycle covers exactly the active state's `stride_length` at `SpeedRatio = 1`, and the controller's event is the sole audio trigger. **Stride-retune gate (Round-5 re-route)**: any `stride_length_*` change invalidates footfall-sync unless clips are re-authored/re-scaled to match — authored cycle ≈ registry stride within **±10 %** tolerance. Authored cycle length is IMPORTED ASSET metadata and is NOT verifiable headless: the enforcement claim therefore routes EXPLICITLY to the pre-M4 animation-import story (owner: gameplay-programmer; evidence artifact: measured clip cycle lengths recorded under `production/qa/evidence/`; loud-fail on out-of-tolerance, never silent compensation) — the same evidence-routing pattern as the stand-probe/#18 gate; NO H.0.7 leg exists or is claimed for this check. NOT playback-rate compensation (rejected as scope creep); affected M8 evidence must be refreshed on any such change. Audio must not emit independently from animation time, nor from the polled `r_noise` readout — the sole audio trigger for footsteps is the `step_event` stream itself (consumption-channel pin, F5g); the sole OTHER controller audio is the per-stride `rustle_event` (rule (h)) — audio-only, fire-once-per-stride at `rustle_threshold 0.70`, never a hearing event. Crouch = silent (no footstep layer and no rustle at all). Loudness must match the published ring from the arming frame (walk ~4 m, run ~6 m): what the player *hears* is frame-exact with what guards hear — that identity is the fairness language.
- **No VFX**: this system owns none.
