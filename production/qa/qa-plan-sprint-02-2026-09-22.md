## 3. Detailed Test Protocols

### A. Arena Geometry & Headroom Invariant Test (`SCENE-01`)
- **Corridor Width Assert**: Main corridor measured at $\ge 1.50\text{ m}$, side branch measured at $\ge 1.20\text{ m}$.
- **Solid Layer Assignment**: Verify all floor slabs, walls, and alcove ceiling blocks are assigned to Layer 20 (`World` / `Solid`).
- **Alcove Headroom Metric**: Verify ceiling block underside sits at $Y = 1.40\text{ m}$ above the floor slab ($Y = 0$).

### B. Player Capsule & Locomotion Input Test (`SCENE-02`)
- **Key Binding Verification**:
  - `W, A, S, D`: Triggers movement matching camera-relative planar basis.
  - `Shift (Hold)`: Escalates velocity to $V_{\text{run}} = 6.25\text{ m/s}$.
  - `C / Ctrl`: Toggles Crouch stance ($H = 1.20\text{ m}, V_{\text{crouch}} = 2.50\text{ m/s}$).
- **Headroom Block Verification**: Move into the $1.40\text{ m}$ alcove, attempt to press Crouch key to stand up. Verify stand is denied, player remains crouched, and UI indicates `Headroom: BLOCKED`.

### C. Cinemachine Orbit Rig & Collision Safety Test (`SCENE-03`)
- **Mouse Delta Orbit**: Moving mouse X rotates azimuth yaw; moving mouse Y adjusts pitch clamped strictly between $[-35^\circ, +65^\circ]$.
- **Stationary Invariant (`AC-P25`)**: With player stationary, orbit camera full $360^\circ$ — player capsule must NOT rotate.
- **Deocclusion Spherecast**: Back camera against perimeter wall. Verify camera instantly pushes forward without clipping through the mesh ($D_{\text{min}} = 0.40\text{ m}$), and smoothly pulls out when wall is cleared.
- **Chase FOV Scaling**: Trigger sprint. Verify camera FOV expands from $60^\circ$ to $68^\circ$.

### D. NavMesh Patrol Guard Test (`SCENE-04`)
- **Bake Certification**: NavMesh is baked with Humanoid Agent settings (Radius 0.30m, Height 1.80m).
- **Smooth Navigation**: Guard traverses waypoints continuously without stuttering, jittering, or falling off the mesh.
